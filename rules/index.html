Cults — How to play

For 2 to 6 players. You lead a cult for three rounds. Send minions to the wheel, gather resources, recruit members and win contests. Whoever has the most followers after round III wins.

Pieces

  • Followers are your score, shown by the follower mark . You start with 1 (yourself). Each member you hold adds what it is worth, the number beside the mark on its card. Followers you recruit or win are yours too, and only those can be stolen.
  • Minions are your workers, small copies of your emoji: 3 in round I, 4 in round II, 5 in round III.
  • Resources come in one color per sphere on the table. A resource is a plain dot in its color . A dot split into the table's colors means "any resources", your choice of colors.
  • Members are cards you recruit from the shop. Most have an ability. Each member counts as many members as it is worth in followers (at least 1), or more if its card says so. Followers from abilities never add members.

The wheel

  • The wheel has one sphere per player, each in its own color, with six spaces: three on the outer ring, two on the middle ring and one in the center. Each sphere's contest is printed on its rim.
  • The outer ring opens in round I, the middle ring in round II and the center in round III. Open rings stay open.
  • Pale spaces with gold edges are open now. Taken spaces dim. Dark spaces open in a later round.
  • A space holds one minion per round, unless a card lets you join a taken space (Crowd In). Every minion comes home at round end.

Setup

Round I's turn order is drawn at random. Then, starting with the player who goes last, each player picks an artifact from the list on the wheel: a boon for your cult all game, which comes with 1 resource of its color. Each sphere has one artifact, and a taken one leaves the list. The first player gets the one left over. This happens only before round I.

Turn order

  • Turns snake: down the order and back up, one minion per turn (1-2-3-4, 4-3-2-1, 1-2-3-4…) until everyone has placed every minion. The players at each end take two turns in a row.
  • Round I's order is the random draw. From round II, fewest followers goes first; ties go to whoever went later last round.
  • An extra turn (Overtime, Go-Getter, Night Owl…) lets you place one more minion and recruit again, right away. Extra turns never happen in round I, and later only while a space would still be free after every minion still to come has landed.
  • A skipped turn is passed over: play goes on to the next turn, and you place that minion later in the round. Nobody loses a turn or a minion.

Your turn

  1. Place a minion on an open space and do what it says. If no space is open, that minion sits out.
  2. Recruit one thing, or pass:
    • A member from the shop, paying its cost.
    • Or followers with any resources: 1 resource for 1 follower, 5 for 5, or 10 for 10, as often as you like.

You can use a member's ability any time during your own turn, as often as its card allows ("once per round", "once per game"). A space that recruits a member for you (Open Door) doesn't use up your recruit. Recruit Twice and Spree let you recruit more than once this turn.

The shop

  • 6 members are face up in round I, 7 in round II and 8 in round III. When one leaves, the top of the face-down pile refills the row. The pile shows how many are left.
  • Between rounds the whole row goes to the bottom of the pile and a fresh row is dealt.
  • A cost has a colored part, paid only in that member's color, and an "any" part, paid in any resources. Members of no color (guests) cost only "any".
  • The pay sheet suggests a payment; tap one of your resources to swap it in.
  • A discount ("your next member costs 4 less") waits until your next member that costs something, comes off the colored part first, and is used up by that member. While you hold one, each card shows what you'd pay, with its list price struck above it, or "free".
  • Point at a card to see what you're short of. The "affordable" switch fades the members you can't pay for.

Recruiting

To recruit is to bring a member or followers into your cult by your own doing: recruit from the shop (paid or free), take a member from a rival, swap one in with your own Red action (Even Swap, Promote, Demote), or recruit followers (each purchase is one recruit). A member a rival moves into your cult is not your recruit.

  • "Whenever you recruit" happens on each of these.
  • A member's "On Recruit:" happens when it joins your cult by your own doing, not when a rival moves it to you. "Round Start:" happens each time a round begins, before anyone places. A card can have both.

Copying and stealing

  • To copy an action is to get its own printed gain, the amount on its chip. The taker's member bonuses and gifts to others are not copied, and the taker keeps what they got. An action that took or traded something (a steal, a swap, a paid recruit) is done again by the copier. Copying a copy lets you choose again; never choose an action already in that chain.
  • Green's copies: Echo copies the previous action, including your own on a snake double turn. Encore copies an action with a rival's minion, Reprise one with your own, and Greatest Hits every action with your minion, one at a time in the order you pick. Every Green action gives +1 green, copied or not. Agents stops everyone, its owner too, from copying its owner's actions.
  • To steal is to take from a rival. Stolen resources keep their colors. Unless the card names one rival, a steal can be split among rivals, and a bonus that doubles or adds to a steal applies once to the total. Crime Wave takes half of one rival's pile of one color, rounded down. Nothing drops below 0.
  • Rounding is always down unless a card says otherwise.

Round end

  1. Members with round-end abilities act.
  2. Each sphere's contest is scored on what players hold right now. Its rim says what it counts, usually the most of its color held.
  3. The leader gains +4 followers in round I, +6 in round II and +10 in round III. Tied leaders each gain half, rounded up: +2, +3 or +5, however many tie. You need at least 1 of what's counted to score.

Then minions come home, the shop deals a fresh row and the next round's order is set.

Game end

After round III is scored, game-end member bonuses are added. Most followers wins; a tie goes to the player with more members.

On screen

  • Point at anything for its full rule and what you'd get.
  • Speed (Quick, Steady, Relaxed) is on the setup page and in the ☰ menu. A click or the space bar skips any animation to its end.
  • The ☰ menu also has this page, a replay of how it works, and the game's log.

Playing with friends

  • One person taps Host online game and shares the link or room code; the others open the link or tap Join with code. The host adds bots and starts.
  • Reloaded or lost connection? Open the same link to get your seat back. If someone leaves, the host can let a bot play for them.
  • Something looked wrong? Open the menu, tap Copy game record, and send it to Cory.

Card rulings

  • Impostor and Doppelganger become copies when recruited. Doppelganger also does the copied member's "On Recruit" right then; Impostor doesn't. Their copied abilities and later round-start effects work normally.
  • Panel's "On Recruit" promotes or demotes another member; Panel cannot move itself.
  • Loyalists protects its owner's other members from every swap, its owner's own swaps included, but not itself, and it does not block theft. Two Loyalists (a Doppelganger copy) don't protect each other.

Glossary

Followers
Your score. You start with 1 (yourself); members add what they are worth, and you gain more from contests, recruiting followers and abilities. Most followers after round III wins.
Loose followers
Followers you bought or won, not the ones your members are worth (and never yourself). Only loose followers can be taken away.
Member
A card you recruit. It counts as its printed follower value in members, or a larger member count stated on the card. Bonus followers do not add members. Most cards have an ability.
Worth
How many followers a member counts for while you hold it: the number beside the follower mark on the card. On: Twins, Volunteer, Shift Crew, Retinue, Late Bloomers, Chefs and 1 more.
Full set
One resource of each color on the table. Holding 2 of every color is 2 full sets; your fewest color sets how many you have. On: Bumper Crop.
Plant
Sower: at round start you put some of your yellow on the card (it leaves your supply). At round end you gain that many of each other color. No card in the deck uses this right now.
Set aside
Investor: at round start you put some of your gold on the card (it leaves your supply, so it doesn’t count for Most gold). At round end it comes back doubled. No card in the deck uses this right now.
In play
Held by any player. Members in the shop or the pile are not in play.
Extra turn
Another turn right after this one: you place one more minion and may recruit again. Never in round I; later only if a space remains after every minion still due has landed. On: Go-Getter, Late Riser, Courier, Night Owl.
Skip
A skipped turn is passed over: play goes on to the next turn, and the skipped player places that minion later in the round. Nobody loses a turn or a minion. On: Sleep In, Hold Up, Last Call, Early Bird, Bouncer, Pocket Watch and 1 more.
Cast out
Send one of your own members to the bottom of the pile. It leaves your cult. On: Farewell, Estate Sale, Undertaker.
Leaves your cult
Any way a member stops being yours: you cast it out, a rival steals it, or a swap takes it away. On: Martyr.
Turn into followers
Trade resources or members for followers with a White space or member (Exchange, Cash In, Farewell…). Recruiting followers in your recruit step is not turning. On: Exchange, Cash In, Cashier, Money Changer.
Now and at round start
Happens once when you recruit the member, then again each time a later round starts.
Copy
Copy an action: get its own printed gain (what its chip shows). Members’ bonuses on it and its gifts to others don’t repeat. Its taker keeps what they got. Copying a copy lets you choose again; never choose an action already in that chain. Each green action gives +1 green, including when copied. Agents blocks everyone from copying its owner’s actions. Echo copies the previous action, Encore an action with a rival minion, Reprise one with your minion, and Greatest Hits every action with your minion. On: Echo, Greatest Hits, Stunt Double, Tribute Band.
Face-up
The members in the shop that everyone can see. When one leaves, the pile refills the row.
Pile
The face-down deck the shop refills from. Swept members go to its bottom; the market also refreshes between rounds. On: Out of Style, Peek, Bury, Bargain Bin, Patroness, Window Shopper and 5 more.
Once per round
Usable one time each round; it comes back when the next round starts. On: Night Owl, Hot Streak, Squatter, Straggler.
Once per game
Usable one time in the whole game. On: Speculator, Headhunter, Go-Getter, Debutante, Rainmaker, Chefs and 6 more.
Round start
When a round begins, before anyone places a minion. On: Banker, Host, Late Riser, Clockmaker, Benefactor, Peddler and 6 more.
Round end
After the round’s last minion is placed. Round-end abilities happen first, then each quadrant’s contest is scored. On: Dyer.
Game end
After round III is scored. Game-end bonuses are added, then the most followers wins (ties: more members). On: Tycoon, Mob, Patriarch, Timekeepers, Patron Saint, Snob and 4 more.
On recruit
Happens when the member joins your cult by your own doing, paid or free: recruit, take or swap (your own red action). Being given a member does not trigger it, and a rival moving it into your cult doesn’t either. On: Banker, Treasurer, Tryout, Mentor & Protégé, Host, Recruit Bonus and 25 more.
Recruit
Bring a member or followers into your cult by your own doing: recruit or take a member (paid or free), swap one in with your own red action, or recruit followers (each follower purchase is one recruit). A member a rival moves into your cult isn’t your recruit. On: Proselytizer, Recruiter, Go-Between, Connoisseur, Trendsetter, Shoplifter and 1 more.
Rival
Any other player.
Leader
The player with the most followers right now. Tied players all count as leaders. On: Raiders.
Minions on the board
Your minions on spaces this round, including a space you joined. They all come back when the round ends.
Next member costs less
A discount: the next member you recruit that costs something is that much cheaper (colored part first). It waits until you use it. On: Intro Offer.
Steal
Take resources or a member from a rival. Stolen resources keep their colors. Lie Low protects your members and resources until round end; some members stop steals too. On: Collect Debts, Help Yourself, Lie Low, Crime Wave, Shake Down, Poach and 12 more.
Colorless
A member of no color (a guest card). Its whole cost is paid in any colors. Its member quantity still counts, but never toward a color, and it brings nothing to cards that pay “its color”.
Any (cost)
“Any” in a cost or a gain: resources of any colors, your choice.
Contest
Each quadrant’s round-end rule. Whoever leads it when the round ends gains +4 / +6 / +10 followers in rounds I / II / III; tied leaders each gain half the prize, rounded up. You need at least 1 of what it counts.
Snake order
Turns go first to last, then last to first, and so on, one minion each turn (3 / 4 / 5 minions in rounds I / II / III). Fewest followers picks first from round II.
Effects
Followers gained from members’ abilities during play (not what members are worth, not buys, not contests).
