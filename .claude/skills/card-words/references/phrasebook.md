# Phrasebook: forms we use and forms we killed

Check here before drafting. A rejected form comes back only if you can say what's different. Add every call the same turn, with the date and why.

## Approved (card text)
- Growing by round: "+2 red in round I, +3 in round II, +4 in round III" or "+1 purple (+2 in round II, +3 in round III)". Two growing parts: "+1 follower and +2 white in round I, +2 and +3 in round II, +3 and +4 in round III" (Allowance, Cory Oct 23).
- Steal both: "Steal 1 resource and 1 follower in round I, 2 and 2 in round II, 3 and 3 in round III."
- Up to: "Turn up to 1 resource (2 in round II, 3 in round III) into 2 followers each." (Exchange, Oct 23)
- Fewest with ties: "+1 of each color you hold the fewest of." (Top Up, Oct 23)
- Fewest followers: "if no rival has fewer followers than you (not counting this card)" (Late Bloomers, Oct 23)
- Your own member to the bottom: "Cast out one of your members to the bottom of the pile" and "Whenever you cast out one of your members" (Cory, Oct 24).
- Turns: "Skip your next turn." "The next turn is skipped." "Take another turn right after this one." "Take two turns after this one." (wording.md)

## Approved (panels)
- A flat +1 moves up to line 2 when line 2 is free (Oct 23).
- Rate in line 1 when line 2 can drop: "+2 followers per minion left" (Last Call, Oct 23).
- Steal both: "Steal 2 and 2 followers" (Help Yourself).

## Rejected
- "more each round" under a number (Oct 24): read as "+3 more every round".
- "+4 next round" (Oct 24): read as a promise to the taker ("get 4 next round").
- "× round" ("+2 gold × round", Oct 23): made a round III player do the math.
- "both grow each round" when only some parts grow (Oct 22: "'more each round' is confusing on actions that do multiple things").
- "Bury a member" for your own member (Oct 24): clashes with Blue's Bury. Now "Cast out".
- "Get rid of" (Oct 24): replaced by "cast out", one word for one idea.
- "(ties: all of them)" (Oct 23): made a table argue.
- "already holding a minion" on Redistribute (Oct 23): the minion you just placed counts.
- "loose followers": nobody knew what it meant.
- "you pick": the house word is "you choose".

## Open
- Growing-by-round panel line 2 (Oct 25, Claude gut call; Cory confirmed Oct 28): the schedule "I +2 · II +3 · III +4", this round's number on line 1. Rejected: "+4 next round" (reads as a promise to the taker), "more each round" (reads as extra, and unclear which part grows).
