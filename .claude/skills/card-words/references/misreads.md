# Misreads: the traps

Read each candidate as a new player would: two seconds, every word taken literally, no knowledge of the code. These are the ways Cults text has actually been misread, with the real case. Check every candidate against each trap.

## When
- **Promise vs schedule.** A future time next to a number reads as something the taker will get later. "+4 next round" on Gather read as "take it now, get 4 next round" (Cory, Oct 24). A growing space's future value is a fact about the space, not a reward: say it as the space's schedule (round labels, no verb), or not at all on the panel.
- **"More" means extra.** "+3 / more each round" read as "+3 more every round", meaning the gain climbs by 3 (Cory, Oct 24: "reads weird and is probably wrong"). "More", "extra" and "additional" next to a number add to it.
- **Turn vs round.** "this turn" ends when you finish placing and recruiting; "this round" ends after the last minion. "Skip your next turn" was once read as losing the minion (Cory, Oct 22: you never lose a turn).
- **When it fires.** "When recruited:", "Now and at round start:" and "Round end:" are different. A member with no opener is always on.

## Whose
- **Taker vs everyone.** "+2 silver, others get 1" says who. "each rival", "you", "anyone (includes you)" are three different words. Never "everyone else" or "other players".
- **Your member vs a shop member.** Blue's Bury buries a shop member; White's Cast out sends your own member. One word for each (Cory, Oct 24: "cast out").

## Where from
- **Bank vs steal.** "+1 follower" means from the bank. Help Yourself's panel said "Steal 1, +1 follower" after Cory made it steal the follower too, so it read as a free follower (text review, Oct 23). If it comes from a rival, "steal" covers both: "Steal 1 and 1 follower".

## How many
- **Per vs flat.** "+1 green" alone on Chorus, when its line 2 dropped, read as a flat 1. "+2 followers" alone on Last Call read as a flat 2, when it's per minion left. The rate belongs in line 1 when line 2 can drop.
- **Exact vs up to.** "Turn 2 into 4" reads as all or nothing. If you can stop early, say "up to" (Exchange, Oct 23).
- **Ties.** "the fewest" with a tie: "each color you hold the fewest of" covers ties without a bracket (Top Up, Oct 23). "(ties: all of them)" made a table argue.
- **Doubled effects.** "+1 white … +1 white" on one card reads as a typo (Allowance printed white twice, Oct 23).
- **What a number counts.** "Steal 1 per magenta action" needs "with a minion"; whether the one you just placed counts must be said once ("this one too").

## In what order
- **Then.** "+2 yellow, then swap up to 3" tells you the yellow can be swapped. If the engine gives a part first, the text says it first (Exchange gives its white first but printed it last).

## Hidden by space
- **Dropped lines.** The wheel drops line 3, then line 2, when a panel is tight. A condition, a rate or a rival's share that lives only there disappears. Line 1 must be true and complete alone, even if it's not the whole rule.

## Words that mean something else
- **Bury** is Blue's space name, so "bury a member" on White read as Blue's action. "Cast out" replaced it.
- **Loose followers** meant nothing to players (Cory: "what's a loose follower?"). Say "followers you bought or won".
- **Pick vs choose.** House word: "you choose".
