# Pitch cheatsheet (read once per thread, with design-core.md)

## The game in ten lines
- Turn: place a minion on an open space and do it, then recruit ONE thing: a shop member or one follower card. Minions 3/4/5 by round; snake order; fewest followers goes first from round II.
- Follower cards are fixed cards: 1, 5 or 10 followers for as many resources, any colors. A space can change how often you recruit or what you pay with, never the cards.
- Spaces: 3 per color in round I, 2 more in round II, 1 center in round III; rings stay open. Round I fills exactly.
- Shop: 5/6/7 face up by round, pile underneath, row goes to the bottom between rounds.
- Members: colored part + any part (floor 2), worth followers, abilities. They move (swap, steal, bury).
- Contests: every color scores most of its color held at round end, +2/+4/+6. Hold to win it, spend to recruit.
- Scarce: resources in round I, the recruit in round III. Round II spaces must change what you own or how much you recruit (bar: Promote, Shake Down).
- Every space gives at least 1 of its color; lean conservative; grows by round.
- A space does its whole effect the moment it's taken. "Whenever", "this round", "at round end" are member effects.
- Colors own verbs: Red moves members, Green copies, Magenta steals, Blue works the shop and recruits more, Purple bends turns, White cashes white into followers, Yellow gets many colors, Gold doubles gold, Silver gives.

## The core
Start from the color's theme in one line, lightly: each idea fits the color as one clean mechanic, never a mechanic plus a bonus, but it needn't echo the theme closely (Cory: "don't over index on the theme").
Engines and synergy, not good picks in a vacuum (Cory). Judge every idea by what it combines with: name its members and the other colors' spaces it feeds. Strength bands (design-core section 5): round I about 2 to 4, round II 4 to 6 or a member changing hands, round III a burst; members 2 to 10.

## Make it land (design-core.md section 4)
Trade one scarce thing for another (Last Call: position for followers). Break a felt limit, best in round III (Crowd In, the white cash-in). Read the table (Echo). Flip what a resource means (High Tide: you want rivals rich in silver). Hand a rival the choice (Shake Down). One visible count. One sentence, done when taken.

## Kill an idea if
1. It needs a piece or rule that doesn't exist (follower cards of another size).
2. It's net zero on placements, or a smaller copy of the color's round III action.
3. Its whole effect is a formula on your own pile.
4. It's access or information without power.
5. It doesn't grow by round, or grows into a round III monster (check Green copying it).
6. It loses to the same ring's reworked sibling.
7. It lasts past the moment it's taken.
8. It charges you, rescues you, places out of turn, rewards the leader, cuts costs, needs memory, or names another color.
