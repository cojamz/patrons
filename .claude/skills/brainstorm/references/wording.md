# Wording: how the game says things

Use this **only after the ideas exist**, when you write the pitch up. Never think *in* these words while brainstorming (Cory, Oct 22: "that should never undermine more complicated brainstorming… we should never brainstorm with the language of the game in mind"). Think in whatever terms help, then translate. The house style for printed rules is design/rule-style.md; these are the phrasings live cards use today, plus his corrections.

## Messages to Cory
Plain, short, reader-friendly, always (Cory, Oct 22). One idea per sentence. Say what a player does and gets, in the game's own words below. No shorthand, no invented terms, no "basically".

## Turns and timing (live card text)
- Skip yourself: "Skip your next turn." (Sleep In). You are passed over once; play continues and you still place every minion. Never "you lose a turn".
- Skip whoever's next: "The next turn is skipped." (Hold Up)
- Go again now: "Take another turn right after this one." (Right Away). A member's: "take an extra turn after this one" (Go-Getter).
- Two now: "Take two turns after this one." (Crunch Time). It moves two of your turns up. It does not "do it twice" or repeat anything.
- Go last: "Skip your turns until everyone else is out of minions." (Last Call)
- Rule bend for the round: "This round you can place on taken spaces." (Crowd In)
- Growing by round: "+1 purple (+2 in round II, +3 in round III)" or "+2 red in round I, +3 in round II, +4 in round III". Rounds are I, II and III.
- "Minions you have left" means the ones not yet placed this round.

## Recruiting and followers
- Recruit means bringing a member or followers into your cult by your own doing. After you place, you recruit one thing: a member, or one follower card.
- The follower cards read "Recruit 1 follower", "Recruit 5 followers", "Recruit 10 followers". In talk with Cory, "buy followers" is his phrase and stays.
- Extra recruits: "Recruit twice this turn." / "Recruit 3 times this turn." Free member: "Recruit the cheapest member in the shop."
- "Followers you bought or won" are the ones that can be stolen or sold (never "loose followers").
- Members: "worth 2 followers" (the follower mark on the card); "counts as 3 members".

## Members' abilities
- Openers, exactly: "When recruited:", "Now and at round start:", "Round start:", "Round end:", "Game end:", "Once per round:", "Once per game:", "Whenever …,".
- A player "uses" a member's ability on their own turn (rulebook: "You can use a member's ability any time during your own turn").

## The shop and the pile
- "in the shop" for the face-up members; "the pile" for the face-down deck.
- "Send … to the bottom of the pile, then refill." ("Bury" as shorthand is pending Cory.)

## Taking and copying
- "Steal 1 resource", "Steal half of one rival's pile of one color, rounded down", "steal it" for a member. "Steal a rival's member you choose." (Poach)
- "Copy the previous action", "Copy an action with a rival minion". A copy gets the space's printed gain.

## Contests
- "Most purple: the purple you hold when the round ends." Prize "+2 / +4 / +6 followers by round, ties split". Say "the purple contest" or "most purple".

## General
- "rival" for another player, "each rival", "anyone" includes you. "you choose". Numbers as digits. "half, rounded down".
- The follower icon is "the follower mark".
