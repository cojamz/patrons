# Touching the screen
You just opened screen code. Before changing anything Cory will see, read design/taste.md in full (ten rules, his own calls, the slop list) and look at the visual language in design/prototypes/language/.
- Plain and clear before clever. Words beat symbols, except the resource dot and the follower mark.
- Every choice is made by clicking on the board, never in a chip grid or selector screen.
- When he approves a direction and asks for one change, change only that.
- Snappy: clicks and hovers answer within 100 ms measured as "card complete"; the wheel lands in 400 ms; motion 150-350 ms per beat, never making him wait to act.
- Done means you saw it yourself in a screenshot of a fresh frozen build at about 1270x700.
- Lessons for screen work: design/lessons/screen.md.
