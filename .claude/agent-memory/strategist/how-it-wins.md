# Strategist memory (durable findings; newest last)

- 2026-10-21 · Pack lumpiness: follower packs are 1/5/10 and one buy per turn, so holding 2-4 or 6-9 means a cheap member is the efficient spend. Under +1/action incomes, members fill the gaps between packs. Evidence: core.js TIERS.
- 2026-10-21 · Round I always fills exactly (3N spaces, 3N minions, N colors = N players); the last placer is forced. A dead round-I space costs that seat. R2/R3 have N spares each.
- 2026-10-21 · Round III anchors are 1 per color = N for N players, and the trailer picks first (fewest-followers-first snake), so anchor power works as the game's catch-up. Squatter breaks it (Cory: "TOO strong in round III").
- 2026-10-21 · At 2 players the snake gives back-to-back turns after the first (ABBAABB), so tempo effects matter less there.
- 2026-10-21 · Every resource buys followers 1-for-1 now, and every color contests held resources, so Gold's old identity (hoard, gold-as-any) has turned into the base game. Only doubling is Gold's own.
- 2026-10-21 · Colored parts gate members under +1/action; spending a color on its members weakens your stake in its contest. That's where hold-vs-spend lives.
- 2026-10-21 · On Recruit fires for whoever moves or takes a card (Red swaps, Poach), so big On Recruit cards are Red fuel and theft magnets.
- 2026-10-21 · Peek late in a round sets the next round's fresh 7 (rows refresh off the pile top).
- 2026-10-21 · Dealt decks: 2p Gold+Red+guests 27 cards, 3p +Blue 37, 4p +Green 49. Pile cycles at 2-3p; at 4p nearly all cards show, but a player owns only 2-4 per color, so design members standalone.
- 2026-10-21 · Color-counting effects (sets, "per other color") and "everyone else" gifts shrink at 2-3 players (Cory #84 on Yellow).
- 2026-10-21 · member-feel.md is Claude's audit and contradicts Cory (Recluse). Use only his quotes for taste.
