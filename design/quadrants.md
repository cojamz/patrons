# Quadrants: v4 draft
The scoring rule is each quadrant's only printed Favor source. Every quadrant has 3 R1, 2 R2 and 1 R3 actions.
You can buy an item of a color only right after placing a worker in that quadrant.
**Repeat** = do a space's effect again without placing a worker. Repeating does not unlock buying.
**1/2/4** = Favor at the end of rounds I, II and III.

## Aurum (Gold): wealth is power, and everyone can see who's richest
**Rule: Crown.** Most gold at round end gains 1/2/4 Favor. Everyone tied gets it.
| Ring | Action | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| I | Mint | +2 gold |
| I | Patron | +1 gold, +1 per use of your chosen quadrant |
| I | Levy | Take 1 gold from the gold leader |
| II | Royalties | +1 gold per item you own |
| II | Tariff | +1 gold per quadrant you occupy |
| III | Treasury | Double your gold |
**Interactions:** Gold items cost gold, so every buy weakens your Crown. Noctis takes from leaders. Solara needs gold for sets. Chronis can Echo your Mint.

| Item | Cost | Effect | Favor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strongbox | 3 gold | Your gold can't be stolen | 0 |
| Ledger | 2 gold | Mint gives +3 gold | 0 |
| Signet Ring | 2 gold | You win Crown ties outright | 1 |
| Gilded Bust | 4 gold | none | 2 |

## Noctis (Black): the knife that always points at whoever is winning
**Rule: Shadow.** Most black at round end steals 1/2/4 Favor from the Favor leader. If you are the leader, steal from second place instead.
| Ring | Action | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| I | Skulk | +2 black |
| I | Ransack | Steal 2 resources from one player |
| I | Grudge | +1 black per player above you in Favor |
| II | Extort | Each opponent gives you 1 resource |
| II | Plunder | Steal half of one color from a player |
| III | Nightfall | Take all black from every opponent |
**Interactions:** Shadow is the built-in brake on Crown runaways. Theft breaks Solara's sets. A Chronis repeat of Ransack stings. Black is also the one resource you hold to defend against Noctis.

| Item | Cost | Effect | Favor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Veil | 2 black | Ransack and Plunder can't target you | 0 |
| Dagger | 2 black | Ransack steals 3 | 0 |
| Night Mark | 3 black | You win Shadow ties outright | 1 |
| Raven Idol | 4 black | none | 2 |

## Chronis (Green): one great action, played twice
**Rule: Echo.** +1 Favor each time you repeat an action.
| Ring | Action | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| I | Gather | +2 green |
| I | Relive | Repeat a space you occupy, else +2 green |
| I | Echo | Repeat a space an opponent occupies, else +2 green |
| II | Rewind | Pay 2 green: repeat any ring-I space |
| II | Haste | +1 green, place your next worker now |
| III | Eternity | Repeat every space you occupy |
**Interactions:** Chronis amplifies whatever is strongest on the board: Echo a Mint to chase the Crown, or a Skulk to chase Shadow. It is a reading game, because you are copying the opponent's best spot. Haste speeds you up in the race for contested spaces.

| Item | Cost | Effect | Favor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sundial | 1 green | +1 green at each round start | 0 |
| Hourglass | 3 green | Once per round, repeat a space you occupy | 0 |
| Echo Stone | 2 green | Each repeat also gives +1 any resource | 1 |
| Chrono Seal | 4 green | none | 2 |

## Solara (Yellow): the alchemist who turns anything into what you need
**Rule: Harmony.** At round end, +1 Favor per full set you hold (one of each color in play).
| Ring | Action | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| I | Harvest | +2 yellow |
| I | Forage | +2 resources, any colors |
| I | Transmute | Swap up to 3 resources for any colors |
| II | Prism | Pay 2 yellow: +1 of each other color |
| II | Bloom | +1 of each color you lack |
| III | Flourish | +2 of each color you hold |
**Interactions:** Solara competes with everyone for their basic resource spaces. Noctis theft breaks sets. The Crown wants gold hoarded, while sets want it spread, so gold gets pulled two ways. Sets are kept, not spent, so they score every round and grow into the R3 burst.

|---|---|---|---|
| Alembic | 2 yellow | Transmute swaps up to 5 | 0 |
| Cornucopia | 3 yellow | +1 any resource at each round start | 0 |
| Prism Lens | 2 yellow | Pay any item's cost in yellow | 1 |
| Sun Disc | 4 yellow | none | 2 |

## Choices made in this draft
- Gold: Levy targets the gold leader only. Noctis: Shadow targets the Favor leader only. A fixed target means no choosing whom to hit, so no kingmaking or dogpiling.
- Chronis: "else +2 green" means placing first is never a dead turn.
- Grudge replaces a space-blocking idea. Every space fills in R1, so a block would leave someone's worker stranded.

## Open risks
- Item Favor is a second Favor source next to each quadrant's rule. It's either allowed or it becomes the market's own single channel.
- Eternity repeating Treasury quadruples gold. That's the intended R3 burst, but sims should check it.
- Shadow pulls Favor down from whoever leads. Watch whether it flattens the Crown runaway too much.
