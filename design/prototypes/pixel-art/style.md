# Cults pixel icons: style guide

**The rule, in one paragraph.** Every icon is a 24×24 grid of single-character palette keys (24 strings of 24 chars in `icons.js`), shown only at 24, 48 or 96px so each pixel is exactly 1×, 2× or 4× and stays square. The palette is fixed: `.` transparent; four shared neutrals `k` (ink, only for lines *inside* a shape), `d` dark, `m` mid, `l` light (skin, paper, glints, sparkles); and three steps of the icon's sphere color, `1` shade, `2` base, `3` highlight. Digits always mean "this sphere", so a drawing recolors by changing `sphere`, and nothing else ever brings in a second hue. Light comes from the top-left: `3` goes on upper-left edges and `1` on lower-right edges and undersides. Never draw the outer outline; the renderer adds a 1px `k` outline on every side of the silhouette (diagonal corners stay open), so leave the outer row and column empty. **Members** are a person, head and shoulders, shoulders cut off by the bottom edge, face in `l` with 1px `k` eyes, clothes in the sphere color. They carry exactly one prop (hat, bag, mask, coin stack) that sticks *out* of the body's silhouette, usually on the right, so the icon reads from its shape alone at 24px. **Actions** are a verb on an object: hands, tools and things, never a head or face. Use a glove (Magenta), a coin (Gold), goblets (Silver), notes and echoes (Green). A resource is always a flat disc like the rules-text pip (`2` fill, `3` top-left, `1` bottom-right), and a member as an object is a cream meeple. The sphere hue should cover roughly a third or more of the filled pixels; neutrals carry the detail. **Avoid** gradients, opacity, anti-aliasing, curves and sub-pixel shapes; 1px lines on the outside of a shape (they vanish at 24px); text, numbers or arrows (the card text says those); dark `d` masses bigger than a hat (they sink into the table); a palm sitting on top of a head (it reads as hair); the same cream on two touching shapes without a `k` or color seam between them; and anything a stranger couldn't name at 24px. When unsure, cut detail rather than add it.

**Check before handing off:** `node -e "import('./design/prototypes/pixel-art/icons.js').then(()=>console.log(CultIcons.ICONS.flatMap(CultIcons.validate)))"` must print `[]`. Then look at the sheet (`sheet.html`) at 24px on the table background, and click the outline and background toggles.

## Fanning out the rest (223 = 66 actions + 157 members incl. 7 guests; 10 done)

- **Batch by sphere**, one drawing agent per module (11 modules + guests = 12 batches of 6 actions + 7–15 members). One drawer per batch keeps a sphere's people looking like one family. Each agent gets this file, `icons.js` (as the reference set), its sphere's card texts, and a list of the sphere's signature motifs to reuse. It writes `icons-<sphere>.js` with the same shape, so nothing merges until review.
- **Reuse before drawing.** The 16 vanilla "Worth N followers" members of Blue and Green are one 8-step rank ladder (plain robe up to a jewelled patron) drawn once and recolored. Twins, Crew, Shift Crew and Retinue share a "pair" template. Roughly 200 drawings cover 223 cards.
- **Loop per batch:** draw, run `validate`, render a 24/48px contact sheet, self-critique against this guide, then fix (in this calibration each icon needed 2–4 passes, and hands and pockets were the hardest). Then a separate critic agent, which never saw the card texts, names each icon from its 24px picture. Anything it can't name goes back once. Cory then reviews one contact sheet per sphere.
- **Estimate:** about 15–20 icons per agent-hour, so the 12 batches run in parallel finish in about 1.5–2 hours of wall clock, plus one critic and fix pass of about 1 hour. Expect 10–15% to need a human-directed redraw.
