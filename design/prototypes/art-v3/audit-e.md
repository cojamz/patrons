# Tone-to-role audit, batch e (gold, 9 members, one person each)

Hard rule (ROLLOUT-NOTES, audit.md): no tone reads as the joke, the threat or a service trope; shady roles span tones.

| id | role | tone | place | expression |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| gold.banker | bank manager (authority, steady income) | deep | solo, frontal | proud, closed smile |
| gold.merchant | market trader with an abacus | golden | solo, 3/4 | laughing with eyes closed |
| gold.seed (Treasurer) | keeper of the strongbox key | palepink | solo, frontal | polite, closed smile |
| gold.speculator | gambler flipping a coin (shady-ish) | freckled | solo, 3/4 | sly side-glance |
| gold.taxman | tax collector (mild antagonist) | fair | solo, frontal | stern, level |
| gold.thrift (Penny Pincher) | old miser with his purse (comic) | tan | solo, 3/4 | worried side-glance, pursed lips |
| gold.tinker (Alchemist) | inventor turning a flask to gold (hero) | verydeep | solo, 3/4 | pleased closed smile, eyes on her flask |
| gold.tycoon | old money with a monocle | coolbrown | solo, frontal | proud |
| gold.underdog | scrappy kid with a piggy bank (hero) | warmtan | solo, frontal | plucky small smile |

Checks:
- The darkest tones (verydeep, deep) are the clever inventor and the bank manager: competent, front and lit, warm faces.
- The antagonist-leaning roles (Speculator, Tax Collector) are freckled and fair; the comic miser is tan, drawn as a
  worried old man with no caricature features (normal nose, white comb-over, a cardigan); Tycoon (coolbrown) is
  proud and well dressed, not sinister.
- Alchemist on verydeep skin: calm eyes on her flask and a closed smile, never wide eyes or an open "O" mouth
  (a first draft had both; changed for the bug-eyed / minstrel risk).
- No cultural costume: headwear is a newsboy cap, an official's peaked cap, a card-sharp's eyeshade.
- Ages: 50s banker, middle-aged merchant, older treasurer, young speculator, 40s collector, old miser, adult
  alchemist, old tycoon, a kid. Genders mixed (4 women, 5 men).
- Whole-set review (round 2, foreman's batch-g artist): Alchemist's very deep face relit so it reads at 1x: the lit half
  of the face on the D step (forehead, cheekbone, nose bridge), warm brown irises, a closed smile with no light row under
  it. Penny Pincher: a centred closed mouth (the old pursed dark pixel read as drool), the purse lit top-left and outlined
  so it separates from the coat. Merchant: the abacus redrawn with a dark-outlined wood frame, three light rods and
  beads in separated pairs (it read as a rug).
