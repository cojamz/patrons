Checks:
- The darkest tones are a calm older gentleman (verydeep), a prospector who struck gold (deep), an owner-chef (coolbrown)
  and the money changer at her own counter (brown): competent, front and lit, closed mouths.
- The shady role (Fence) is fair; the comic role (Impressionist) is freckled and the gag is the prop, not the face.
- Money Changer: the darker person (brown) is in front, working and smiling; the lighter customer stands behind.
- Caterer is cast as the chef-owner (toque, proud, tray held high like a showpiece), not a waiter; no moustache on brown skin.
- Farmer: no straw hat and no head-wrap (a first draft had a kerchief; swapped for a bun, washerwoman-trope risk).
- Deep faces: lit on the D step at forehead, cheekbone, nose bridge; no light row near the mouth, no open mouths.
- Eyes: lid-bar on Pocket Watch, Caterer, Fence (3 of 9); round on Recruiter, Impressionist, Changer, Farmer;
  creased-shut on Prospector, Bargain Bin, the customer.
- Ages and genders: 5 women, 5 men; young, middle-aged and two elders.
