Checks:
- Shady roles span palepink (Saboteur), porcelain (Con Men behind), olive (Insider), warmtan (Party Crasher), brown (Con Men front) and deep (Anarchist); none is the darkest-only.
- Every group card puts its darkest person in front with the friendliest face (Launderers, Dabblers, Con Men, HR).
- Very deep skin is on the referee (an everyday authority, not a mystic); deep on a cheerleader and a punk (young roles). Faces lit top-left with the D step on forehead, cheekbone, nose; 1px eye whites, no bright slits; no open mouth framed by a beard.
- Service trope avoided on Launderers: a laughing couple at the wash (rolled sleeves, towel, bubbles), no head-wrap, no basket, the lighter partner behind in curlers.
- Props: whistle, pom-pom, bib "1", banknote fan, flask, ID badge, work glove, towel and bubbles, wrench, palette, spray can, binoculars, three cards, clipboard, coin. No crossbars on ties, no plus signs, no teeth.
- Eyes vary: lid-and-iris, round dot eyes (Party Crasher), closed laughing arcs (Big Spender, Launderers behind, HR right), glasses (HR), side-glance (Insider, Con Men behind), whites with iris (Cheerleader, Alchemist, Tiebreaker).
