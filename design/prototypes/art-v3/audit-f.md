Checks:
- Shady roles span palepink (Saboteur), porcelain (Con Men lead crook), olive (Insider), warmtan (Party Crasher), brown (Con Men partner, level and friendly) and deep (Anarchist, cheerful punk).
- Con Men (critic round 1): the lead-crook costume (bowler, moustache, cards) is on the porcelain man; the brown partner stands beside him at the same size and on the same eye line, both grinning. This departs from the template's behind-person depth on purpose.
- Every group card puts its darkest person in front with the friendliest face (Launderers, Dabblers, Con Men, HR).
- Very deep skin is on the referee (an everyday authority, not a mystic); deep on a cheerleader and a punk (young roles). Faces lit top-left with the D step on forehead, cheekbone, nose; 1px eye whites, no bright slits; no open mouth framed by a beard.
- Service trope avoided on Launderers: a laughing couple at the wash (rolled sleeves, towel, bubbles), no head-wrap, no basket, the lighter partner behind in curlers.
- Props: whistle, pom-pom, bib "1", banknote fan, flask, ID badge, work glove, towel and bubbles, wrench, palette, spray can, binoculars, three cards, clipboard, coin. No crossbars on ties, no plus signs, no teeth.
- Eyes vary; the lid bar is on 5 of 16 (Coach, Insider, Saboteur, Tiebreaker, Dabblers behind). Round eyes with a glint: Party Crasher, Extra Hand, Front-runner, Scout, Alchemist, Launderers front. Closed laughing arcs: Big Spender, Launderers behind, HR right. Glasses: HR front. 1px white with an iris: Cheerleader, Con Men, Dabblers front, Tiebreaker.
- Deep faces (Cheerleader, Anarchist, Tiebreaker) use the new bright D on the forehead, cheekbone and nose bridge; closed mouths, no light row on the mouth line, no lipstick tones.
- No freckles in this batch (Extra Hand's were removed); no cheek speckle.
