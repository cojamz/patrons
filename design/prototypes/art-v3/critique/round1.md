# Critic, round 1 (samples v1): Launderers 4, Kingpin 6, Night Owl 4, HR 4.5, Doorman 6, Pilgrim 5. Verdict: not yet.
Set-wide, in order:
1. Hand-draw faces; no generated rectangle masks / vertical terminator split. Each face: curved stair-step jaw, brow-ridge
   shadow row, nose built from shadow on one side, cheek plane, chin shadow on the neck. Shadows as 1-2 big form clusters.
   At least one true 3/4 head per three cards.
2. Head-size/eye-line standard. 1 person: head ~18x20, eye line y~19, shoulders from y~34. 2 people: front head 16 wide,
   back 13-14, raised 3px, back body on sphere steps 1-2. 3 people: front 15, back 12. Lock in a template.
3. ~20 colors per portrait: share the darkest step across materials, skin and cloth shadows share a cool family,
   merge any ramp step used in <4 pixels. Lights warm, shadows violet.
4. Bodies are designed: neck, sloped shoulders, a character-specific collar, one cloth fold. Multi-person: separate
   value bands + sel-out edges. No pillow shading.
5. One readable story prop per card; banned: the default half-lidded smirk. Expressions per character via brows/mouth;
   varied eyes (wide+glint, shadowed under brim, squint, glasses).
Doorway reads as a tombstone; the right-jamb lip fights the light. (Foreman's call: drop the doorway for members; the
card and seat give the backdrop. Keep the sel-out.)
Per-portrait notes: Launderers bubbles read as rivets (make light rings with glints, overlapping); striped can reads as
candy (folded linen / wicker basket + rolled sleeves); woman's face pasted block, heavy red lids, tooth notch.
Kingpin: noir shadow band under the brim; round the jaw, jowls; C-shaped ear; lapels as clean diagonals; padded shoulders.
Night Owl: wide 3x3 eyes with glint + raised brows; companion smaller, lower, darker; yawn oval; clean pompom; mug handle
+ steam; a star in the doorway area. HR: separate the three by value and collar; clean bun; redhead inside the frame;
nose; polite closed smile. Doorman: head size standard; shaped walrus mustache; sideburn; gloved salute or epaulettes;
slope shoulders. Pilgrim: shell as a ribbed fan >=7x6; cloak shaded as cloth with a fold, warm lights; brim underside
darker; braid as 2px diagonal clusters or a beard; serene closed eyes.
