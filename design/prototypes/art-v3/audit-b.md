Checks:
- Shady or comic roles span light to medium: Bouncer golden, Raiders freckled, Hot Streak warmtan, Hoarder palepink, Gossip olive, Late Riser freckled. None is the darkest in the batch.
- Service roles span tones and none is a trope composition: Cashier golden, Shopkeeper fair (recast from warmtan in round 2: a moustached shopkeeper in brown skin reads as a known cartoon stereotype), Courier warmtan, nurse palepink, Shift Crew coolbrown front + fair behind.
- The darkest person on every group card stands in front, lit and friendly (Night Owl, Shift Crew, Broker, Heiress, Martyr).
- Deep and very deep faces (Closer, Early Bird, Night Owl, Broker, Heiress, Shift Crew, Martyr, Timekeepers) get a forehead/cheekbone/nose-bridge highlight and eyes with a warm iris and a 1px white, never two white slits.
- Allowed expressions only: no tooth pixels, no winks, no half-lid smirks; the one-corner smiles (Gossip, Raiders, Undertaker) keep both eyes level.
- No crucifix shapes: ties on Hot Streak and Broker are a knot plus a slanted taper; the Hoarder's candlestick is tilted.
- Round 2 (critic b.md): no light row under any deep-skin nose (Early Bird's moustache is two wedges with a gap; Heiress's pearls sit low on the neckline at y37-39); deep faces closed-mouthed with small warm lips, warm gold irises, lit foreheads and nose bridges. Martyr hoods stay rounded.
