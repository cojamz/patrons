Checks: no service role is cast only dark (Majordomo palepink, Restocker palepink, Doorman tan is an authority at a rope, not a salute). Heroes and status roles span verydeep to porcelain. No freckle scatter (removed), no tooth pixels, no crossbar props (the crown has three points, the keys were replaced by a cloche, the halo is an oval).

Round 2 (after the critic, 6.1): the Doorman is recast fair (top hat + uniform + grey moustache on a brown face reads as the porter image) and swaps tone with the Regular (now tan); the Window Shopper is delighted, not longing (longing outside a shop on a brown face reads as poverty). Every back face now has its own eyes (1px iris + 1px white, or arcs), brows, mouth and hair. Cheek planes sit on the cheekbone, not under the eye.
Round 3 (critic round 2, ~6.9): no tone changes. Lip tones replace ink on back-face mouths; deep faces keep the new warm top step (Patroness now lit by forehead, nose-bridge and cheekbone clusters instead of a whole-face lift).
