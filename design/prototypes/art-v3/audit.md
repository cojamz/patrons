Checks:
- The threat (Kingpin) is fair-skinned; the authority figures (Doorman, HR, Pilgrim) span tan, coolbrown and deep.
- Service roles are not all light: the laundress is olive, front and centre; the man behind is palepink.
- The darkest person in each group card stands in front (Night Owl, HR).
- Night Owl's dark-skinned lead had wide, startled eyes in round 2. That was a bug-eyed risk, so round 3 gives calm, level eyes.

## Allowed expressions (widened by the foreman: these are quirky townsfolk, charm matters)
warm smile (closed, or a small open mouth with no tooth bar), laughing with eyes closed, delighted, surprised (round mouth, raised brows), worried, sheepish, proud, dreamy, sly side-glance, scheming, stern, serene, asleep or yawning, bored, polite. Shady roles span every tone; no tone is always the stern or sly one.

## Banned
- toothy grin or a tooth bar;
- half-lid smirk;
- wink;
- bug-eyed stare (on any tone);
- service-trope compositions: head-wrap washerwoman, saluting bellhop, a scowling dark-skinned figure at the back.
