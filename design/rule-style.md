# How rules are written

Cory, 2026-10-15: "rule language should be extremely precise", with no fluff. This page is the house style for every
rule a player reads: member and space text, hovers, prompts, the glossary and the rulebook. Not the log, not flavour.
Each card says exactly what happens, in the fewest plain words, and the same idea always reads the same way.

## Shape of a card
- **One opener, then a colon.** A member that isn't always on starts with exactly one of these:
  `When recruited:` · `Now and at round start:` · `Round start:` · `Round end:` · `Game end:` ·
  `Once per round:` · `Once per game:`. A trigger reads `Whenever <event>, <effect>.` A condition reads
  `While <state>, <effect>.` No opener means the rule is always on.
  (The shop's timing tag is read off the opener, so a stray "At round end," mislabels the card.)
- **Effect, then any limit.** "+1 gold per member you have." Not "You gain 1 gold for each of your members."
- **Short sentences.** A period beats ", and" or "; then". Two effects are two sentences.
- **No restating the rulebook.** A card says what is special about it. "Worth 2 followers." is a whole card; no "No effect." after it.

## Fixed forms
- **Gains:** `+3 gold`, `+1 follower`, `+1 of each other color`, `+1 gold per member you have`, `+1 purple per 2 minions`.
  Computed amounts: `Gain gold equal to …`. Losses: `−2 followers`. Never "you get", "receive", "comes back".
- **Numbers** are digits. "2 or more", never "2+". "half, rounded down" (or up), always said.
- **Growing by round:** `+2 gold in round I, +4 in round II, +6 in round III` (decided 2026-10-15).
- **Doubling:** "double". Not "twice", "doubled", "counts double" mixed.
- **Choices:** "you choose" (never "pick", "you like", "whoever you like").

## One word per idea
- **space** for a place on the wheel (never "action").
- **rival** for another player; **each rival** (never "everyone else", "all others", "other players"); **anyone** includes you.
- **in the shop** for the members on sale (never "face-up"); **the pile** for the face-down deck.
- **recruit** = a member joins you from the shop or pile. **steal** = take resources, followers or a member from a rival.
  **copy** = get a space's printed gain. **swap**, **release**, **sell** (followers for resources), **buy followers**,
  **trade** (resources for followers), **pay**.
- **followers you bought or won** (never "loose"; Cory: "what's a loose follower?").
- Magenta steals: `…, as magenta.` once, at the end.

## Timing words
- **now** = the moment it happens. **round start** = before anyone places a minion. **round end** = after the last
  minion, before the contests. **game end** = after round III is scored. **this round**, **this turn**, **since your last turn**.
- **after this one** for extra turns.

## Prompts
`<Card>: <what you decide>.` One clause, the choice as a verb: "Kickback: choose a rival for 1 gold."
Counts in brackets at the end: "(2 left)".
