# Cults — How to play

You lead a cult. Place minions on the wheel, gain resources, recruit **members** and gain **followers**. The game lasts three rounds. Most followers after round III wins.

## What you have
- **Your cult leader**: the robed figure that stands for you. It wears your emoji; your **minions** are small copies of that emoji.
- **Minions** are your workers: 3 in round I, 4 in round II, 5 in round III. Minions not yet placed wait beside your leader.
- **Followers** are your score, shown by a little-person mark. You start with 1. Each member is worth the followers beside the mark on its card (usually 1). Some members gain you more followers during play. Only followers you bought or won can be sold or stolen; followers your members are worth, and your starting 1, cannot.
- **Resources** come in four colors, one per quadrant (for example gold, red, blue and green). In costs and card text, a resource is a plain dot in its color; a dot split into the table's four colors means "any resources".

## Setup
In seat order, each player chooses 2 starting resources. Picks are public, and the shop's members are already face up. Choose the bots' level (Easy, Normal or Hard) when you set up the game. Speed (Quick, Steady or Relaxed) sets how fast bots move and moves play out; change it on the setup page or in the ☰ menu.

## The board
The wheel has four colored quadrants. Each quadrant has its own spaces and its own contest, printed on its rim.
- The **outer ring** opens in round I, the **middle ring** in round II, the **inner ring** (the 4 spaces around the hub) in round III. Open rings stay open.
- Each space holds one minion per round. All minions return home at round end.
- Each space shows its gain in a few words. Point at a space (long-press on a phone) for its full rule and what you'd get. Spinning the wheel changes nothing.
- To **copy** a space is to gain that space's own gain this round, as printed (what its chip shows). The taker's member bonuses (Grand Host's double, Caterer) and the space's gifts to others are not copied. Copies never reach the center (the inner ring) or another copy.
- To **steal** is to take from a rival. Resources a Magenta card steals arrive as magenta.

## A turn
Turns go in snake order (1-2-3-4, then 4-3-2-1, and so on). Round I's order is random. From round II, fewest followers goes first; ties go to whoever went later last round.

On your turn:
1. **Place a minion** on an open space and resolve it. A space that recruits a member does not use your buy, unless it says "as your buy".
2. **Buy one thing**, or pass:
   - **Recruit a member** from the shop: 10 face up, refilled from the face-down pile at the head of the row (its count shows what's left). A cost has a colored part (that color only) and an "any" part (any resources). The suggested payment is filled in; tap one of your resources to swap it in. Discounts ("your next member costs 4 less") and price-changing members (Penny Pincher: gold counts double) apply to every member you pay for, including one a space recruits (Snap Up, Half Price). While you hold a discount, each card shows your price, with the list price struck beside it ("free" if it costs nothing). A card you can't afford is faded; point at it to see what you're short of.
   - Or **buy followers** with any resources: 2 for 1 follower, 4 for 4, 8 for 8, 10 for 10. Only 8 for 8 is limited: the table holds 2 per player (8 with four players), first come, first served. 10 for 10 has no limit.
   - Once per round, before you buy, you may **release** one member you didn't recruit this round and haven't used. It goes to the bottom of the pile; you take back its colored part, in its color (in any colors if its color isn't on the table). The "any" part never returns, so a member with no colored part can't be released.

Members work for you in three ways: some pay at each round start ("Now and at round start" also pays when recruited), some react to what others do, and some you use once.
- An **extra turn** (Overtime, Go-Getter, Night Owl…) happens only while a space is open; with 4 players, round I has none.
- A member whose color isn't on this table is **colorless**: it gives nothing to cards that pay "its color".

## Round end
Each rim shows its contest, prize and tiebreak, and the emoji of the current leader, for example "MOST MEMBERS · ties: red · +2", then the follower mark and the leader's emoji. Point at a rim for full standings. At round end, each contest's leader gains followers: +2 in round I, +4 in round II, +6 in round III. Tied leaders all score unless the tiebreak settles it (Red: more red members). You need at least 1 of what's counted to score. The count is taken at round end, so anything spent before then doesn't count. The Reckoning pays the quadrants one at a time; tap to skip it.

## Game end
The game ends after round III. Game-end member bonuses are added, then most followers wins. Ties go to more members.

## Playing with friends
- Type your name on the home screen (choose your emoji in the lobby). One person taps **Host online game** and shares the invite link or room code. Others open the link (or type the code and tap **Join with code**) and wait for the host to start.
- The host adds bots, chooses their level and the table, and starts. Each person plays on their own device.
- If you reload or lose connection, open the same link to get your seat back. If someone leaves, the host can let a bot play for them until they return.
- Something looked wrong? Open the menu, tap **Copy game record**, and send it to Cory with a note.
