# Cults — How to play

You lead a cult. Send your minions to the wheel, gather resources, recruit **members** and win **followers**. Whoever has the most followers after three rounds wins.

## What you have
- **Your cult leader** is the robed figure that stands for you. It wears your emoji, and your **minions** are little copies of that emoji.
- **Minions** are your workers (some cards still say "worker"). You get 3 in round I, 4 in round II and 5 in round III. The ones you haven't sent yet wait by your leader.
- **Followers** are your score, shown by a little-person mark. You start with 1 (yourself). Every member you recruit is worth followers too: usually 1, sometimes more (the number beside the mark on its card). Some members win you more followers during play.
- **Resources** come in four colors, one per quadrant on the table (for example gold, red, blue and green). In costs and card text each color is a small colored symbol; the ringed symbol means "any resources".

## Setup
In seat order, each player picks 2 starting resources. Everyone can see what you pick, and the members for sale are already face up, so you can pick toward one.

## The board
The wheel is split into four colored quadrants. Each quadrant has its own actions and its own way to score, printed on its rim.
- The **outer ring** opens in round I. The **middle ring** opens in round II. The **inner ring** (the 4 spaces around the hub) opens in round III. Rings stay open once opened.
- Each space holds one minion per round. All minions come home between rounds.
- Every space says what it does in a few words. Point at a space (long-press on a phone) to read it in full and see what you'd get. You can spin the wheel; nothing else changes when you do.

## A turn
Players take turns in a snake order (1-2-3-4, then 4-3-2-1, and so on). Round I's order is random. After that, whoever has the fewest followers goes first; if tied, whoever went later last round goes first.

On your turn:
1. **Send a minion** to an open space and do what it says. Some spaces recruit a member for free; that doesn't use up your buy.
2. **Buy one thing**, or pass:
   - **Recruit a member** from the shop, which always shows 10 face up. A cost has a colored part (that color only) plus "any" (any resources). The suggested coins drop into the cost for you; tap a coin to swap it.
   - Or **buy followers** with any resources: 2 for 1 follower, 4 for 3, 8 for 6, 10 for 10. The two big buys are limited: the 6-follower buy has 2 per player in stock (8 in a 4-player game), and the 10-follower buy has 4. When the last 10-follower buy is gone, the game ends after that round.
   - Once per round, before you buy, you may **release** one of your members you didn't recruit this round: it goes to the bottom of the pile, and you take back its colored part in its color (in any colors if its color isn't on the table). The "any" part doesn't come back, so a member with no colored part can't be released.

Members keep working for you: some pay out at each round start (a member that says "Now and at round start" also pays the moment it joins you), some react to what other players do, and some you use once. Some members come in more than one copy; a "×2" on a card means you hold two of them.

## Round end
Each quadrant scores by the rule printed on its rim (for example Gold: "most gold"; Red: "most members"). The leader gains followers. That's +2 in round I, +4 in round II and +6 in round III. Tied leaders all score, unless the rule names a tiebreak (Red: more red members wins the tie). You need at least 1 of what's measured to score. It's counted at the moment the round ends, so anything you spend before then doesn't count.

## Game end
After round III, end-of-game member bonuses are added. The most followers wins. If players tie, the one with more members wins.

## Playing with friends
- One person taps **Host online game** and shares the invite link (or the room code). Everyone else opens the link, or types the code and taps **Join with code**.
- The host can add bots, pick the table and start. Each person plays on their own device.
- If you reload or lose your connection, open the same link again and you get your seat back. If someone leaves, the host can let a bot play for them until they return.
