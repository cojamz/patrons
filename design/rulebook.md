# Patrons — How to play

You lead a growing following. Recruit **members**, gather resources, and win **followers**. Whoever has the most followers after three rounds wins.

## What you have
- **Followers** are your score. You start with 1 (yourself). Every member you recruit is worth followers too: usually 1, sometimes more (shown as ★ on the card). Some members' abilities also give you followers during play; these are called **effects**.
- **Resources** come in four colors: gold, red, blue and green.
- **Workers** are your turns. You get 3 in round I, 4 in round II and 5 in round III.

## Setup
In seat order, each player picks 2 starting resources. Everyone can see what you pick.

## The board
The round board is split into four colored quadrants. Each quadrant has its own actions and its own way to score.
- The **outer ring** opens in round I. The **middle ring** opens in round II. The **inner ring** (the 4 spaces around the hub) opens in round III. Rings stay open once opened.
- Each space holds one worker per round. All spaces empty out between rounds.
- Spin the board to read a quadrant up close. The quadrant facing you is shown in full beside the board, and you can point at any space to read it.

## A turn
Players take turns in a snake order (1-2-3-4, then 4-3-2-1, and so on). Round I's order is random. After that, whoever has the fewest followers goes first; if tied, whoever went later last round goes first.

On your turn:
1. **Place a worker** on an open space and do what it says. Some spaces recruit a member for free; that doesn't use up your buy.
2. **Buy one thing**, or pass:
   - **Recruit a member** from the shop, which always shows 6 face up. Costs show a colored part (that color only) plus "any" (any resources).
   - Or **buy followers**: 2 of anything for 1 follower; 4 (at least 2 colors) for 3; 6 (at least 3 colors) for 6. Gold counts as any color here. The 6-follower buy is limited. When the last one is bought, the game ends after that round.

Members keep working for you: some pay out each round, some react to what other players do, and some you use once.

## Round end
Each quadrant scores by the rule printed on its rings (for example Gold: "most gold"; Red: "most members"). The leader gains followers. That's +2 in round I, +4 in round II and +6 in round III. Tied leaders all score, unless the rule names a tiebreak (Red: more red members wins the tie). You need at least 1 of what's measured to score. It's counted at the moment the round ends, so anything you spend before then doesn't count.

## Game end
After round III, end-of-game member bonuses are added. The most followers wins. If players tie, the one with more members wins.
