# Cults — How to play

Lead a cult for three rounds. Most **followers** after round III wins.

## Pieces
- **Minions** are your workers, small copies of your emoji: 3 in round I, 4 in round II, 5 in round III.
- **Followers** are your score, shown by a little-person mark. You start with 1. Each member is worth the followers beside the mark on its card (usually 1). Only followers you bought or won can be sold or stolen.
- **Resources** come in one color per quadrant, and there is one quadrant per player. A resource is a plain dot in its color; a dot split into the table's colors means "any resources".
- **Members** are recruited from the shop. Each is worth followers, and most have an ability.

## Setup
In seat order, each player chooses 2 starting resources.

## The wheel
One colored quadrant per player, each with its own spaces and its own contest, printed on its rim.
- The **outer ring** opens in round I, the **middle ring** in round II, the **inner ring** in round III. Open rings stay open.
- Each space holds one minion per round. All minions return at round end.
- To **copy** a space is to gain that space's own gain this round, as printed. The taker's member bonuses (like Grand Host's double) and gifts to others are not copied. Copies never reach the center (the inner ring) or another copy.
- To **steal** is to take from a rival. Resources a Magenta card steals arrive as magenta.

## A turn
Turns go in snake order (1-2-3-4, 4-3-2-1, …). Round I's order is random; from round II, fewest followers goes first (ties: whoever went later last round).
1. **Place a minion** on an open space and resolve it. A space that recruits does not use your buy, unless it says "as your buy".
2. **Buy one thing**, or pass:
   - **Recruit a member** from the shop (10 face up, refilled from the pile). Pay its colored part in that color, and its "any" part in any resources. Discounts and price-changing members apply to every member you pay for.
   - Or **buy followers**: 2 resources for 1, 4 for 4, 8 for 8, 10 for 10. The table holds 2 of the 8-for-8 buy per player, first come, first served.
   - Once per round, before you buy, you may **release** a member you didn't recruit this round and haven't used: it goes under the pile and you take back its colored part, in its color (any colors if its color isn't on the table).

An **extra turn** happens only while a space is open; round I has none, since every round-I space fills. A member whose color isn't on the table is **colorless**: it gives nothing to cards that pay "its color".

## Round end
Each contest's leader gains +2 followers in round I, +4 in round II, +6 in round III. Ties all score unless the rim's tiebreak settles it. You need at least 1 of what's counted. The count is taken at round end.

## Game end
After round III, add game-end member bonuses. Most followers wins; ties go to more members.

## On screen
- Point at anything (long-press on a phone) for its full rule and what you'd get. A faded shop card is one you can't afford.
- When paying, the suggested payment is filled in; tap a resource to swap it in.
- Speed (Quick, Steady, Relaxed) is on the setup page and in the ☰ menu. Tap to skip the round-end Reckoning.

## Playing with friends
- One person taps **Host online game** and shares the link or room code; others open the link or tap **Join with code**. The host adds bots and starts.
- Reload or lost connection: open the same link to get your seat back. If someone leaves, the host can let a bot play for them.
- Something looked wrong? Open the menu, tap **Copy game record**, and send it to Cory.
