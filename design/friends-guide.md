# Playing Cults with friends

Cory sent you a link. Here's all you need.

## Joining
1. Open the link on a laptop or a phone.
2. Type your name, pick your emoji and tap **Join with code** (the code is already filled in).
3. Wait for the host to start the game.

## What to expect
Two to four players, three rounds, about 40 minutes with four people (our estimate from 12 turns each; nobody has timed one yet). The host may fill empty seats with bots.

## Three things to know before your first turn
1. **Send a minion.** Tap an open space on the wheel and do what it says. Point at a space (long-press on a phone) to see what you'd get.
2. **Buy one thing.** A member from the shop, or followers. Followers are the score.
3. **The rims score at round end.** Each colored quadrant's rim names what it rewards ("MOST GOLD"). Whoever leads it when the round ends wins +2 followers, then +4, then +6.

The full rules are under How to play, in the menu.

## If you get disconnected
Open the same link again on the same device: you get your seat back. If you're gone a while, the host can let a bot play for you until you return.

## Sending feedback
Open the menu, tap **Copy game record**, and paste it to Cory with a note on what happened. It lets him replay your game move by move.
