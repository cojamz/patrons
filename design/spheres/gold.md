# Gold (Cory's design, in progress)

## Core
- If you're ahead on gold, you get followers or members each round (the Crown).
- Gold is the money for buying members.

## Action ideas
- Simple: +3 gold.
- Joint venture: +3 gold, and give 1 gold to someone else.
- +1 gold, and every other player who can gives you 1 gold. ("Maybe too good.")
- Jackpot (round III?): double your gold. Liked.
- Cash out (round II, so players get two rounds at it): gain 1 follower for every 2 gold, or 1 gold per follower. Something in that space.

## Member ideas (all should be people)
- **Banker** (replaces Coin Purse): +3 gold at each round start. Costs about 2 gold + 1 wild, or 1 gold + 2 wild.
- **Piggy Bank:** keep, but rename it as a person.
- **Alchemist:** every 2 of your other resources count as 1 gold.
- **Tycoon:** at game end, +1 follower per 3 gold you hold.
- Your gold counts double at round end. Maybe too strong. Variant: pick a resource, and it counts double at round end.
- Most members cost 1 wild plus some gold, but that should vary.

## Not gold, just parked here
- **Doppelganger / Mimic / Actor:** an expensive member that becomes a copy of another member. Could live in any sphere.
- Principle (Cory): effects should feel powerful and be open or relative (double, half, match, per X) rather than small fixed amounts.
