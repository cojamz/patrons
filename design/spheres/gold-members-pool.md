# Gold member pool v3 (2026-10-12)

## Keepers (Cory's pass on v2)
- Midas: you may take any resource gain as gold instead. (Cory: really good, simple, plays with other quadrants)
- Goldsmith: whenever you buy a fixture, gain 1 gold.
- Tax Collector: whenever a rival buys a fixture, gain 1 gold.
- Safety Net: at round start, top your gold up to 5. (strong; must be cheap)
- Investor: at round start, set aside any gold; at round end it comes back doubled. (round end, so it counts for scoring)
- Jackpot: once per game, gain gold equal to the richest rival's gold. (expensive)
- Thrift: gold you spend on members counts double.
- Interest: at round end, +1 gold per 3 you hold.
- Underdog: while you have the least gold, your gold gains are doubled.
- Counterfeiter: once per round, add 3 to your gold for the Crown only.
- Vault: rivals can't steal from you.
- Heir: whenever you lose a member, gain 3 gold. (replaces Insurance)
- Pawn Shop: once per round, release a member for twice its cost in gold. (pending: Cory asked if once per round)
- Merchant: whenever anyone recruits a gold member, gain 1 gold.
- Hustler: pick a color; from now on, actions there give you +1 gold.
- Pending: Fixer (the discount also works on fixtures); Generous (probably cut: it only fires on Joint Venture's 1 gold).

## Cut
Bookkeeper, Copycat, Crown Prince, Consolation, Spendthrift, Accountant, Patron, Insurance, Bulk Buyer, Collector, Early Bird, Closer, Golden Touch.
Cory's taste: no Crown-specific effects for now, nothing win-more, no fiddly timing for small gains.
