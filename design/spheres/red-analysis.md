# Red: deep pass (Claude, 2026-10-13)

## Identity
Red is quantity over quality. Lots of cheap members with weak or no effects. Other spheres make good members; Red makes many.
Each member is 1 follower, so cheap red members double as cheap followers (a cost-1 member beats buying followers at 2 for 1). That's Red's way to win, and also its risk.

## Issues, by size
1. **Runaway scoring.** "Most members" never resets. Members don't leave (Trade keeps counts). So whoever leads keeps leading: they win the ring, already hold the followers, and have the bigger engine. The contest is settled by round II.
   - Gold's Crown doesn't do this, because gold gets spent and spending vs holding is the tension. Red has no such tension: recruiting is good for everything.
   - Fix: count members recruited *this round*. It resets each round and stays live. It still feels like Cory's "most members, like gold".
2. **Hire is dead after round I.** Ring-I spaces stay open in later rounds, and Hire Up is Hire +2 red. From round II on, nobody takes Hire while Hire Up is open.
   - Fix: make the two different, e.g. Hire Up takes the two cheapest.
3. **Turnout explodes in round III.** Round III has ~20 placements, so ~20 recruits counting the freebies. Late in round III, Turnout pays ~15–20 red, triple the band.
   - Fix: +1 per 2 recruits, or cap it at 6.
4. **Trade becomes theft.** You pick both members, so you'll always give your worst and take their best. The counts stay equal, but someone loses their engine piece for a Volunteer. That feels bad.
   - Fix: the other player picks which of your members they get. It's still a swap, and both sides have a say.

## What's already good
- Hire scales on its own. The cheap end of the shop gets bought out, so "the cheapest face-up member" gets better as the game goes.
- Scout + Turnout + Hire make a real shop game: thin the row, wait, then pounce.
- Red tiebreaks + cheap red members: the sphere feeds its own scoring.
- Free members total ~8–10 extra recruits a game on top of ~34 bought. The 60-card deck holds.

## For the member pool
Lean into quantity: cheap, simple, count-padding (Twins, Volunteer), recruit triggers. Few big engines. Red's signature cards should be about having many, not having good.
