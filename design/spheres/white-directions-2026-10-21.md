# White: three directions to start from (Oct 21)

Claude's pitch for Cory. Broad shapes only: the round III action, the play it makes, how it touches the finished colors. Cards and numbers are his. Nothing here is approved.

## Where White stands

Today White trades followers: sell them for white, buy them back, and All In ("trade all your resources for followers, 1 for 1"). Under the Oct 20 economy that's the follower buy in another coat, and Cory asked "what's a loose follower?". Every direction below drops it.

What the finished colors already own: Red moves members, Green copies actions, Magenta steals, Blue runs the shop and the number of buys. What nobody owns yet: the contests on the rims, the spaces themselves (blocking), and the follower gap between players. Each direction takes one of those. Recluse ("Game end: +2 followers per rival with more members than you") joins White in any of them.

## 1. White cashes contests early [Claude's pick]

Round III: take the prizes of the contests you lead right now, instead of at round end. A contest taken early doesn't score again that round.

- The play: hold a color to lead its contest, cash it mid-round, then spend that same pile on members. Every other color makes you pick hold or spend; White lets you do both, in order, if you can get there first. A narrow lead stops being safe: "she'll cash red next turn, someone top up red" becomes table talk all round, not just at round end.
- Why it's grounded: holding versus spending is the core tension since Oct 20, and the contests are where it lives. This is the only direction that plays right on top of it. It adds no followers to the game: an early prize replaces the round-end one.
- With the finished colors: Red, Green, Magenta and Blue all score held color now, so every player has a rim worth cashing, and White's spaces are wanted beyond the White player. Magenta can break a lead before it's cashed. Green copying a cash would cash the copier's own leads.
- Rounds I and II: smaller cashes (one contest), and ways to build or match a lead in any color, so you can plan "tie it now, cash it next turn".
- Risk: it changes how a base rule reads (a rim can be "taken early"), and it softens the hold-or-spend weigh for whoever cashes.

## 2. White guards the wheel

Your note: "a defensive blocking sphere". Round III: shut one color's spaces to rivals for the rest of the round.

- The play: blocking is the soul of the draft ("will my spot still be open?"), and right now it only happens by accident of turn order. White makes it a plan: deny the round III burst (Recruit a Member, Poach, Spree, Greatest Hits) to the player who's built toward it, or keep a color's spaces for yourself. Rounds I and II: spaces that hold more than one space, or protect what you have.
- Why it's grounded: the space is the first of the three things every turn spends, and no color bends it. It's the counterweight to three colors that act at rivals' stuff.
- With the finished colors: Green can still copy a shut space's action (Encore, Echo), which is the natural answer. Squatter ignores it. Magenta and Red defenses (Lie Low, Bodyguard, Loyalists) already exist, so White's guarding is about spaces, not piles.
- Risk: pure denial gives no followers by itself and can feel bad at a friends' table; White's own payoff has to come from somewhere else.

## 3. White pays the one behind

Your note: "White rework, fewer followers as catch-up". Round III: gain followers to close part of your gap to the leader, never past them.

- The play: the follower leader becomes everyone's reference point. You can sit back early and cash in late, and the leader has to weigh how far ahead is too far. Recluse lives here naturally.
- Why it's grounded: it's your own idea for White, and it's the one direction that's a plan for whoever is losing.
- With the finished colors: Magenta's steals and Red's free recruits are other ways back; the trailer already picks first from round II.
- Risk: it stacks on the catch-up the board has (snake order, fewest followers first), and a leader may feel nothing is safe. Two players is where it would show first.
