# Red: the member-mover sphere (name tbd)

About members themselves: getting them, moving them, cashing them in, copying them. Red actions and members pay out in red.

## How Red actually works (Claude, 2026-10-10, deeper pass)
- What's scarce: (1) buys. There's one per placement, and that's the real cap, because by round III people have more resources than buy slots. (2) The 5 face-up members everyone shares. (3) Resources, last.
- So the power order of Red's verbs: an extra recruit breaks the cap, so it's the strongest thing in the game and belongs only in the burst. A swap keeps your member count and upgrades a card without spending your buy, so it's strong. A release turns a spent member into fuel, which is weak early and big late.
- Nobody owns members in round I, so any verb that acts on your own members is dead there. Red's ring I must work with no members (fallbacks), and its real verbs live in rings II and III. Red is a late bloomer by nature, which fits the burst.
- Interaction through the board: anything Red releases or swaps away goes face-up into the row, where anyone can recruit it. Red feeds the market.
- Knock-on effect on Gold: with the stall rule gone, Gold's ring-II "+2 gold, buy any revealed member" is now just +2 gold. It needs a new job.

## Actions (v3)
- I · Turnout: +1 red per member anyone has recruited this round (min 2, max 5). ≈2–4u
- I · Scout: +2 red. Look at the top 3 of the deck and put one face-up. ≈2.5–3u (it sets up your own buy)
- I · Liquidate: release a member into the row for red equal to its cost +2 (no member: +2 red). Early ≈2u, late ≈4–6u: it scales
- II · Swap: return a member to the row and recruit a face-up one, paying only the difference. It doesn't use your buy. ≈3–4u
- II · Copy: one of your members copies any member in play until round end. ≈3–5u
- III · Rally: make two extra recruits this turn (you still pay). ≈5–7u, and it's the burst

## Members (Claude draft)
- ×2 Recruiter: whenever you recruit, +1 red.
- ×2 Host: at round start, +1 red per 2 members you have.
- ×2 Scout: whenever a rival recruits, +1 red.
- ×2 Undertaker: whenever anyone releases a member, +1 red.
- ×2 Matchmaker: whenever you swap, +2 red.
- Doppelganger: becomes a copy of any member in play.
- Headhunter: once per round, swap one of your members for a face-up one.
- Twin: when you recruit a member you already own, +3 red.
- Talent Scout: you may recruit the top card of the deck, unseen, for 2 less.
- Encore: once per round, use one of your members' once-per-round powers again.
