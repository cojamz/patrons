# Member-mover sphere (name tbd)

About members themselves: getting them, moving them, cashing them in, copying them.

## Verbs Cory liked
- Recruit anywhere: an extra recruit from the face-up row.
- Swap: trade one of your members for a face-up member.
- Poach: take a rival's member, paying the leader. (It overlaps Gold's bribe member.)
- Release (Liquidate): release a member for its cost +2.
- Copy: one of your members acts as a copy of another member in play.

## Members
- Doppelganger: becomes a copy of any member in play.
