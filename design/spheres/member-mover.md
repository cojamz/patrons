# Red: the member-mover (basic layer, Claude's stab, 2026-10-12)

Gold hoards; Red spends. Red's actions pay out in red, and its members reward recruiting.
Priced against Cory's anchors (Joint Venture, Steal 3, Windfall, Strike).

## Scoring (pick one)
- ★ Most red spent this round takes the ring's followers. (The mirror of Gold's Crown: Gold rewards holding, Red rewards spending.)
- Most members recruited this round.
- Priciest single recruit this round.
- Most members of one color.

## Ring I
- ★ Muster: +3 red.
- ★ Scout: +2 red, then replace any face-up members from the pile.
- ★ Appraise: gain red equal to a face-up member's cost (max 4).
- Extra: Tithe: +1 red per player who has recruited this round (min 2).
- Extra: Showcase: +2 red; reveal 2 more members, and they join the row this round.

## Ring II
- ★ Swap: return one of your members to the pile, and recruit a face-up one costing up to 2 more.
- ★ Headcount: +2 red per color among your members.
- Extra: Poach: recruit a rival's member, paying them its cost.
- Extra: Liquidate: return a member to the pile for red equal to its cost +2.
- Extra: Double Up: recruit a copy of a member you own at half price.

## Ring III
- ★ Rally: recruit face-up members free, up to a total cost equal to your member count.
- Extra: Encore: make two extra recruits this turn (you pay).
- Extra: Payroll: +2 red per member you have.

## Members (15)
1. Recruiter: whenever you recruit, +1 red.
2. Host: at round start, +1 red per 2 members you have.
3. Talent Scout: whenever a rival recruits, +1 red.
4. Agent: whenever anyone recruits a red member, +1 red.
5. Mentor: your members cost 1 less.
6. Loyalist: at round start, +1 red per red member you have.
7. Crowd-Pleaser: whenever you buy followers, +1 red.
8. Envoy: pick a color; your members of that color cost 1 less.
9. Late Bloomer: while you have the fewest members, your recruits cost 2 less.
10. Reserve: once per round, set a face-up member aside; only you can recruit it.
11. Steward: once per round, swap one of your members for a face-up one, paying the difference.
12. Founder: when you recruit it, also recruit a face-up member costing 2 or less, free.
13. Headhunter: once per game, recruit any face-up member free.
14. Doppelganger: becomes a copy of any member in play.
15. Patriarch: at game end, +1 follower per 2 members.

## Extra members
- Herald: whenever a rival recruits a member you own a copy of, +2 red.
- Sergeant: your round-III recruits cost 2 less.
- Regular: whenever you take a red space, +1 red.
- Patron: at round end, if you recruited 2+ this round, +2 red.
- Talent Pool: at round start, look at the top 3 of the pile and put one face-up.
