# White actions, first full draft (Oct 22)

Cory's gist (Oct 22): White is "basically better exchange for followers". Round III "exchange all white for followers"; "one action could be, get rid of a member for 4 followers or something"; "we'd have to balance all these but that's the gist". Every action gives at least 1 white (his rule), and resource gains lean conservative.

Every text and number below is Claude's fill-in, not approved. Each space was played through a real turn at 4 players against placing that minion on a plain space instead (Red's Gather: +2 / +3 / +4 red).

## The ladder

The base follower recruit is 1 for 1, one recruit a turn, and at most 10 at a time. White is the same 1 for 1 with no cap and no recruit spent (Cory, Oct 22: "2 is way too many. the idea is you can have a lot of white and go above 10"). So the play is piling white past 10 and cashing it in one go.

## Round I (three spaces, about 2 to 4 total, all still worth taking later)

1. **Save Up**: "+1 white × round." Total with the +1: 2 / 3 / 4. The plain anchor; it builds the pile round III cashes.
2. **Small Wins**: "+1 follower × round." Total: 1 follower + 1 white in round I, 3 + 1 in round III. Points now instead of fuel. A follower straight off a space skips the recruit, so it's worth a little more late.
3. **Bleach**: "Turn up to 2 × round of your resources into white." Round I: up to 2, round III: up to 6. It feeds the round III cash-in, and lets a non-White player join it. It turns other colors into pile you can cash past the 10 cap.

## Round II (two spaces)

4. **Cash a Few**: "Turn all your white into followers." The same cash-in a round early (rate and any cap are his). Walked through: it's worth more than a normal recruit only once you hold over 10 white, or when you want your recruit for a member. Cashing empties your pile before round II's white contest (worth 4), so cash or hold is a real choice.
5. **Let Go** (his): "Send one of your members to the bottom of the pile: +4 followers." Walked through: a 1-follower member that's done its job (a spent "On recruit" like Stunt Double or Panel, a Volunteer) nets +3. Selling a strong member is a real loss. With Red's free recruits it's a two-space combo: Open Door takes the cheapest member, Let Go turns it into 4.
   - Flags: you said Release (the old "get rid of a member" rule) "needs to go entirely". This is a space, not a base rule, so I'm reading your idea as overriding that for White. Silver's thread also pitched a member give-away (to the trailer, for followers equal to its cost); one of the two should own it.

## Round III (one space)

6. **Cash Out** (his): "Turn all your white into followers." 1 for 1, no cap, no recruit. The +1 white comes first, so it counts. Walked through: a White player holding 18 white gets 18 followers in one go, where normal recruits would take two turns and both their recruits. Cashing drops you out of the white contest (worth 6), the last hold-or-spend call of the game.

## What moves to members
- "Take 2 followers from each rival ahead of you" (his round III pick before the exchange idea) becomes a White member: the color's catch-up.
- Recluse (moving in from Blue) fits beside it.

## Risks
- Buy followers only: White pays followers, so it helps that plan. White's cash-in is 1 for 1 on white only, and white comes in small amounts. Watch the buy-followers-only win rate (target about 25%) once it's in.
- A White player who hoards white wins the white contest and then cashes it: they can't do both in the same round, since cashing empties the pile before round end.
