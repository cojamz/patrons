# Red member pool v1 (30 effects, 2026-10-13)
Shaped by Cory's Gold taste: simple, hooks other quadrants, rival-watching, catch-up, a win condition, nothing scoring-specific.

1. Recruiter: whenever you recruit, +1 red.
2. Host: at round start, +1 red per 2 members you have.
3. Talent Scout: whenever a rival recruits, +1 red.
4. Agent: whenever anyone recruits a red member, +1 red.
5. Mentor: your members cost 1 less.
6. Envoy: pick a color; members of that color cost you 1 less.
7. Late Bloomer: while you have the fewest members, your recruits cost 2 less.
8. Patriarch: at game end, +1 follower per 2 members.
9. Founder: when you recruit it, also take the cheapest face-up member free.
10. Headhunter: once per game, take any face-up member free.
11. Reserve: once per round, set a face-up member aside; only you can recruit it.
12. Doppelganger: becomes a copy of any member in play.
13. Crowd-Pleaser: whenever you buy followers, +1 red.
14. Loyalist: at round start, +1 red per red member you have.
15. Twins: counts as 2 members.
16. Big Family: counts as 3 members. No effect.
17. Talent Pool: at round start, look at the top 3 of the pile and put one face-up.
18. Fallback: whenever you lose a member, take the cheapest face-up member free.
19. Ringleader: at round end, +1 red per member you recruited this round.
20. Shepherd: at round end, if you recruited nothing this round, take the cheapest face-up member free.
21. Proselytizer: whenever you buy followers, take the cheapest face-up member free.
22. Patron: whenever a rival recruits a member costing 4+, +2 red.
23. Muse: once per round, recruit the top of the pile unseen, for 2 less.
24. Mob: at game end, +1 follower per red member.
25. Recruiting Drive: whenever you recruit 2+ members in one turn, +2 red.
26. Bouncer: rivals can't trade with you.
27. Steward: once per round, swap one of your members for a face-up one, paying the difference.
28. Herald: whenever a rival recruits a member you own a copy of, +2 red.
29. Volunteer: costs nothing. No effect.
30. Grassroots: at round start, if the cheapest face-up member costs 1, take it free.
