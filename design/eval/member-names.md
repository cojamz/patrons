# Member names: people and groups only

Cory's rule (2026-10-14) is that a member is always a person or a group of people, never a room, object, event or concept. This list covers every member that breaks that rule, plus the borderline ones. Each line gives the old name (sphere, effect): two candidates, then my pick. The candidates follow the action-naming rules. Each is a person or group you could draw, at most 14 characters, in plain English, in the sphere's voice, and says the effect. A script checked every candidate against all 277 current action and member names and the calibration batch: none is a duplicate or over length. [Cory's name] marks a name Cory chose himself (Gold's 15, and Red's keepers and maybes).
