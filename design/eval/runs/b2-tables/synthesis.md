# Batch 2: table synthesis (tables 1–7; table 8 pending)

## Problems across the whole system
1. **Fixtures crowd out members.** 50–60% of all followers came from fixtures, and Laurel at 2 for 1 beat most members. Fix (core rules v0.2):
   - Laurel: 3 for 1.
   - Garland: 6 for 3, with at least 2 different resources.
   - Diadem: 9 for 6, with at least 3 different resources.
   - Members keep their base follower.
2. **Member decks run dry.** A stall emptied by round III in 4 tables. Fix: 8 members per sphere (already in core rules).
3. **The same ambiguities came up at every table:**
   - what members cost;
   - how many Diadems there are;
   - who counts as "leader" when players are tied;
   - members bought too late to ever fire;
   - duplicate member names;
   - "Favor" versus "followers".

   Core rules settle these.
4. **Sharing and echoing feel the same** ("take the best space twice"). Fix: split their domains.
   - **Encore** echoes only your own past spaces: memory and time.
   - **Gatecrashers** shares rivals' spaces: crowding.
5. **Dead first picks.** Gatecrash and Heist do nothing when nothing is occupied yet. Fix: a fallback gain.

## Table 8 (Encore / Auction House / Ladder / Night Shift): followers 46 / 28 / 26 / 22
- **Encore** ran away. Echoes recomputed the yield for the player echoing, so 8–13 resources per placement. Core rule: an echo copies the original taker's yield.
- **Diadem** found on turn 4. Fixtures were about 70% of all followers, which confirms the v0.2 price rise.
- **Night Shift:** Last Word goes to whoever places last in snake order, and that's the leader. Fix: give it to whoever has the most amber workers, with ties going to the player furthest behind.
- **Ladder** works as a catch-up, but "take from the leader" does nothing when the leader holds no crimson.
- **Name clashes:** "echo" means something different in Green and Amber, and "Encore" names both Green's rule and an Amber action. Core rule: names are unique, and "echo" is reserved for Green.
