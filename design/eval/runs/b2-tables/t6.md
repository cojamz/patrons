# Table 6: Mosaic / Bookie / Auction House / Gatecrashers
Final scores: Newcomer (Mosaic) 25 · Opportunist 24 · Spoiler (Gatecrashers) 21 · Builder (Auction House) 17.
Interplay: 3/5.

**Spheres:**
- **Mosaic:** fun and easy for a newcomer. Strong, and needs no partner.
- **Bookie:** distinct, with the best interplay. Tips worked as "a gift with a hook". Medium strength.
- **Auction House:** weakest. Its stall was bought out by round II. Its Connoisseur member punishes going deep. Windfall was dead in round III.
- **Gatecrashers:** the strongest swing. Sharing a rival's scaling space plus its members came to about +12 in one placement.

**Dominant line:** Gatecrashers piggybacking on rivals' scaling spaces. Diadems in round III.

**Feel-bad moments:** Pickpocket broke a newcomer's set. The player who bought the most members finished last.

**Ambiguities:**
- What currency members cost.
- How many Diadems there are, plus reshuffles and empty stalls.
- How tied followers are broken.
- Sharing versus "every round-I space fills".
- Whether a tip pays a worker who shares the space.
- When "ahead" is counted.
- Name clash: Lookout appears twice.
- "Favor" versus "followers".

**Fixes:**
1. Deeper member decks, or a reshuffle.
2. Cap sharing: no per-member or per-set scaling for the worker who shares.
3. Make Connoisseur scale off members owned.
