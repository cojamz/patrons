# Table 7: Treasury / Kiln / Caravan / Bookie
Final scores: Spoiler (Bookie) 37 · Builder (Gold) 32 · Newcomer (Caravan) 21 · Opportunist (Kiln) 19. The Diadem clock ran out in round III.
Interplay: 3/5.

**Spheres:**
- **Gold:** fun, distinct, strong. Holding gold for the Crown against buying Diadems is a real tension. Moneychanger is dead text if fixtures take any color.
- **Kiln:** weakest. Its own resource (heat) only buys Kiln members, and the stall rule blocks buying cheap elsewhere. Burning a member returns it to the deck and kills its ability. Its scoring caps are tiny.
- **Caravan:** distinct, mid. Its scoring went uncontested, a free 6. Fellowship is dead on your last worker, and Pitch Camp sometimes does nothing.
- **Bookie:** dominant. Tips nearly always pay, because every round-I space fills and 20 of 24 fill in round III. Tout plus Watcher plus Spread plus Big Book turned every rival placement into chips, and chips into Diadems.

**Dominant line:** farm Bookie chips into Diadems and buy no engine. The fixture ratios reward hoarding. Coin Purse (cost 1, +1 follower, plus income) is strictly better than a Laurel.

**Feel-bad moments:** dead Fellowship and Pitch Camp turns. A 7-cost Firebrand burned for 3 resources. Kiln stuck in last place all game.

**Ambiguities:**
- Do members give a base follower?
- What do fixtures cost, and how many Diadems are there?
- How do stalls refill, and where does a burned member go?
- Does an old tip trigger Big Book?
- Can Hedge move a tip?
- Smelter and Recast interactions.
- The Caravan spec says "Favor" where the others say "followers".
- Can you share a tipped space?

**Fixes:**
1. Fixtures need a currency rule (e.g. Garland and Diadem take 2 or more colors). Flatten the Diadem ratio.
2. Cut Big Book to 1 any per paid tip, and cap Watcher at 2.
3. Give Kiln's heat an outlet outside its own stall. Fellowship should carry to your next placement. Pitch Camp should stake wherever your worker stands.
