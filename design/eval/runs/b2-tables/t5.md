# Table 5: Encore / Vault / Caravan / Ladder
Final scores: 27 · 25 · 24 · 23.
Interplay: 3/5.

**Spheres:**
- **Encore:** strongest, fun and distinct. Its power depends on a high-yield partner to copy (Ladder here).
- **Vault:** distinct and readable. Compound into Windfall was the burst. Locking cash away meant several turns with nothing to buy (feel-bad). Doesn't need any particular partner.
- **Caravan:** flat until round III. Ties zero out its scoring in round I. Sharing never fired.
- **Ladder:** broken. The player ahead of you often holds 0 crimson, so the challenge is a free ride. Its catch-up never reaches last place.

**Dominant line:** Garland (5 for 3, 0.6 per resource). 16 Garlands were bought against 0 members in round III. Fixtures made up about 55% of all followers, and items stopped mattering after round II.

**Ambiguities:**
- How ties are broken.
- An echoed Overtake taken by the leader.
- How Bloom and Tend count echoes.
- Whether round III's center is one space or one per quadrant.
- How stalls refill, and how many Diadems there are.
- What currency members and fixtures cost.
- The timing of Windfall's fixture buy.

**Fixes:**
1. Flatten fixture value per resource.
2. Ladder: a challenger must beat the player ahead by 2 or more, and last place gets a bonus.
3. Caravan: ties pay 1 each, and Fellowship carries over to your next round.
