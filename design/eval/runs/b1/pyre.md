## Crimson: The Pyre (Martyr)
Pitch: Burn members, blaze brighter.
Verbs: release, kindle (gain ember)
Scoring: **Ashes**: at round end, +1 follower for each member you released this round, up to 1 / 2 / 3 (rings I / II / III). Per-event, capped. Game max 6.
Emits: release (by anyone's choice, public), gain 3+ at once (Bonfire, Conflagration), recruit (Phoenix free recruit, Ferryman trade-in), give (Herald of Ash spill), stall churn (released members leave, stalls refill)
Consumes: releases by anyone (Vigil, Mourner), member count (Kindle), cheap recruits (Lamplighter)

Resource: ember. Released members leave the game (base rule for the generic cult verb).

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | Kindle | +2 ember, plus 1 per 2 members you have. |
| I | Offering | Release a member for its cost +1 in any colors, or gain 2 ember. |
| I | Vigil | +1 ember, plus 1 per member released this round by anyone. |
| II | Bonfire | Release a member for its cost +3 in any colors, or gain 3 ember. |
| II | Phoenix | Release a member; recruit one free costing at most 2 more. Else +3 ember. |
| III | Conflagration | Release up to 3 members for 3 any colors each, or gain 6 ember. |

| Member | Cost | Effect (≤14 words) | Extra followers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Moth | 1 | When released, gain 1 ember. | 0 |
| Mourner | 2 | When a rival releases a member, gain 1 ember. | 0 |
| Lamplighter | 3 | When you recruit a member costing 2 or less, gain 1 ember. | 0 |
| Ferryman | 4 | Once per round, you may release a member to pay its cost toward recruiting. | 0 |
| Herald of Ash | 6 | When you release a member, gain 3 ember; each rival gains 1 ember. | 0 |

Notes:
- **Core tension.** Kindle pays you to *hold* members; Ashes and every release space pay you to *burn* them; Vigil pays you to watch others burn. Releasing costs the member's follower (−1.5u) but Ashes refunds it as 1 follower at round end while under the cap, so a release is net-neutral on score and the space's payoff is the profit. Over the cap, releasing genuinely costs you. The cap (1/2/3) is the throttle and makes the round-III burn the climax.
- **Shape:** per-event, capped, personal (no contest). Readable from the board: each player's released-this-round count sits beside their panel as a small ash pile (cleared at round end).
- **Phoenix:** the free recruit is in addition to the turn's normal buy and comes from this sphere's stall (trial stall rule). Upgrading a 1-cost Moth into a 3-cost member is the intended line.
- **Ferryman:** a trade-in. Once per round, during any buy, release a member and count its cost as payment.
- **Saint of Cinders:** released members go face-down in an ash pile and still count as followers. It turns Conflagration into pure profit. It's the intended round-III bomb, at the top price.
- **Offered members** (if an altar sphere is on the wheel) can't be released until they return. That's a generic rule about members being away.
- **Combos (generic):** cheap members anywhere are fuel, so the Pyre competes for cheap stall members. Any sphere that rewards gaining 3+ at once or recruiting gets fed by Bonfire, Conflagration and Phoenix. Any sphere that scales with member count pulls the other way, which is good tension.
- **Risks:** (1) Saint + Conflagration + Herald in round III: release 3, gain 9 any + 9 ember, keep followers, +3 Ashes ≈ 25u from one space plus items. That's a deliberate burst, but red-team it. (2) Mourner and Vigil depend on someone releasing. Offering fills every round I, so there's usually at least 1 release, but with no other release sphere they run thin (they still have a fallback).
