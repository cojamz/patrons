## Saffron: The Wayfarers (pilgrim)
Pitch: Be everywhere; leave shrines behind.
Verbs: wander (yield scales with spheres you stand in), raise (a permanent shrine), shift (move a worker)
Scoring: The Long Road. At round end, whoever has presence in the most spheres takes 1 / 2 / 3 followers. Ties: most saffron wins; if still tied, all take. Presence means a worker or a shrine.
Emits: gain (multi-colour; 3+ at once on Journey's End), share a space (Fellowship, Wanderer), mark (shrines mark spheres), frees a space (Shift empties one mid-round), give (Innkeeper), holds the most saffron (tiebreak)
Consumes: share a space (Innkeeper), rivals' placements on inner rings (Shrine-keeper), round end (Saint), release (Acolyte)

Glossary: **Shrine**: your token on a sphere's rim; at most one per sphere per player; it stays all game. **Presence**: you have a worker or a shrine in that sphere. **Neighboring space**: a space in an adjacent sphere.

|---|---|---|

Notes:
- **Arc.** Round I: spread thin or raise shrines. Shrines are permanent presence, so by round III your workers can stack where you like while Procession and Journey's End pay off every sphere you've touched. Journey's End with 4 spheres gives 8 resources: the burst.
- **Shift is the movement verb.** The worker it moves leaves its space empty, so a rival can take it later (a lure, never a toll). A moved worker's new space resolves normally but can never move another worker (L9). You still buy from the Saffron stall for that placement.
- **Combos.** Raise Shrine early, then Pathfinder and Shrine-keeper (shrines become income from both your own and rivals' placements). Fellowship or Wanderer into a crowded sphere, plus Innkeeper (warm: the rival you join gets paid too). Acolyte plus any release action elsewhere: a release that also builds a shrine.
- **Value pass** (u):
  - Alms Road 1–3 early (avg about 2), 3–4 late.
  - Raise Shrine 1 + about 1.5 presence equity, so about 2.5.
  - Fellowship 2 + 0.5–1, so 2.5–3.
  - Shift 1 + one extra space (about 3), so about 4.
  - Procession 1 + 2–4 off-colour, so 3.7–4.6.
  - Journey's End 6–7.4.
  - All within their ring bands; Alms Road starts at the low edge of round I and climbs.
  - Members (price ÷ ability EV, bought at the start of round II): Acolyte about 0.5u of net ability (fodder); Wanderer EV 1.5, price ≈ 2.4; Innkeeper EV 2.5, ≈ 3.0; Pathfinder EV about 5 (about 5 triggers), ≈ 4.5; Shrine-keeper EV 6–7 (about 7 rival inner placements × 0.5 shrine coverage), ≈ 5.5, so slightly cheap at 5; Saint EV about 4 followers + 1 extra = 7.5u, ≈ 6.0.
  - Silo index about 0.6. Spill ratio: Innkeeper 1.0. Contest total 6 followers, within the cap.
- **Lints.** L1 pass: the longest effect is 14 words. L2 pass. L3 pass. L4 pass (no takes). L5 warn: shrines need a rim slot per sphere. L6 pass. L7 pass. L8 warn: Shift on your first worker of a round gives only +1 saffron. L9 pass: a moved worker never moves another. L10 pass: Long Road and Saint pay out at round end.
- **Risks.**
  - In round III, presence saturates at 4 spheres, so the Long Road tiebreak (saffron) decides often. That's acceptable as seasoning, but watch it.
  - Saint in a 4-sphere engine yields 6 followers for 6u, near Diadem efficiency but conditional. Flag it for the red team.
  - Shift opens a space mid-round; the UI must highlight newly empty spaces.
