## Gold: The Gilded Covenant (Tithers)
Pitch: Wealth is the true faith.
Verbs: gain, sponsor (mark a space), trade
Scoring: The Coffers. At round end, whoever holds the most gold gains followers: I 1 / II 2 / III 3 (ties: every tied player gains it). Game total at most 6.
Emits: gain (gold, often 3+ at once), give (Patronage), mark a space, hold the most of a color
Consumes: buy an item (recruit: Collection, Almoner), rivals taking spaces (Patronage), member count (Tithe, Banker)

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | Tithe | +2 gold, +1 more for every 3 members you have. |
| I | Patronage | +1 gold. Mark an empty space: its taker and you each gain 1 gold. |
| I | Indulgence | Trade up to 3 resources for gold one-for-one, then +2 gold. |
| II | Usury | +1 gold for every 2 gold you hold (max +4). |
| II | Collection | +2 gold, +1 per member rivals recruited since your last turn. |
| III | Jubilee | Double your gold, up to +7. |

| Member | Cost | Effect (≤14 words) | Extra followers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fickle Donor | 1 | Release at any time: gain 2 gold. | 0 |
| Tithe-Taker | 2 | Tithe gives +1 more gold. | 0 |
| Moneylender | 3 | You may pay gold in place of any color when recruiting. | 0 |
| Almoner | 4 | Whenever a rival recruits a member, gain 1 gold. | 0 |
| Banker | 5 | At each round start, +1 gold per member you have. | +1 |
| Gilded Prophet | 7 | At game end, +1 follower per 2 gold you hold (max 5). | +1 |

Notes:
- Core tension: gold is both the Coffers score and your best currency (Moneylender). Every recruit paid in gold costs you Coffers standing. Gilded Prophet resolves the tension late: the hoard itself becomes followers.
- Fallback rule (L8): if Patronage finds no empty space (only the very last pick of round I), it gives +1 gold instead. Mark is a token on the wheel (L5 warn, same as v2).
- Values (u): Tithe 2 early / 3-4 late; Patronage 2 + rival 1 (spill 0.5); Indulgence ~2.3 (conversion + 2); Usury 3-4; Collection ~4 (2 + about 2 recruits since last turn, never dead); Jubilee 5-7 (capped, fixes uncapped Treasury). All in band.
- Members (price / total EV incl. 1.5u per follower): Donor 1/2 = 50%; Tithe-Taker 2/4 = 50%; Moneylender 3/5 = 60% (was 133% as once-per-round Broker's Scale); Almoner 4/6 = 67% (was 33% auto-buy at 3); Banker 5/7.5 = 67% (bought in R2: ~5 gold at R3 start + 2 followers); Gilded Prophet 7/~9.5 = ~74% for a gold hoarder (2 followers + ~4 from the hoard). This beats Garland's rate only if you commit to holding gold, which is the fix for 7-cost Favor items losing to fixtures.
- Dropped Vault (defensive item dangled with no taker on the wheel) and Guild Charter (trap).
- Silo index ~0.5: Usury/Jubilee compound gold, but Collection, Almoner and Patronage feed off the table.
- Lints: L1 pass (max 14), L2 pass, L3 pass, L4 n/a, L5 warn (mark token), L6 pass, L7 pass, L8 pass with fallback rule, L9 n/a, L10 pass (Gilded Prophet pays at game end, which is the final round end).
