## Sunlight: The Radiant Choir (Evangelists)
Pitch: Give freely, gather the faithful.
Verbs: give, trade (convert colors), set
Scoring: Harmony. At round end, +1 follower per full set you hold (one of each wheel color), max I 1 / II 2 / III 3. Sets are counted, not spent. Game total at most 6.
Emits: give (Alms, Ascension), gain 3+ at once, set
Consumes: gives from any source (Missionary, and sets fill faster from rivals' gifts), gain 3+ (Witness), member count (Sun Prophet)

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | Sermon | +2 sunlight, +1 more if you hold a full set. |
| I | Pilgrimage | +2 resources, each a different color. |
| I | Alms | +3 of one color. Each rival gains 1 of it. |
| II | Conversion | Convert any of your resources to other colors, then +2 of any color. |
| II | Revelation | +1 of every color. |
| III | Ascension | +2 of every color. Each rival gains 1 resource of their choice. |

| Member | Cost | Effect (≤14 words) | Extra followers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Novice | 1 | At each round start, +1 resource of a color you lack. | 0 |

| Sun Prophet | 7 | At game end, +1 follower per 2 members you have. | +2 |

Notes:
- Charisma is generosity that converts people. Gifts spill onto the table, Conversion "switches" colors, and the capstone rewards the size of your following.
- Zenith's spill fixed: Ascension gives the actor 8u and the table 3u (spill ratio 0.375, was 1.5). Alms gives 3u to the actor and 3u to the table (ratio 1.0, at the limit, round I only).
- Values: Sermon 2-3; Pilgrimage 2.4; Alms 3 (+3 to the table); Conversion ~3.4 (2.4 + flexibility, no longer a flat swap); Revelation 4; Ascension 8. All in band. No space can be dead: each one gains something unconditionally.
- Harmony now has ring caps (1/2/3), so Halo Bearer and gifts help you reach the cap rather than run past it.
- Members: Novice 1/~2.7 = 37% (cheap-recruit tier, intended); Pilgrim 2/4 = 50% (was Lens at 100%); Witness 3/6 = 50% (about 9 triggers from round II); Missionary 4/~7.5 = 53% (fed by Alms, Ascension and any sphere that gives; replaces the 33% auto-buy Gift Basket); Halo Bearer 5/~6.5 = 77% (2 followers + about 2 Harmony ticks + Sermon bonus; watch it); Sun Prophet 7/~9.75 = 72% (3 followers + about 3.5 from a 7-member cult). It's a recruit-everything payoff that rewards the main loop instead of competing with Garland.
- Kaleidoscope is cut (trap). Interplay is generic throughout: colors, sets, gives, members.
- Lints: L1 pass, L2 pass, L3 pass (Alms spill is a lure, not a toll), L4 n/a, L5 pass, L6 pass, L7 pass, L8 pass, L9 n/a, L10 pass (Sun Prophet pays at game end, the final round end).

SET NOTES (all four):
- Links (emit → consume): recruit (all) → Collection, Almoner, Tithe, Sun Prophet; give (Patronage, Alms, Ascension, Mirror Twin) → Missionary, Harmony; gain 3+ (Tithe, Usury, Jubilee, Alms, Revelation, Ascension) → Witness; any rival's space → Mirror, Puppet Master; rival members → Graft, Defection; echo (Verdant + Puppet Master) → Rite, Tend; leader/ahead → Lurk, Pilfer, Blackmail, Conspiracy, Spymaster; occupied spaces → Infiltrate, Cat Burglar, Patronage. That's 9+ links, and every sphere both emits and consumes.
- Swap test: no defensive member is left to dangle. Every cross-sphere hook (recruit, give, gain 3+, spaces, members, leader) has emitters in at least 3 spheres. Echo outside Verdant comes only from Puppet Master, but the Rite is Verdant's own, so nothing breaks if either is absent.
- Scoring shapes: majority (Coffers), threshold against the leader (Conspiracy), per-event (Rite), set collection (Harmony). Each caps at 1/2/3, at most 6 followers per game, within the 25% contest budget.
- Each 7-cost capstone scales with its sphere's engine (gold hoard, copying the leader, tripled plants, cult size) rather than being a flat +2 Favor, so none is dominated by Garland or Diadem.
- Fixtures unchanged: Laurel 2→1, Garland 5→3, Diadem 8→6 (2 per player, game clock).
