## Violet: The Veiled Eye (Seer)
Pitch: Foresee where rivals will go
Verbs: foretell (put a vision on a space), portent (name a color and get paid later), seek (recruit hidden members)
Scoring: Fulfillment. At round end, each of your visions that a rival took this round pays 1 / 1 / 2 followers (rings I / II / III).
Emits: mark a space; give (whoever takes a vision space gains 1); buy an item (from hidden stock)
Consumes: mark a space (anyone's marks, not just visions); rival placements on a color; round start; buy an item

Glossary (for the rules panel, not the action text):
- **Vision**: a violet marker on an empty space in any ring, including rings that haven't opened yet. When a rival places a worker there, the vision is fulfilled: you and that rival each gain 1 omen, the marker flips face-up for scoring this round, and it's removed at round end. An unfulfilled vision stays on the board into later rounds. A vision is a "marked space".
- **Later**: placed after this worker, during the same round.

Notes:
- **Feel.** You predict where the table will go. Round I has 12 spaces for 12 workers, so every space fills and an early vision almost always comes true: it works as a lure (the taker gains 1), not a toll. In rounds II and III about 4 spaces stay empty at round end, so a vision is a real read. Rivals can also refuse a vision to deny your follower, at the cost of their +1. That's tension through the board, never harm. A vision placed on a ring that hasn't opened yet is a vision of next round, a delayed payoff that lands when that ring opens.
- **Timing tension.** Portent and Doomsday count only workers placed after yours, so a prophecy pays most when made early, with the least information. Whoever is last in Favor picks first, which makes these a catch-up lure. Both always pay something (Portent's +1, Doomsday's minimum of 2), so there are no dead turns.
- **Combos.**
  - Twin Sight + Revelation: a vision engine.
  - Read Signs + Sign-Reader feed off any sphere that marks spaces (sponsors, plants and so on).
  - Doomsday + any sphere that dumps workers or holds them late: the late workers land after yours and count.
  - Scryer + Seek give foresight into the market.
- **Lint self-check:**
  - L1 pass: longest effect is 14 words.
  - L2 pass: only colors, marked spaces, stalls and rivals are named.
  - L3 pass: no round-I tolls; visions are lures.
  - L4 pass: no taking.
  - L5 warn: hidden stall decks need a private peek view, and Portent and Doomsday need a color token on the player panel.
  - L6 pass: one scoring rule.
  - L7 pass: 3 / 2 / 1 spaces and 6 members.
  - L8 pass: every space has a floor (Revelation always counts the vision it just placed).
  - L9 pass: no loops; The Blind Prophet places one vision per round.
  - L10 pass: Fulfillment is tallied at round end, and Portent and Doomsday pay resources at round end.
- **Value bands** (round I / II / III):
  - Foretell: 3.4 / 2.75 / 3.4. That's 1 omen, plus the chance of fulfillment times (1 omen + the scoring value). Round I runs about 13% over the band, which is fine.
  - Portent: about 2.2 / 3 / 3.5. It scales with the number of spaces per color.
  - Read Signs: 2–4, rising as marks accumulate.
  - Revelation (round II): about 4.75.
  - Seek: about 3.3, from 2 omen plus 1–1.5u of flexibility.
  - Doomsday (round III): about 6–8, with a ceiling of about 10–12 when worker-dump effects pile into the named color. **FLAG:** watch this ceiling.
- **Spill ratio** on visions: about 0.3 (the taker's 1 against the actor's roughly 3.4).
- **Contest budget:** a focused Seer fulfills about 1 / 2 / 2.5 visions, for 1 + 2 + 5 = about 7–8 followers. That's at the top of the ≤6–7 budget. **FLAG:** if mind-play confirms it, drop round III to 1.
- **Member pricing** (follower 1.5u + 50–70% of the ability's EV; per-use numbers are from the start of round II):
  - Dreamer: EV about 2 against cost 2. **FLAG:** slightly cheap.
  - Star-Gazer: EV about 2 against cost 3.
  - Sign-Reader: EV about 4 against cost 3.
  - Scryer: about 5 buys × 0.8u, so EV about 4, against cost 4.
  - Twin Sight: 2 uses × about 2.5u, so EV about 5, against cost 5.
  - The Blind Prophet: 2 visions at about 3u, plus 3u for the 2 followers, so EV about 10.5 against cost 7 (67%).
- **Silo index:** about 0.35. Visions only pay when rivals move, and Portent and Doomsday read the table.
- **Risks:**
  - Rivals may collectively boycott visions (the rich-get-richer read), so mind-play should check whether the +1 lure is enough.
  - Hidden-member peeking is information the UI must show privately.
