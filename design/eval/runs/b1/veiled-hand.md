## Shadow: The Veiled Hand (Infiltrators)
Pitch: Topple the mighty from within.
Verbs: take (only from players ahead), share a space, sway (the leader only)
Scoring: Conspiracy. At round end, every player holding more shadow than the leader gains followers: I 1 / II 2 / III 3. The leader is whoever has the most followers before round-end scoring; with tied leaders, compare against the highest shadow among them. The leader never scores it. Game total per player at most 6.
Emits: take (from ahead), share a space, echo (Puppet Master), sway, recruit from any stall
Consumes: the leader / ahead-behind, occupied spaces, rivals' spaces (Puppet Master), rivals' members (Defection)

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | Lurk | +1 shadow per player ahead of you, minimum 2. |
| I | Infiltrate | +1 shadow and 1 resource. Your next worker may share an occupied space. |
| I | Pilfer | +1 shadow. Take 1 resource from a player ahead of you (else +1 shadow). |
| II | Smuggle | +2 shadow. This turn, recruit from any stall, paying 1 less. |
| II | Blackmail | +1 shadow. Take 1 resource from each player ahead of you (none: +2 shadow). |
| III | Defection | +2 shadow. Sway a member costing ≤4 from the leader (else +3 shadow). |

|---|---|---|---|

Notes:
- New scoring shape (a threshold against the leader rather than a majority): several players can win, nobody loses followers to it, and the leader can only blunt it by holding shadow, which drags the leader into this sphere. Fixes v2's L4 fail (Favor taken from second place). The leader is set before scoring, so the order of round-end payouts never matters.
- Shadow was weakest in v2. Every space is now in band: Lurk 2-3 (lures trailing players, scales as the table spreads); Infiltrate ~3 (1 + 1.2 + share option); Pilfer ~2.5 (fallback when leading); Smuggle ~4; Blackmail ~3.5 with a fallback; Defection ~6.5-8 (+2 shadow + a member worth ~1.5u follower + ability, plus the swing against the leader).
- Harm budget: it's small and only points up. Takes never exceed what a target holds (no one drops below 0). The one follower loss in the game is Defection: once per game, the leader only, with a cost cap that protects their capstone. Sway moves a member mid-round, but followers are tallied at round end, so L10 holds. Flag Defection for the playtest feel-check.
- Members: Sleeper 1/~2 = 50%; Cat Burglar 2/~3.5 = 57% (gets you into full round-I spaces of this sphere); Fence 3/4.5 = 67% (no longer adds harm, unlike v2's Dagger); Smuggler 4/~7 = 57% (was 133% as Smuggler's Map); Spymaster 5/7.5 = 67% (2 followers + Conspiracy insurance); Puppet Master 7/10-14 = 50-70% (copying the leader's best round-III space is the capstone burst; does nothing while you lead, which is intended).
- No pure defensive members, so nothing dangles when this sphere is off the wheel. Veil, Vault and Dagger are gone.
- Echo rule (global): an echo resolves a space's effect without placing a worker and can never copy an echo, so Puppet Master can't chain.
- Lints: L1 pass, L2 pass ("this sphere" means the member's own sphere), L3 pass (Pilfer only reaches up), L4 pass, L5 pass (Infiltrate needs two workers on one space, warn as in v2), L6 pass, L7 pass, L8 pass (every space has a fallback), L9 pass, L10 pass.
