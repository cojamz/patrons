## Shadow: The Veiled Hand (Infiltrators)
Pitch: Topple the mighty from within.
Verbs: take (only from players ahead), share a space, sway (the leader only)
Scoring: Conspiracy. At round end, score if your shadow is at least 3 / 5 / 7 and more than every leader's: I 1 / II 2 / III 3 followers. Leaders never score it. Leader = most followers at round start (ties: all tied players lead). In round I nobody leads, so only the threshold applies.
Emits: take (from ahead), share a space, echo (Puppet Master), sway, recruit from any stall
Consumes: the leader / ahead-behind, occupied spaces, rivals' spaces (Puppet Master), rivals' members (Defection)

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | Lurk | +1 shadow per player ahead of you (min 2, max 3), and 1 resource. |
| I | Infiltrate | +1 shadow, 2 resources. Next worker this round may share an occupied I–II space. |
| I | Pilfer | +1 shadow, 1 resource. Take 1 resource from a player ahead (none: +1 resource). |
| II | Smuggle | +3 shadow. This turn, recruit from any stall, paying 1 less (min 1). |
| II | Blackmail | +1 shadow per player ahead of you, +2 resources. Take 1 from the leader. |
| III | Defection | +3 shadow. Sway a member costing ≤4 from the leader (you lead: +4 resources). |

| Member | Cost | Effect (≤14 words) | Extra followers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sleeper Agent | 1 | On your turn, release: take 2 resources from a player ahead of you. | 0 |
| Cat Burglar | 2 | Your workers may share occupied ring I–II spaces in this sphere. | 0 |
| Fence | 3 | Once per action you take from a player: +1 shadow, +1 resource. | 0 |
| Spymaster | 4 | At round end, before scoring, +1 shadow per player ahead of you. | +1 |
| Smuggler | 5 | You may recruit from any stall; your first each round costs 1 less. | 0 |
| Puppet Master | 7 | Once per round, echo a space a player ahead of you took (not a sway). | +1 |

Notes:
- **Shadow is a track, not a resource.** It is never spent and never taken, and it carries over between rounds. Takes reach only spendable resources. 'Resource' on Shadow spaces means any colour of your choice. Shadow members are paid in any resources, so recruiting never drains the stat Conspiracy reads (this fixes mind-play's newcomer trap). Value it at about 0.5u per shadow, since its only use is clearing thresholds.
- **Standings freeze at round start.** 'Ahead' means strictly more followers at round start. Tied players are not ahead of each other. 'Leader' means most followers at round start, and with a tie every tied player leads. The board marks this all round. Nothing a player buys mid-round changes who leads, which ends sandbagging and the late-R3 kingmaker flip. In round I everyone is at 1 follower, so nobody is ahead: Lurk gives 2, Pilfer falls back, and Conspiracy I is threshold-only.
- **Conspiracy thresholds of 3/5/7** stop one round-I placement from paying 6 followers (red-team's confirmed exploit). Reaching 7 by round III takes real investment in this sphere. The 'max 6' cap is dropped as redundant (1+2+3). Leaders now gain shadow only from base effects, never from fallbacks: every fallback pays resources. That stops the turtle where the leader blanks Conspiracy with +3-shadow fallbacks.
- **Global take rule:** a take never exceeds what the target holds. The taker picks the colour. Takes happen only on your own turn, which is why Sleeper Agent is turn-only.
- **Global sway rule:** a sway can't be shared or echoed, and the leader loses at most 1 member to sway per round. Infiltrate and Cat Burglar reach only ring I–II, and Puppet Master excludes sways. Defection therefore hits once per game. With tied leaders, the swayer picks which one. Flag Defection for the playtest feel-check.
- **Echo rule (global):** an echo resolves a space from the echoer's position without placing a worker. It can't copy an echo or a sway.
- **Blackmail** now hits only the leader for 1, with shadow scaling by the number of players ahead. This stops the last-seat sweep of mid-pack players and the Fence ×3 stack, and Fence is now once per action. A leader on Blackmail gets +2 resources, a modest lure.
- **Smuggle/Smuggler:** recruiting from another stall is the turn's one buy, not an extra one. Smuggler moved to cost 5 with no shadow rider (both judges and red-team agreed), and Spymaster moved to 4 (it was near the trap line at 5).
- **Values (shadow = 0.5u, any-colour resource ≈ 1.2u):**
  - Ring I: Lurk 2.2–2.7; Infiltrate ~3 (share limited to I–II); Pilfer ~2.5 either way.
  - Ring II: Smuggle ~3.3; Blackmail ~3.5 (leader 2.4).
  - Ring III: Defection ~6.5 (leader fallback ~5.3).
  - Members: Sleeper 1/~2 = 50%; Cat Burglar 2/~3 = 67%; Fence 3/~4.5 = 67%; Spymaster 4/~6 = 67%; Smuggler 5/~8 = 62%; Puppet Master 7/10–13 = 55–70%.
- **Lints:**
  - L1: Lurk, Infiltrate, Pilfer, Blackmail, Defection and Puppet Master are at the 14-word cap. Clarifications live in the global rules.
  - L4: takes are capped, and the only follower loss is 1 sway per game.
  - L5: show the sharing workers, the frozen leader marker, and each player's shadow against their threshold and the leader's shadow.
  - L10: followers are tallied at round end.
