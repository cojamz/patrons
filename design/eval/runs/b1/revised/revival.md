## Crimson: The Revival (Prophet)
Pitch: Outshine the faithful above you.
Verbs: preach (gain zeal, more in a crowd or from behind), testify (give to the trailing), sway (take zeal up the ladder)
Scoring: SWAY. At round end, every player except the leader who has a worker here this round, or holds a member of this sphere, challenges the player directly ahead on zeal. If you hold more, gain this round's value: I = 1 / II = 2 / III = 3. Ties fail. The leader only defends. Followers come from the supply; nobody loses any. (Shape: one challenge per rung, pointing up the ladder.)
Emits: gain (zeal, including gains of 3 or more), give (Testify and Herald to the rival furthest behind), take (Whisper from the player directly ahead; Rebuke from the leader), share a space / crowd (Sermon)
Consumes: ahead/behind (Whisper, Pulpit, Sway, Seeker, Silver Tongue, Hierophant), the leader (Rebuke), give (Hymnal Keeper), a rival's gain of 3 or more (Firebrand), crowding (Sermon), member count (Great Awakening)

| Ring | Action | Effect (14 words or fewer) |
|---|---|---|
| I | Sermon | +2 zeal, plus 1 per rival already in this sphere (max +2). |
| I | Testify | +4 zeal. The rival furthest behind gains 1 zeal. |
| I | Whisper | +2 zeal. Take 1 zeal from the player directly ahead of you. |
| II | Pulpit | +3 zeal, plus 1 per player ahead of you (max +2). |
| II | Rebuke | +2 zeal. Take up to 2 zeal from the leader. |
| III | Great Awakening | +3 zeal, plus 1 per member you have (max +6). |

Notes:
- Rules glossary (resolves the pass-0 and pass-1 ambiguities):
  - Ahead/behind means Favor as shown at the START of this round. Ties go to turn order: whoever picks earlier this round counts as behind. "Furthest behind" and "the leader" use the same ladder. The leader is fixed for the whole round.
  - Core-rule dependency (set level): pick-order ties in Favor break by reversing the previous round's pick order. In round I, use seat order.
  - "Here" on a member card means this sphere. A worker means a placed worker. An echoed or copied Crimson action does not make you eligible for Sway and does not fire Herald.
  - A gain means resources only. Followers are never "gains". "Gain 3+ at once" means one action's total resource gain to one player, item add-ons included.
  - A give is anything moved to a rival, including supply gifts.
  - Zeal "held" for Sway means zeal in your supply. Zeal on an altar, or locked anywhere else, does not count.
  - Only challengers' modifiers apply (Silver Tongue, Hierophant). The defender's zeal is read plain.
- Round-end order: Sway challenges (they read zeal held at the moment round end begins), then Seeker, then the other tallies.
- Identity: the underdog preacher. Zeal is spendable, so every unit you hold for your challenge is a member you didn't recruit. Each rung challenges the one above it once. The leader never scores Sway; holding zeal only defends against #2.
- Combos:
  - Testify or Herald + Hymnal Keeper: the gift to the trailing player pays you back.
  - Whisper the player directly ahead: a 2-point swing in your own challenge.
  - Silver Tongue + Hierophant win a late challenge.
  - Rebuke by #2 on the leader: a 3-point swing in the top challenge.
  - Firebrand watches the table.
  - Great Awakening rewards a cult built over the game.
  - Seeker turns a failed challenge into next-round fuel.
- Value pass (u):
  - Sermon 2–4 (late placers get 4, +33% over the band top, inside ±40%). It is a deliberate lure for late entry.
  - Testify 4 + 1 spilled (spill 0.25). The actor gets +1.5 over a plain ring-I space, which meets the spill rule.
  - Whisper 2 for the leader (no one ahead); about 3–4 for others (2 + 1 taken + swing in their own challenge).
  - Pulpit 3–5.
  - Rebuke 4 for non-leaders when the leader holds 2+ zeal; 2–3 otherwise; 2 for the leader. Never dead.
  - Great Awakening 6–9.
- Sway budget: the leader can't score. At most 1+2+3 = 6 followers per player, typically about 3. At most 3 challengers win per round, so the round III supply injection is ≤ 9.
- Member pricing (EV if bought at the start of round II):
  - Seeker: 1.5 + ~2 (2 zeal on about 1 of 2 failed challenges) = 3.5 (57%).
  - Silver Tongue: 1.5 + ~1.5 = 3 (67%). It only works when challenging, so the leader can't wall with it.
  - Hymnal Keeper: 1.5 + ~4 (at most 3 per round, spill from any sphere included) = 5.5 (55%). The per-rival throttle caps the cross-sphere spill feed.
  - Firebrand: about 6 triggers × 0.5u = 3, so 1.5 + 3 = 4.5 (67%).
  - Herald: 1.5 + 2 rounds × 3 = 7.5 (67%; spill 0.33). The once-per-round cap kills the Testify+Herald+Keeper stack.
  - Hierophant: 4.5 + ~3.75 (it flips about half of 2 challenges worth 2–3) = 8.25 (73%). It sits slightly above the band on purpose, since bulk fixtures should win at the top.
