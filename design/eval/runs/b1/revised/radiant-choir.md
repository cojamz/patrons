## Sunlight: The Radiant Choir (Evangelists)
Pitch: Give freely, gather the faithful.
Verbs: give, trade (convert colors), set
Scoring: Harmony. At round end, whoever holds the most full sets (at least 1) gains I 1 / II 2 / III 3 followers. Ties all score. A full set is one resource of each resource type on the wheel, counted by resource, not by color. Sets are counted, not spent. Game total at most 6, to the winner only.
Emits: give (Alms, Ascension), gain 4+ at once (Alms R2+, Epiphany, Ascension, Pilgrimage late), set
Consumes: gives from any source (Missionary), gain 4+ (Witness), member count (Pilgrimage, Sun Prophet), recruit/release (Novice)

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | Benediction | +2 sunlight, +1 per full set you hold. |
| I | Pilgrimage | +2 resources of different colors, +1 more per 2 members you have. |
| I | Alms | +3 of one color, +1 per round. Rivals gain 1 of their scarcest color. |
| II | Conversion | Swap up to 3 of your resources 1:1 to other colors, then +2 any. |
| II | Epiphany | +1 of every color. |
| III | Ascension | +2 of every color. Rivals gain 1 of their scarcest color. |

Notes:
- Charisma is generosity that converts people. Gifts spill onto the table and go automatically to each rival's scarcest color, so the giver can't kingmake and rivals don't stall the turn picking colors. Harmony is now a race for the most sets. Your gifts help everyone, but the Sunlight player's own engine (Benediction, Epiphany, Halo Bearer, Conversion) is built to win that race.
- Glossary: "per round" = the round number (1/2/3). "Scarcest color" = the resource type the rival holds fewest of; on a tie, they choose. Converting is not gaining or giving. A Poach-style payment is a gain, not a give. "Gives" and "gains" trigger once per action, not once per rival.
- Values. Benediction 2–5 (scales with sets). Pilgrimage 2.4 → about 4.8 with 4 members. Alms 4/5/6u to the actor by round, 3.3u to the table: spill ratio 0.83, and +1.6u over the round-I baseline, so it meets the spill rule. Conversion is about 2.4 + 1.5 flexibility ≈ 3.9 (the cap of 3 stops it swinging majority contests). Epiphany 4. Ascension 8u to the actor, about 3.3u to the table (ratio 0.41). Every space gains something unconditionally, so none is dead.
- Members:
  - Novice 1/~2.5: release swaps its follower (1.5) for 2.4u of flexible colors, about 40%. Releasing also costs you a member toward Pilgrimage and Sun Prophet, which is a real tension.
  - Pilgrim 2/4 = 50%.
  - Witness 3/~6.5: at most 6 triggers from round II plus its follower, about 46%.
  - Missionary 4/~8.5: about 7 give actions table-wide from round II, about 47%. It no longer depends on winning the give spaces yourself.
  - Halo Bearer 5/~7: 2 followers + about 1 set edge in 2 contests + Benediction bonus, about 71%. Watch it.
  - Sun Prophet 7/~8.5–9: 3 followers + about 2–3 from 4–6 members, capped. About 80%, priced near the Diadem rate (1.4u per follower). It is a capstone, not a bargain. Watch it.
- Halo Bearer is a single wildcard and can't complete a set alone (it fixes the round-I rush).
- Renamed Sermon → Benediction and Revelation → Epiphany, to avoid name collisions with Revival and Veiled.
- Out of scope, flagged for the set: the market rule for stall exhaustion (6 members, 2 slots; Sunlight's emptied in round II), and the Favor tie-break for turn order.
- Lints: L1 pass (max 14 words), L2 pass, L3 pass (Alms spill is a lure; the actor nets +1.6u), L4 n/a, L5 pass, L6 pass (Halo/Prophet followers via the items exception), L7 pass (3/2/1 spaces, 6 members), L8 pass (Conversion's +2 is unconditional), L9 n/a, L10 pass (Sun Prophet says "final round end").
