## Violet: The Veiled Eye (Seer)
Pitch: Foresee where rivals will go
Verbs: foretell (put a vision on a space), portent (name a color, get paid later), shift (move a mark)
Scoring: Fulfillment. At round end, each vision of yours a rival took this round pays 0 / 1 / 2 followers (round I / II / III); at most 2 visions score per round.
Emits: mark a space; give (whoever takes a vision gains 2 omen)
Consumes: mark a space (anyone's marks, not just visions); rival placements on a color; round start

Glossary (for the rules panel, not the action text):
- **Open space**: a space on a ring that is open this round. Visions go only on empty open spaces, one vision per space. A vision never blocks another sphere's marks; a space may carry several different marks.
- **Vision**: a violet marker. When a rival places a worker there, the vision is fulfilled: the rival gains 2 omen, you gain 1 omen, and the marker flips face-up. It still counts as a marked space and as yours on the wheel until round end, when it is removed. An unfulfilled vision stays into the next round. If you place your own worker on your vision, remove it unpaid.
- **Payout** follows the current round, not the space's ring.
- **Later**: placed after this worker, in the same round. Placements made in the wait phase count; echoes don't.

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | Foretell | Place a vision on an empty open space; +1 omen per open ring. |
| I | Portent | Name a color: +1 omen now, +1 per later rival there at round end. |
| I | Read Signs | Gain 1 omen per marked space on the wheel; minimum 2, maximum 5. |
| II | Revelation | Place a vision; +1 omen per vision of yours on the wheel, maximum 4. |
| II | Augury | +2 omen, then place a vision or move one of yours. |
| III | Doomsday | Place two visions; +1 omen per vision of yours on the wheel, maximum 6. |

| Member | Cost | Effect (≤14 words) | Extra followers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dreamer | 2 | At each round start, gain 1 omen. | 0 |
| Star-Gazer | 3 | Portent names two colors. | 0 |
| Sign-Reader | 4 | When a rival takes any marked space, gain 1 omen (maximum 3 per round). | 0 |
| Scryer | 4 | When you place a vision, gain 1 omen. | 0 |
| Twin Sight | 5 | Foretell and Revelation each place one more vision. | 0 |
| The Blind Prophet | 7 | At each round start, place a vision on an empty open space. | +2 |

Notes:
- **Feel.**
  - You predict where the table will go.
  - In round I every space fills, so visions are pure lures. The taker gains 2 and you gain 1, with no followers.
  - In rounds II and III about 4 spaces stay empty, so a vision is a real read.
  - The taker always gains more omen than the Seer does from that fulfillment. Refusing a vision is a choice, not a defense you're forced into.
  - The cap of 2 scored visions per round keeps a vision-crowded wheel from feeling like you're bankrolling the Seer. Extra visions still pay omen to both sides.
- **Timing tension.** Portent counts only rivals placed after you, so an early call pays most but is made with the least information. The last player in Favor picks first, so it works as a catch-up lure.
- **Combos.**
  - Twin Sight + Revelation/Doomsday: a vision engine. More visions on the wheel means more omen, while followers stay capped.
  - Scryer + The Blind Prophet / Twin Sight: omen on every placement.
  - Read Signs + Sign-Reader feed off any sphere that marks spaces. They still work on their own visions alone, with a floor of 2.
  - Augury's move keeps a stale vision live.
- **Lint self-check:**
  - L1 pass: the longest effects are 14 words (Portent), and every action is one sentence plus a clause.
  - L2 pass: only colors, marked spaces, stalls and rivals are named.
  - L3 pass: visions in round I are lures that pay the taker 2.
  - L4 pass: nothing is taken.
  - L5 warn: Portent needs a color token on the player panel. Pending Portent counts should be shown live.
  - L6 pass: one scoring rule.
  - L7 pass: 3 / 2 / 1 spaces and 6 members.
  - L8 pass: Foretell has a floor of 1 and Read Signs a floor of 2. Revelation and Doomsday always count the visions they just placed.
  - L9 pass: no loops.
  - L10 pass: followers are paid at round end only.
- **Value bands** (round I / II / III):
  - Foretell: 2 / ~3.75 / ~5.8. It scales with open rings. Round I is at the bottom of the band, and the generous lure to the taker is intended.
  - Portent: ~3 / ~3 / ~1.5. **FLAG:** a round-III Portent pays its round-end omen at game end. That needs a global ruling: either a final buy step after round III, or round-end resources in round III are worth 0.
  - Read Signs: 2–5.
  - Revelation: ~4–4.75.
  - Augury: ~3.75.
  - Doomsday: ~8, ceiling ~10.5. **FLAG:** the ceiling is +50% over the round-III center. Its omen is immediate, so it is live in round III.
- **Contest budget:** about 0 + 1.5–2 + 3.5–4 ≈ 5–6 followers, with a hard ceiling of 6 (0 + 2 + 4). That's within the 6–7 budget.
- **Member pricing** (price ÷ (1.5 + ability EV)):
  - Dreamer: 2 / 3.5 = 57%.
  - Star-Gazer: 3 / ~4.5–5.5 = 55–67%.
  - Sign-Reader: 4 / ~6.5 = 62%.
  - Scryer: 4 / ~6.5 = 62%.
  - Twin Sight: 5 / ~7–8 = 62–70%. The cap moves its value from followers to omen.
  - The Blind Prophet: 7 / ~9.5 = 74%. Slightly high, but its 3 followers bank immediately.
- **Spill ratio:**
  - Round I: about 1.0 (taker 2, Seer 2). That's at the limit, and it's intended as a lure.
  - Rounds II–III: about 0.35–0.5.
- **Silo index:** about 0.35.
- **Risks:**
  - The cap of 2 scored visions per round may make a third vision feel pointless. Mind-play should check that the omen from extra visions keeps Revelation and Doomsday attractive.
  - A round-I Foretell at 2u may be under-taken. If so, make it '+1 omen, +1 per open ring'.
