## Ivory: The Vigil (Zealot)
Pitch: Seal the faithful; they stay.
Verbs: pray (gain faith), seal (make one of your own members loyal), give (to a rival who isn't the leader)
Scoring: STEADFAST. At round end, +1 follower per loyal member you have, max I = 1 / II = 2 / III = 4, printed on each ring. (Shape: a personal capped holding count that everyone can score. No contest.)
Loyal (a seal icon on the member card): to seal is to make one of YOUR unsealed members loyal. You can't release a loyal member. If a loyal member is swayed, it loses its seal. An effect that forces you to release (for example one aimed at the leader) ignores seals: seals never shield the leader.
Emits: gain (faith), give (Gift), release (Doubter), spill (Gathering: each rival gains 1), seal (rivals' Bell-Ringer watches it)
Consumes: give, and receiving a gift (Benefactor); releases of your own members (Prodigal); rivals' seals and releases (Bell-Ringer); seals (Warden, Devotion, Matriarch)

Notes:
- Identity: the patient zealot. Ivory turns members into a sealed flock that pays a follower every round and stays put. Sealing competes with every release-for-resources hook on the wheel. Prodigal and Doubter reward the opposite path (release), so the sphere offers two ways to play: hold the flock, or churn it.
- Arc:
  - Round I: Pledge is a real either/or. Take 3 faith, or pay 1 faith you already hold to seal (a round-I seal can pay up to 3 followers).
  - Round II: Refuge, and now Gathering, both seal. With Pledge still open, there are 3 seal sources, which removes the round-II chokepoint seen in mind-play.
  - Round III: the cap jumps to 4. Covenant has a firm 5-faith floor, so it never fizzles when your flock is already capped.
  - Ceiling: 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 followers. In play it topped out at 6, because it needs 4 or more members and the stall runs dry.
- Combos:
  - Gift + Benefactor: a gift to a non-leader pays 4 faith, and a Benefactor on the receiving side gets +2. This is warm interaction. The chain ends because gains aren't gives.
  - Doubter + Prodigal: release for 5 faith, once per round.
  - Warden + Refuge or Covenant: a small seal rebate.
  - Matriarch + a wide flock: steady round-start faith.
  - Bell-Ringer: watch rivals seal and burn their members.
- Value pass (u):
  - Devotion (I): 2, or 4 with a seal. It is rarely 4 in round I and stays useful late.
  - Pledge (I): 3, or a seal worth about 1.5–4.5 for 1 faith. That is +40% at the edge in round I; justified because it needs both a member and faith already in hand.
  - Gift (I): 2, or 4 for the actor minus the 1 resource given. Spill is 0.25, and it is never dead in round I (with no leader, every rival qualifies).
  - Refuge (II): 2 + a seal of about 3 = 5, the band top. It was 6.
  - Gathering (II): 4 faith, or a seal plus 1 (about 4). Spill is 3, a ratio of 0.75.
  - Covenant (III): 5 + 0–2 seals (about 0–3) = 5–8, in band.
- Member pricing (cost as a share of EV, if bought at the start of round II):
  - Doubter: EV about 2 (50%). It stays at 1 as a single first-come card. Releasing nets only +0.5u, and members are meant to beat the Laurel.
  - Bell-Ringer: 1.5 + about 2–3 triggers = 3.5–4.5 (44–57%).
  - Prodigal: 1.5 + about 1.5 triggers × 3 = 6 (50%). The once-per-round limit stops the release engine.
  - Warden: 3 + about 3–4 seals × 1 = 6–7 (57–67%). Its own Pledge seal nets 0 faith, so the either/or survives.
  - Benefactor: 3 + about 2.5 gifts × 2 = 8 (62%). Gift is now always live.
  - Matriarch: 4.5 + its own loyalty about 1.5–3 + 1–2 round starts × about 2 = 8–10.5 (67–88%). It is a capstone and stays under the Diadem rate.
- Self-lint:
  - L1: all lines are 14 words or fewer. Gathering is at 14.
  - L2: 'seal' and 'loyal' are defined here. 'release', 'sway' and 'give' are generic cult verbs from design-principles. No other sphere is named.
  - L3: Pledge's payment and Gift's gift are optional.
  - L4: no harm is emitted.
  - L5: one seal icon, plus a player-panel counter of loyal members against the current cap.
  - L6: STEADFAST is the only follower rule.
  - L7: 3/2/1 spaces, 6 members, costs 1/2/3/4/5/7.
  - L8: every space has a faith floor. Bell-Ringer and Prodigal consume the generic release verb, which Doubter emits in-sphere.
  - L9: Benefactor fires once per effect, and gains aren't gives.
  - L10: STEADFAST and member followers are counted at round end.
- Rules this sphere needs from the global rules:
  - Released members leave the game.
  - 'Give' means an effect whose text says give; gains from a rival's effect don't count.
  - An empty stall means you may buy only a fixture.
  - The leader is the player with strictly the most followers at the last round end. A tie means no leader, so round I has none.
  - A member's extra followers are banked by the buyer and don't move with a sway.
  - Costs are paid from resources held before the action resolves.
  - 'Round start' effects first fire in the round after purchase.
  - Matriarch's 'Arrives loyal' is not a seal, so Warden doesn't trigger.
- Risks:

  - All income is faith. Revisit once fixture cost colors settle.
  - A 6-member stall ran dry by mid-round II at 4 players. That is a global market-depth question, not Ivory's alone.
