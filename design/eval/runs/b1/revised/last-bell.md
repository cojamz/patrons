## Amber: The Order of the Last Bell (Keeper)
Pitch: Strike first, or speak last
Verbs: hasten (place now), bide (wait, place after everyone), echo (repeat your previous move)
Scoring: The Last Word. A Bell token moves to each worker placed in this sphere. At round end, its holder gains 1 / 2 / 3 followers (by round; printed on rings I / II / III).
Emits: echo; out-of-turn placements (place-now, waiting); share a space (waiting workers join occupied spaces here); give (a joined occupant gains 1 sand)
Consumes: workers placed this round (anyone's); rivals' echoes and out-of-turn placements; ahead / behind (wait order); round start / round end

Glossary (rules panel, not action text):
- **Hold**: your workers not yet placed this round. Waiting and extra workers are not held.
- **Place now**: your next worker places immediately. Your seat passes on the snake turn that worker would have used. The snake order is unchanged.
- **Wait**: the worker goes to your Bell tray. After all regular workers are placed, waiting workers place one at a time, highest Favor first (ties: later seat first). A waiting worker may join an occupied space in this sphere and take its effect, and that space's occupant gains 1 sand. Elsewhere it needs an open space. Waiting workers count as placements and get a buy.
- **Out of turn**: any placement not made on its own snake turn (place now, waiting, extra workers).
- **Echo**: copies an effect's gains only, not "place now" or "wait". Counts are read at the moment of the echo. An echo is not a placement and can't be echoed.
- **The Bell**: moves only when a worker is placed in this sphere. Moving a worker out does not move it.
- **Bide on your last worker**: gives only the sand.

Notes:
- **Feel.** It's about when, not what.
  - Dawn, Hasten, Midnight and Keeper of Hours pay you to finish early.
  - Dusk, Bide, Sleeper and the Bell pay you to finish late.
  - Waiting workers can now always contest the Bell, because they may join occupied spaces here. Rivals can't shut the late game out just by filling Amber.
  - The wait order is highest Favor first, so among waiters the trailing player speaks last. Harm and advantage point up.
  - A joined occupant gets 1 sand, so being sniped still pays a little.
- **Fixes from pass 0:**
  - Midnight no longer dumps every worker, so there's no center sweep and no Diadem drain.
  - Recur can't echo centers or timing effects.
  - The Undying Hour can't take the Bell.
- **No round-I tolls.**
  - Dusk is the last picker's lure: up to 4 in round I. That's justified as a lure, since it lands at the forced seat.
  - Waiting is optional.
  - Hasten on a snake-turn pick still gives 2 sand.
  - **FLAG:** a round-I waiter that joins an Amber space leaves one round-I space empty. That bends "every round-I space fills"; check it with Cory.
- **Combos.**
  - Hasten into a contested ring-II space, then Recur on your next pick to echo it.
  - Bide or Sleeper to take the Bell late.
  - Keeper of Hours with Hasten or Midnight: finish early and get paid before your last buy.
  - Bell-Ringer with Dawn early and Dusk late.
  - Chronicler on any table that echoes or rushes.

- **Value bands** (round I / II / III):
  - Hasten: about 2.5 / 3.5 / 5 (2/3/4 sand plus 0–3u of tempo depending on seat).
  - Bide: about 2.5 / 3.5 / 5 (2/3/4 sand plus the chance to take the Bell).
  - Dusk: about 2.8 / 3.5 / 4.3, and 7 when taken late in round III.
  - Dawn: about 4 early in round II, 2 late; about 5 early in round III.
  - Recur: about 4 in round II; 4–5 in round III (ring-II targets only).
  - Midnight: about 6 sand + 1.5u of tempo ≈ 7.5 early (the center of the band), about 3 late. Re-check in mind-play that it still reads as the burst.
- **Contest budget:** 1 + 2 + 3 = 6 followers, at the top of the cap. Waiting now makes it contestable, not a purchase.
- **Member pricing** (1.5u per follower + 50–70% of ability EV):
  - Early Riser: about 1.2 sand × 2 rounds → price 2.1–2.4, cost 2.
  - Bell-Ringer: about 3 uses × 1 → price 3–3.6, cost 3.
  - Chronicler: about 4–6 triggers × 0.5–0.8 → EV about 3 → price about 3–3.6, cost 3.
  - Keeper of Hours: about 3 sand per round × 2, now paid before your last buy (not lost at the end of round III) → EV about 6 → price about 4.5–5.7, cost 4 (low edge; watch it).
  - Sleeper: 2 sand + wait value + about 40% × 5 followers → EV about 6–7 → price about 4.5–6, cost 5.
  - The Undying Hour: 2 followers (3u) + 2 extra placements with buys (about 6–7u) → total about 9.5, cost 7 (about 70%).
- **Silo index:** about 0.5.
- **Spill:** waiting into an occupied space gives the occupant 1 sand, a spill ratio of about 0.3. Place-now and waiting feed rivals' pace and out-of-turn readers.
