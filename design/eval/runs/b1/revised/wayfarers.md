## Saffron: The Wayfarers (pilgrim)
Pitch: Be everywhere; leave shrines behind.
Verbs: wander (yield scales with spheres you're present in), raise (a permanent shrine), shift (move a worker)
Scoring: The Long Road. At round end, whoever has the most shrines takes 1 / 2 / 3 followers. Ties: all tied players take 1 less.
Emits: gain (multi-colour; 3+ at once on Procession and Journey's End), share a space (Fellowship, Wanderer; the only way into a rival's taken centre), mark (shrines mark spheres), frees a space (Shift empties one mid-round), give (Innkeeper), release (Acolyte)
Consumes: share a space (Innkeeper), rivals' placements on ring II (Shrine-keeper), round end (Saint), release (Acolyte)

Glossary:
- **Shrine**: your token on a sphere's rim. At most one per sphere per player. It stays all game.
- **Presence**: you have a worker or a shrine in that sphere.
- **Share**: place on a space a rival already holds, and resolve it too. A space holds at most 2 workers.
- **Shift move**: the moved worker goes to an outer-ring space in a sphere adjacent to its current one.
  - The move is not a placement, so nothing that triggers on placing fires.
  - You make one buy, from the stall of the space taken.
  - You may not take the space you vacated again this round. Rivals may.

Notes:
- **Arc.**
  - Round I: spread thin, or raise a shrine.
  - Shrines are scarce: Raise Shrine is one space a round, plus Acolyte. They are permanent presence and the only thing Long Road counts.
  - By round III your workers can stack where you like, while Procession and Journey's End pay off every sphere you've touched.
  - Journey's End at 4 spheres gives 8 resources: the burst.
- **Shift.**
  - Bounded to the outer ring, so it is at most one extra round-I-style action, never a centre.
  - The vacated space is a lure for rivals only, because you can't retake it. That kills the vacate-and-retake loop.
  - The "Else +3" fallback clears L8.
- **Sharing has a job.** Sharing is the only way into a rival's taken centre or ring-II space, so Fellowship and Wanderer matter in round III even with spaces in surplus. Innkeeper pays both sides.
- **Combos.**
  - Raise Shrine or Acolyte early, then Pathfinder and Shrine-keeper. Shrines become income from both your own and rivals' placements.
  - Fellowship into a rival's centre, plus Innkeeper: the host gets paid too.
  - Acolyte plus any release action elsewhere.
  - Multi-colour Wayfarer income into any converter, then into a fixture.
- **Value pass** (u):
  - Alms Road: 2 floor, 2–3 in round I, 3–4 late.
  - Raise Shrine: about 1.5 presence equity, plus a Long Road share (1–3 followers × P) and Saint/Pathfinder feed. About 2.5–3 in round I. The "+3 saffron" fallback is for when you already have all 4 shrines.
  - Fellowship: 2 + 0.5 option early, about 4–5 in round III when sharing a rival's centre. It climbs as intended.
  - Shift: an outer space (2.5–3.5) + tempo (about 1), so 3.5–4.5 in rounds II and III. The fallback is 3.
  - Procession: 1 + (presence − 1) × 0.9, so 2.8–3.7.
  - Journey's End: 4–8. It is 2 for someone who stands only in Saffron; that is a reward for investment, not a table lure.
  - Members, priced at 1.5 + 50–70% of ability EV, bought at the start of round II:
    - Acolyte: release costs −1.5 and the shrine is worth about 2–2.5 now that shrines score. It clears the release rule.
    - Wanderer: EV about 2, so ≈ 2.5.
    - Innkeeper: about 3 triggers, so ≈ 3.
    - Pathfinder: 2–3 shrines × 9 placements, about 4.5–6 triggers, so ≈ 4.
    - Shrine-keeper: about 9 rival ring-II placements × 0.5–0.75 coverage, so 5–7 triggers ≈ 5.
    - Saint: (shrines − 1) × 2 round ends + base. That is 3 followers at 2 shrines, 5 at 3, and 7 at 4 (the maximum, which needs heavy investment): 2.3 / 1.4 / 1.0 u per follower against the Diadem's 1.33. The top end is the capstone, reachable by one player.
  - Silo index about 0.6–0.65, because centre-sharing and Shrine-keeper lean on rivals.
  - Spill ratio: Innkeeper 1.0.
  - Contest total: 6 followers. Ties shave 1 each, which limits table inflation.
- **Lints.**
  - L1 pass: the longest effects are 14 words.
  - L2 pass.
  - L3 pass: Raise Shrine is a pure lure.
  - L4 pass.
  - L5 warn: shrines need a per-player rim slot per sphere. Shift needs newly-empty and locked-space highlighting.
  - L6 pass.
  - L7 pass.
  - L8 pass: Shift and Alms Road now have floors. Journey's End's floor is 2.
  - L9 pass: a Shift move is not a placement, so it can't chain. Sharing is capped at 2 per space and is rival-only.
  - L10 pass.
- **Risks.**
  - Saint and Long Road both score shrines, a double dip on one axis. Items are exempt from L6, but watch the top end.
  - Round-I Long Road (1 follower) may still make Raise Shrine seat 1's pick. An Acolyte buy can tie it to 0.
  - Global rules the spec relies on: members are paid in the stall's colour or any colour, and the turn-order tiebreak when Favor is equal.
