## Crimson: The Pyre (Martyr)
Pitch: Burn members, blaze brighter.
Verbs: release, kindle (gain ember)
Scoring: **Ashes**: at round end, +1 follower per member you released this round, from any source; max 1 / 2 / 3 in rounds I / II / III. Per-event, capped, personal. It refunds the follower a release costs, so under the cap a release is score-neutral.
Emits: release (public, from any source), gain 3+ at once (Bonfire, Conflagration with 2+ releases), recruit (Phoenix, Ferryman), give (Herald spill to the next player), stall churn (released members return to their deck)
Consumes: releases by anyone (Vigil, Mourner), member count (Kindle), cheap recruits (Lamplighter)

Resource: ember.
Base rule for the generic verb: a released member goes to the bottom of its sphere's member deck. An item's effect ends when it leaves you. All follower totals, member followers included, are tallied at round end, so a release shows as a pending −1/+1 on the ash pile, never as a live swing.

Notes:
- **Core tension.** Kindle pays you to hold members, one ember each. Release spaces pay a flat 2 any per member, and Ashes refunds the follower while you're under the cap. A burn costs you the member's ability and a point of Kindle, and gains about 1–2u over the fallback, so it is a real choice rather than a coupon. Over the cap, burning really costs a follower. The ladder (release 1 / up to 2 / up to 3, matching the Ashes cap of 1 / 2 / 3) saves the big burn for round III.
- **Releases from any source count** for Ashes, Vigil, Mourner, Moth and Herald. That includes other spheres' release spaces and Ferryman. Mourner and Vigil count each member: a 3-member Conflagration is 3 releases.
- **Phoenix:** the free member *is* your buy this placement. It comes from this sphere's stall (trial rule) and costs at most 2 more than the member released. Upgrading a Moth into a 3-cost member is the intended line.
- **Ferryman:** during any buy, release one member and its printed cost (max 3) counts as payment. It counts as a release.
- **Saint of Cinders:** each round, your first release puts a follower token on your ash pile, which is permanent. It applies only to releases made after Saint is recruited, and it stacks with the Ashes refund, so it nets +1 follower per round. Saint pays off on any release space, so it doesn't depend on Conflagration.
- **Herald of Ash** doesn't fire on its own release. In a multi-release, resolve one at a time in the order you choose.
- **Offered members** (if an altar sphere is on the wheel) can't be released until they return. That's a generic rule about members being away.
- **Shape:** per-event, capped, personal. Each player's ash pile beside their panel shows releases this round against the cap, plus Saint's permanent tokens.
- **Combos (generic):** cheap members anywhere are fuel. Spheres that reward gaining 3+ at once or recruiting get fed by Bonfire, Conflagration and Phoenix. Spheres that scale with member count pull against burning, which is good tension. Herald plus another sphere's release space runs about +2u over that space's band. That is accepted, since it's what a 7-cost item buys.
- **Risks:**
  1. The R-III centre with Herald, Saint and Moth comes to about 15–16u: 7.2 + 6 + 1.5 + 1. That is a deliberate burst bought with 13 cost of items. Re-run the red team.
  2. Moth is at the 40% auto-buy line. It's justified as fuel: it spends a buy and gives up its follower when used.
  3. At a table with no other release sphere, Mourner and Vigil run thinner, but both have floors.

Value pass (u):
- **Kindle:** 2 in R-I, 3–4 in R-II, 5–6 in R-III. Ring-I base, scales with members.
- **Offering:** release 3.4 (+36% vs the 2.5 band, before the lost ability); fallback 2.
- **Vigil:** 2 early, 3–5 late. Late value is capped at +3.
- **Bonfire:** 2 releases = 4.8 (+20% vs the 4 band); 1 release = 2.4, worse than the fallback of 3, so a lone burn is a real read.
- **Phoenix:** about 3–4. It saves the cost of a c+2 member and loses the old member's ability; the follower is refunded. Fallback 3.
- **Conflagration:** 3 releases = 7.2, in band; fallback 5. With items: Herald +6, Saint +1.5, Moth +1, about 15.7 total.
- **Member price as a share of EV** (bought at start of R-II):
  - Moth: 1 / 2.5 = 40%, borderline, justified as fuel.
  - Lamplighter: 2 / (1.5 + about 3) = 44–50%.
  - Mourner: 3 / (1.5 + 4–5 triggers at 1u) = 46–55%.
  - Ferryman: 4 / (1.5 + 2 uses × about 3) = 53%.
  - Saint: 5 / (4.5 + 2 rounds × 1.5) = 67%.
  - Herald: 7 / (about 5 releases × 2 + 1.5) = 61%. Spill ratio 1/2 = 0.5.
- **Favor:** Ashes nets 0 under the cap (it is a refund and a throttle). The sphere's net Favor comes through resources into fixtures, plus Saint at up to +3 over a game.
- **Silo index:** about 0.55. Kindle and the release spaces compound on your own members; Vigil and Mourner are fed by the table; fuel comes from every stall.
