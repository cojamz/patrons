## Violet: The Altar (Hierophant)
Pitch: Lay it down, reap at dusk.
Verbs: offer (move your own resources or a member onto your altar), consecrate (put incense from the supply onto your altar)
Scoring: **The Rite**: at round end, if your altar value reaches this ring's number, gain its followers. Printed per ring: I: 4 → +1 · II: 6 → +2 · III: 8 → +3. One check per round, not a ladder. Threshold, non-exclusive. Game max 6.
Emits: gain (incense), give (Benediction gives every rival incense), buy a fixture (Great Rite, at round end), shared stake (every altar is public, and anyone can clear the Rite)
Consumes: rivals buying fixtures (Cantor), resources you can't spend yet (leftovers become altar value), members as tokens (any member counts 2 on the altar)

**Altar rules** (shown on each player panel when this sphere is on the wheel):
- Altar value = 1 per resource on it + 2 per member. There are no other counting rules.
- Altar contents are still yours: they count for hold checks and as followers/members, and can be swayed like anything you hold. They can't be spent, and members' abilities sleep.
- Thurifer adds after any cap and never retriggers itself.
- Round end, after every other sphere's round-end checks: check the Rite → Great Rite buy (only altar resources pay) → everything on the altar returns to you, including consecrated incense.

| II | Sanctify | Put incense on your altar equal to its value (min 2, max 4). |

Notes:
- **Core tension.** The altar is a locked savings box. Everything on it comes back at dusk, consecrated incense included. Nothing is lost, but you can't spend it this round. Every offer trades this round's buying power for the Rite. In round III, returned contents are worthless after the game, so R3 consecration is pure Rite fuel, and Great Rite is the only way to cash a full altar.
- **Post-threshold play.** Censer and Vespers pay more once your altar meets this round's Rite. Clearing early turns the rest of your round into income, so it isn't dead.
- **Benediction** is the warm space. Rivals get spendable incense in hand. Gifts never land on rival altars, so the last picker can't tip someone's threshold (removes the kingmaker lever).
- **Great Rite** buys exactly one fixture with altar resources (members can't pay; Diadem limits apply). Typical use: 8–12 leftover resources become a Diadem or Garland.
- **Combos (generic):**
  - Cheap members are altar tokens worth 2 each.
  - A hold-the-most sphere likes the altar: offered resources count as held but can't be spent away.
  - Cantor watches the fixture race.
  - Deacon + Thurifer + Sanctify is the incense engine (Sanctify caps at 4; Thurifer adds after the cap).
- **Risks:**
  - (1) If every player clears every Rite, it inflates without separating anyone. If the next mind-play shows that, raise the thresholds to 5/7/9.
  - (2) Great Rite is a single R3 space decided by turn order. It needs the global Favor tie-break defined.
  - (3) Round-start members (Deacon, Oracle) are dead buys in R3. The UI should grey their ability once no round start remains.
  - (4) Pool-level: Veiled Eye is also printed Violet. One of them must change color before both can sit on the wheel.

Value pass (u). Consecrated incense is worth about 0.8u (delayed), and 0 in R3 except through Rite or Great Rite:
- **Censer:** 2, or 3 once the Rite is met. **Offer:** 1.2 + Rite progress ≈ 2–2.5. **Benediction:** about 2.4 delayed + about 0.6 progress ≈ 3. Spill is 3 × 0.9 = 2.7, ratio 0.9.
- **Vespers:** 3, or 5 once met. **Sanctify:** 2.5 (empty altar) to about 4.5 (4 placed).
- **Great Rite:** 3 incense of Rite progress + converting about 8–12 worthless leftovers into one discounted fixture ≈ 7–9.
- **Member price as a share of EV** (bought at round-II start):

  - Deacon ≈ 1.5 + 2 × (1.6 + 0.7) = 6.1 → 49%.
