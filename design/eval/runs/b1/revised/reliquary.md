## Ivory: The Reliquary (curator)
Pitch: Covet, unveil, and anoint members.
Verbs: reserve (set a stall member aside for yourself), refresh (redeal a stall), recruit (extra and discounted buys); also uses the generic verb release
Scoring: The Fold. At round end, whoever recruited the most members this round takes 1 / 2 / 3 followers (rounds I / II / III). Ties all take one less. Reserving doesn't count. Recruiting from the reserve, Induction and Anoint all count. Releasing doesn't reduce the tally.
Emits: buy an item (often 2 per turn late), buys from any stall, refresh (new stall members for everyone), release (members return to their decks), gain (Votive)
Consumes: rivals' expensive recruits (Watcher), its own member count (Votive, Archivist), its own recruit tally (Seer, Fold), release (Relinquish)

Glossary:
- **Reserve.** Move a stall member onto your panel. The stall refills at once. You hold at most 2; if full, swap one out to the bottom of its deck. Only you may recruit it, as any later buy. You may pay for it in ivory. An unrecruited reserved member goes to the bottom of its deck at the end of the round after you reserved it.
- **Refresh.** Put a stall's members under its deck and deal 2 new ones.
- **Release.** Discard your member to the bottom of its deck; you lose its follower. You can't release a member recruited this round.
- **Costs.** Costs mean printed costs. Discounts stack, but a paid recruit never costs less than 1. Free stays free.

Notes:
- **Arc.**
  - Round I: Covet the member you want, Unveil to find cheap ones, and Votive pays as your following grows.
  - Round II: Induction gives a double recruit, and Relinquish turns old members into cash.
  - Round III: Anoint is a free member up to 6. The reserved member you Coveted in round II is ready to pull. Seer and Archivist turn the shopping spree into the burst.
  - The sphere makes a table explosive (S7), now with a bounded ceiling.
- **The market is the board.**
  - Covet denies a member to everyone else, but only for about a round.
  - Unveil reshuffles what everyone can buy.
  - Watcher makes you root for rivals' big buys.
  - Released members come round again.
- **Combos.**
  - Covet in round II, then Anoint the reserved 6-cost in round III. Sacristan cheapens other reserve pulls.
  - Novice + Votive hits the cap sooner.
  - Steward + Induction gives cheap double recruits that take the Fold.
  - Relinquish an old member whose release ability fires.
- **Value pass** (u):
  - Votive: 2 early, 3–5 late (capped). This exceeds the ring-I band late on purpose: round-I spaces must scale, and late it competes with the 7u center.
  - Covet: 1 + about 1 flex + about 0.5 denial (time-limited) + about 0.5 for paying in ivory = 3.
  - Unveil: 1 + 0.5 info + about 0.5–1 any-stall, so 2–2.5.
  - Induction: 1 discount + 1 flex + about 1.5 extra-buy tempo, so about 3.5. The fallback when broke is 2.
  - Relinquish: flat 3. Releasing a 4-cost gives 4 − 1.5 = 2.5, a 6-cost 5 − 1.5 = 3.5, a 7-cost 5.5 − 1.5 = 4. All within the ring-II band of 3–5.
  - Anoint: average member up to 6 ≈ 3.5u + 1 flex + about 1 Fold equity, so about 5.5–7.

- **Contests and spill.**
  - Fold contest total: 6 followers per winner. With ties taking one less, a full-table tie in round I pays nobody.
  - Silo index about 0.6. Spill ratio about 0, which is a known gap against "many actions spill" (see Risks).

- **Risks.**
  - Unveil can still bury a stall member a rival was waiting for (not aimed at the leader). Consider restricting it to the Ivory stall or one you placed in this round.
  - Ivory spills nothing. Consider a small spill on Unveil.
  - Global rule needed: members are paid in their stall's color. Ivory's reserve payment rule depends on it.
