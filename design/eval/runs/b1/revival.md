## Crimson: The Revival (Prophet)
Pitch: Outshine the faithful above you.
Verbs: preach (gain zeal, more in a crowd), sway (take from a player ahead), recruit
Scoring: SWAY. At round end, +1 follower for each player ahead of you who has less zeal than you, counting at most 2 such players. Ring values per player: I = 1 / II = 1 / III = 2. A leader with no one ahead scores the ring value once instead, if they hold the most zeal. Swayed followers come from the supply, so nobody loses Favor. (Shape: a pairwise comparison against those above you, not a majority.)
Emits: gain (zeal, including gains of 3 or more), give (Testify), take (Whisper takes zeal, Poach takes a member, both only from a player ahead), buy an item (an extra recruit), share a space (Sermon draws a crowd)
Consumes: ahead/behind (Pulpit, Sway, Poach, Herald, Fickle Convert), give (Hymnal Keeper), a rival's gain of 3 or more (Firebrand), share a space (Sermon), member count (Great Awakening)

| I | Sermon | +2 zeal, or +3 if a rival is already in this sphere. |
| I | Testify | +3 zeal. Give a rival 1 zeal from the supply. |

Notes:
- Identity: the underdog preacher. Zeal is a real currency, so every unit you hold back for Sway at round end is a member you didn't recruit. Same spend-or-hold tension as the old gold contest, but compared player by player against whoever sits above you.
- How sway works under the principles: follower sway pays out from the supply (Favor goes down only rarely, and only from the leader). The real sway is of zeal (Whisper) and of members (Poach). Poach pays the victim the member's full cost, so the leader is forced to sell rather than robbed. That's small harm, aimed up, and even the victim gets paid.
- Poach is an extra recruit on top of your normal one-per-placement buy. Its stall-or-rival choice means it is never dead for the leader. A member transferred mid-round moves its follower, which is tallied at round end (consistent with L10).
- Fickle Convert: if several players behind you have more zeal, it goes to the one with the most (ties go to the one furthest behind). It resolves before the round-end tally. It's a cheap member with a built-in risk. If it is made loyal or otherwise protected from being taken, it stays put (a nice generic combo).
- Combos: Testify + Hymnal Keeper (the gift pays you back). Whisper both raises your zeal and lowers theirs, a 2-point swing in the Sway comparison. Silver Tongue + Hierophant lock up Sway late. Firebrand watches the table for big gains from any sphere. Great Awakening rewards a cult built over the game: the round-3 burst.
- Value pass (u): Sermon 2–3; Testify 3, with 1 spilled to a rival (spill 0.33); Whisper about 2.5 counting the swing; Pulpit 2–5, about 4 on average; Poach about 3.5–4 (2u for the extra buy, plus choice, plus a member that isn't on any stall); Great Awakening 7–9 late, at the top of the band.
- Member pricing (EV if bought at the start of round II, then cost as a share of EV): Fickle Convert about 1.5u (67%); Silver Tongue 1.5 + about 2 = 3.5 (57%); Hymnal Keeper 1.5 + about 4 = 5.5 (55%); Firebrand 1.5 + 12 triggers × 0.5 = 7.5 (53%); Herald 1.5 + 3 uses × 2 = 7.5 (67%; rivals behind gain about 1.5 per use, spill under 1); Hierophant 4.5 + about 3.5 = 8 (75%, a little rich for a capstone, which is deliberate since the bulk fixtures should win at the high end).
- Self-lint:
  - L1 pass (longest are Sermon, Whisper and Poach at 13 or fewer).
  - L2 pass (no other sphere named; zeal is this sphere's own resource).
  - L3 pass (no round-I cost).
  - L4 pass (takes only from a player ahead, and only when they have it; Poach compensates; Fickle Convert moves up the table only).
  - L5 pass (zeal counts are already on player panels; Sway reads off Favor order plus zeal).
  - L6 pass (Sway is the only source of followers).
  - L7 pass (3/2/1 spaces, 6 members).
  - L8 pass (Whisper and Poach have fallbacks).
  - L9 pass (no echoes; Hymnal Keeper fires on give, and Testify's gift isn't re-triggered).
  - L10 pass (Sway and Fickle Convert resolve at round end).
- Risks:
  - The Sway total can reach 2 + 2 + 4 = 8 followers in a game. That's just above the 6–7 contest guide, though about 4–5 is typical, so watch it.
  - Pulpit and Sway lean toward catch-up. The leader still has Sermon, Testify, Great Awakening and the leader clause, but check parity (Q8) in mind-play.
