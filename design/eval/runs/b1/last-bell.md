## Amber: The Order of the Last Bell (Keeper)
Pitch: Strike first, or speak last
Verbs: hasten (place now), bide (place after everyone), echo (repeat your previous move)
Scoring: The Last Word. At round end, whoever placed the latest worker in this sphere this round takes 1 / 2 / 3 followers (rings I / II / III). Ties can't happen because placements are sequential.
Emits: echo; back-to-back placements and late placements (these shift the round's pace for every pace-reading effect)
Consumes: workers placed this round (anyone's); echo (rivals'); round start / round end

Glossary (for the rules panel, not the action text):
- **Wait**: the worker sits on your panel's Bell tray. After every player's last regular worker is placed, waiting workers are placed in snake order. They count as placements for every effect.
- **Skipping a turn**: the snake order is unchanged, and your seat simply passes on the turn your worker was already used for.

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | Hasten | +2 sand. You may place your next worker immediately instead of on its turn. |
| I | Bide | +2 sand. Your next worker may wait, placing after everyone's last worker. |
| I | Dusk | +1 sand, plus 1 per 3 workers already placed this round. |
| II | Dawn | +2 sand, plus 1 per worker you have yet to place. |
| II | Recur | Echo your previous placement this round (not an echo); otherwise +3 sand. |
| III | Midnight | +2 sand, +1 per worker you hold; place them all now, back to back. |

| Member | Cost | Effect (≤14 words) | Extra followers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early Riser | 2 | At each round start, gain 1 sand. | 0 |
| Bell-Ringer | 3 | Dusk counts every 2 workers placed, not every 3. | 0 |
| Chronicler | 3 | When a rival echoes, gain 1 sand. | 0 |
| Sleeper | 4 | Once per round, a worker of yours may wait until the end; +1 sand. | 0 |
| Keeper of Hours | 5 | At round end, gain 1 sand per worker placed after your last one. | 0 |
| The Undying Hour | 7 | Each round, place one extra worker last of all, if a space is open. | +2 |

Notes:
- **Feel.** It's a sphere about when, not what. The snake draft stays intact: Hasten and Midnight pull your turns earlier and leave gaps, and Bide adds a final waiting phase. Nobody loses a turn they didn't choose to give up.
- **Core tension.** Dawn, Hasten and Midnight reward going early (grab contested spaces and dash). Dusk, Bide and the Last Word reward going late (read the table, have the final say). Midnight dumps all your workers early, which forfeits the Last Word. Keeper of Hours pays you to finish early, while the scoring rule pays you to finish late.
- **No round-I tolls.**
  - Dusk is a lure for the forced last picker in round I (it pays up to 4).
  - Waiting is optional, since in round I it only gets leftovers.
  - Hasten on your last worker is just +2.
- **Combos.**
  - Hasten into a strong space, then Recur to echo it.
  - Recur after any round-III center from another sphere: a big burst. **FLAG:** check this in mind-play.
  - Bide or Sleeper + a sphere that pays for later placements, or reads marks left during the round.
  - Midnight floods spaces early, which feeds any rival's "per later worker" or "marked space" effects. That's spill through the board.
- **Lint self-check:**
  - L1 pass: longest effect is 14 words.
  - L2 pass: only workers, spaces, rivals and echo are named.
  - L3 pass.
  - L4 pass: no taking.
  - L5 warn: needs a Bell tray for waiting workers and a round-placement counter on the wheel. Both are simple.
  - L6 pass.
  - L7 pass.
  - L8 pass: every space has a floor (Recur gives +3 if there's nothing to echo; Midnight gives at least +2).
  - L9 pass: Recur can't echo an echo, and echoing Midnight is impossible because you have no workers left.
  - L10 pass: the Last Word is resolved at round end; Keeper of Hours pays resources at round end.
- **Value bands** (round I / II / III):
  - Hasten: 3 (2 + 1u of tempo).
  - Bide: 2 / 3.5 / 3.5. Round I sits at the band floor on purpose, since waiting is weak while every space fills.
  - Dusk: about 2.8 / 3.5 / 4.2. It climbs because more workers are placed each round.
  - Dawn (round II): about 3.5, and about 4 in round III.
  - Recur: about 3.5–4, or about 7 when it echoes a center. **FLAG**, but it fits the round-III burst intent.
  - Midnight (round III): taken early, 6 sand + about 4u of tempo = about 10, slightly over the band (**FLAG**); taken mid-round about 6; taken late about 2–3. Taking it early is the read.
- **Contest budget:** at most 1 + 2 + 3 = 6 followers, which is within budget.
- **Member pricing** (follower 1.5u + 50–70% of the ability's EV):
  - Early Riser: EV about 1.5 against cost 2.
  - Bell-Ringer: 2–3 uses × about 1.2, so EV about 3, against cost 3.
  - Chronicler: about 3–4 rival echoes, so EV about 3.5, against cost 3. It only fires if some sphere on the wheel echoes; this sphere's own Recur guarantees one emitter.
  - Sleeper: (1.5 + 1) × 2 rounds, so EV about 5, against cost 4.
  - Keeper of Hours: about 3 per round × 2, so EV about 6, against cost 5.
  - The Undying Hour: extra leftover placements about 6u, plus 3u for the 2 followers, so EV about 10.5 against cost 7 (67%). In round I every space fills, so its extra worker usually has no space, which the "if a space is open" clause covers.
- **Silo index:** about 0.45. Dusk, Dawn and the Last Word all read the table's pace.
- **Risks:**
  - Last Word kingmaking: the seat that naturally places last (snake end) is favored. Bide and Sleeper let anyone contest it, but mind-play should check seat bias.
  - Several waiting workers resolving at the end could slow the finish. The UI should auto-prompt them in order.
