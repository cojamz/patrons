## Violet: The Altar (Hierophant)
Pitch: Lay it down, reap at dusk.
Verbs: offer (move your own resources or a member onto your altar), consecrate (put incense straight onto an altar)
Scoring: **The Rite**: at round end, if your altar holds 4 / 6 / 8 or more, +1 / +2 / +3 followers. Threshold, non-exclusive (everyone who clears it scores). Game max 6.
Emits: gain (incense; altar returns at round end), give (Procession puts incense on every rival's altar), buy a fixture (Great Rite, at round end), shared stake (every altar is public, and every player can clear the Rite)
Consumes: rivals buying fixtures (Cantor), resources you can't spend yet (leftovers become altar value), members as tokens (any member counts 2 on the altar)

**Altar rules** (shown on each player panel when this sphere is on the wheel):
- Altar value = resources on it, plus 2 per member.
- Altar contents still count as yours for any "hold" check, but can't be spent and members' abilities sleep.
- Offered members can't be released or swayed.
- At round end: check the Rite → resolve Great Rite purchases → everything returns to you.

Notes:
- **Core tension.** The altar is a locked savings box. Anything on it is still yours (it comes back at round end, plus whatever incense was consecrated onto it), but you can't spend it this round. So every offer trades this round's buying power for the Rite's followers. It's the anti-Pyre: it sacrifices *tempo*, not members. Members offered come back. The sacrifice is only that their abilities sleep for the round.
- **Shape:** a personal threshold that anyone can clear. It's the opposite of a majority contest, readable at a glance (one number on each panel vs. the ring's printed 4/6/8), and never zero-sum.
- **Round-III twist:** resources are worthless after the final round, so late in round III players naturally pour leftovers onto altars to clear 8. Great Rite is the only way to turn a locked altar into fixtures at round end. That's the burst, and the race for that single space is the round-III drama.
- **Procession** is the warm space. It gives every rival real incense (it returns to them) and a nudge toward their own Rite.
- **Cantor** watches the fixture race. It links to any sphere that pushes fixture buying, and to the Diadem clock.
- **Combos (generic):**
  - Cheap members (Novice, any 1–2 cost member) are altar tokens worth 2 each.
  - A sphere that rewards holding the most of a color loves the altar, because offered resources still count as held but can't be spent away.
  - A release-heavy player and an altar-heavy player both want cheap members, which puts them in tension over stalls.
  - Thurifer + Acolyte + Sanctify is the incense engine (Sanctify caps at +5; Thurifer adds its +1 once).
- **Risks:**
  - (1) Grand Hierophant makes the Rite worth up to 3/4/5 for one player. Watch the totals.
  - (2) Procession prompts no one (automatic), but Oracle's round-start offer adds a decision at round start for its owner only.
  - (3) If the rest of the wheel is thin on fixtures early, Cantor is slow.

Value pass (u):
- **Censer:** 2 early, 4 with altar ≥ 4 (+33% late; scales by design). **Offer:** 1.2 + Rite progress ≈ 2.5–3. **Procession:** 3 (returns) + progress; spill to rivals 2.7, ratio 0.9.
- **Vespers:** 3–4 (altar 4–6). **Sanctify:** 2 with an empty altar; 5–6 with 2–4 incense offered.
- **Great Rite:** 3 + 1–2 discount + 1–2 extra round-end buys + rescues locked leftovers ≈ 7–8.
