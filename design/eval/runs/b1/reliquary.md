## Ivory: The Reliquary (curator)
Pitch: Covet, unveil, and anoint members.
Verbs: reserve (set a stall member aside for yourself), refresh (redeal a stall), recruit (extra and discounted buys); also uses the generic verb release
Scoring: The Fold. At round end, whoever recruited the most members this round takes 1 / 2 / 3 followers. Ties all take. Reserving doesn't count; recruiting from the reserve does.
Emits: buy an item (lots of it, often 2–3 per turn late), buys from any stall, refresh (new stall members for everyone), release (members return to their decks), gain (Tithe)
Consumes: rivals' expensive recruits (Watcher), member count (Tithe, Hierophant, Zealot), its own recruit tally (Prophet), release (Relinquish)

Glossary: **Reserve**: move a stall member onto your panel; at most 2 at a time; the stall refills at once; you alone may recruit it with any later buy. **Refresh**: put a stall's members under its deck and deal 2 new ones. **Release**: discard your member to the bottom of its deck; you lose its follower.

Notes:
- **Arc.** Round I: Covet the member you want and Unveil stalls to find cheap ones; Tithe pays more as your following grows. Round II: Induction gives you two recruits in one turn, and Relinquish turns old members back into cash (plus another shot at the Fold). Round III: Anoint is a free member, possibly a 7. Prophet and Hierophant turn the round-III shopping spree into the burst. This sphere makes a table explosive (S7): a high score scale.
- **The market is the board here.** Covet denies a member to everyone else, Unveil reshuffles what everyone can buy, and Watcher makes you root for rivals' big buys. Released members go back to the bottom of their deck, so they come round again.
- **Combos.** Covet early, then Almoner, then Anoint pulls the reserved 7-cost for free. Zealot + Tithe/Hierophant (and any other sphere's per-member scaling). Steward + Induction: cheap double recruits that win the Fold. Relinquish an Acolyte-style member whose release ability fires too.
- **Value pass** (u):
  - Tithe 2 early, 3–5 late (capped).
  - Covet 1 + about 1.5 (flex and denial), so 2.5.
  - Unveil 1 + 0.5 info + 1 any-stall, so 2.5.
  - Induction 1 discount + 1 flex + about 1.5–2 extra-buy equity, so 3.5–4.
  - Relinquish 3 flat, or cost + 2 − 1.5 (a 4-cost gives 4.5).
  - Anoint: average member about 4u + flex + Fold equity, so 6–8.
  - All within their ring bands.
  - Members (price ≈ 1.5 + 0.6 × ability EV): Zealot about 1, ≈ 2.1, so 1 is fodder; Almoner 1.5, ≈ 2.4; Watcher about 3 triggers, ≈ 3.3; Steward about 4.5, ≈ 4.2; Hierophant 3–4 followers for an ivory player (about 5u), ≈ 4.5–5; Prophet about 4 followers + 1 extra = 7.5u, ≈ 6.0 (73%, the upper edge).
  - Silo index about 0.5 (stalls are shared). Contest total 6 followers.
- **Lints.** L1 pass: the longest is 14 words. L2 pass. L3 pass. L4 pass. L5 warn: the reserve needs 2 panel slots and the per-round recruit tally needs a counter. L6 pass. L7 pass. L8 warn: Induction gives nothing extra if you can't afford a member. L9 pass: no repeat chains; Steward and Almoner have floors; Anoint is a single recruit. L10 pass: Fold, Hierophant and Prophet pay at round end.
- **Risks.**
  - In round III, Prophet + Anoint + Induction can bank 5+ followers in one round. That's intended as the burst, but the red team should price the ceiling.
  - The recruit-then-Relinquish loop pumps the Fold tally; it's bounded by placements and costs a follower each time.
  - Covet can hate-draft a rival's needed member; there's a cap of 2 reserved.
