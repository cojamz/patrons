## Ivory: The Vigil (Zealot)
Pitch: Seal the faithful; they stay.
Verbs: pray (gain faith), consecrate (make a member loyal), offer (give to a rival for faith)
Scoring: VIGIL. At round end, +1 follower per loyal member you have, up to a cap of I = 1 / II = 2 / III = 4. (Shape: a personal capped holding count that everyone can score. No contest.)
Loyal (a seal on the member card): a loyal member can't be taken or released, and it counts for Vigil.
Emits: gain (faith; Ascension and Communion can be gains of 3 or more), give (Offering; Almsgiver's return gift), a release (Doubter), protection against take (loyal members), spill (Communion)
Consumes: take (Penitent; loyalty blocks it), give (Almsgiver), member count (Ascension), ahead (Penitent fires only on harm from a player ahead)

|---|---|---|

| II | Communion | Every player gains 1 faith per loyal member they have; you gain 2 more. |
| III | Ascension | +1 faith per member you have, then make 2 members loyal free. |

Notes:
- Identity: the patient zealot. While others race for zeal and coin, Ivory turns members into a sealed flock that pays a follower every round and can't be swayed away. Loyalty is the counter to any sphere that takes members or resources, but it's expressed generically: "can't be taken."
- The trade-off in loyalty: a loyal member can't be released either, so loyalty fights any release-for-resources hook on the wheel. Sealing is a commitment, not a free upgrade.
- Arc: round I seals 0–1 members (2 faith each). Round II's Sanctuary and Communion grow the flock. Ascension seals 2 more in round III, just as the Vigil cap jumps to 4: the burst. A Vigil player can reach about 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 followers, but only by recruiting broadly first, which feeds the main loop (buy lots of members).
- Combos:
  - Offering + Almsgiver (+ any "when you give" member in the pool): one gift sets off a small chain of thanks that ends, because gains aren't gives.
  - Saint + Fickle-style members: protection keeps a cheap risky member at home.
  - Penitent rewards the leader for being picked on, which softens feel-bad.
  - Communion is a warm spill that also pays rivals who dabbled in loyalty.
- Value pass (u): Prayer 2 early to 4 late; Consecrate about 2.5–3 (2 faith, or a 2u loyalty seal worth about 1–2 followers over the game plus protection); Offering 2–3, with 1 resource handed to a rival (spill 0.25); Sanctuary about 4–5; Communion about 4 for you at 2 loyal, about 1–3 spilled (ratio about 0.5–0.75); Ascension about 5 faith from members plus 2 seals (about 3u) = about 8, inside the band.
- Member pricing (EV if bought at the start of round II, then cost as a share of EV): Doubter about 1.5–2u (50–67%); Bell-Ringer 1.5 + about 2.5 = 4 (50%); Penitent 1.5 + about 4 = 5.5 (55%, situational, best for whoever is ahead); Almsgiver 1.5 + about 4 triggers × 1.25 = 6.5 (62%); Abbess 3 + about 4 saved = 7 (71%); Saint 6 + loyal Vigil about 1.5 + protection about 1 = 8.5 (82%, a capstone trailing the Diadem on purpose).
- Self-lint:
  - L1 pass (Sanctuary and Communion are both 14).
  - L2 pass ("loyal" is defined on this sphere; other spheres need only the generic "take" and "release").
  - L3 pass (every round-I cost is optional: Consecrate's payment, Offering's gift).
  - L4 pass (no harm emitted).
  - L5 pass (loyalty is a seal icon on the member card; the Vigil cap is printed per ring).
  - L6 pass (Vigil is the only source of followers).
  - L7 pass (3/2/1 spaces, 6 members).
  - L8 warn → pass (Consecrate with no members still gives 2 faith; Sanctuary has its fallback).
  - L9 pass (Almsgiver's return is a gain, not a give, so it can't loop).
  - L10 pass (Vigil pays at round end; Doubter's release removes a follower, tallied at round end).
- Risks:
  - Silo index may sit near 0.6, since loyalty compounds within the sphere. Penitent, Almsgiver, Offering and Communion are the table hooks; check in the interplay graph.
  - Communion's spill can exceed 1 if rivals hold many loyal members. Cap it or keep it as a feature if mind-play shows it's rare.
