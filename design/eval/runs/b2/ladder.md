## Crimson: Ladder
*Was: revival (b1). Fix.*

Pitch: Climb past the player just ahead of you.
Verbs: gain (more from behind or in a crowd), give (to whoever trails), take (from the leader only).

Scoring — OVERTAKE (paid at round end): every non-leader who placed here this round, or holds a Ladder member, compares crimson with the player directly ahead on the follower track. Hold more: gain I 1 / II 2 / III 3 followers. Ties fail. The leader only defends. Followers come from the supply; nobody loses any.
Ladder = followers at round start; ties broken by pick order (earlier pick counts as behind).

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | **Rally** | +2 crimson, +1 per rival already here (max +2). |
| I | **Boost** | +4 crimson. The rival furthest behind gains 1 crimson. |
| I | **Slipstream** | +2 crimson, +1 per player ahead of you (max +2). |
| II | **Overtake** | +2 crimson. Take up to 2 crimson from the leader. |
| II | **Surge** | +3 crimson, +1 of any color per player ahead of you. |
| III | **Breakaway** | +4 crimson, +1 per member you have (max +5). |

Notes:
- Identity: the underdog. The only sphere whose scoring points up the table; it is the game's built-in catch-up.
- Fixed: Whisper (take from the player directly ahead) is gone. Taking now points only at the leader (Overtake), per principles.
- Cut: Hymnal Keeper's give-tracking, Hierophant's +3 (now Ringer +2), most of the glossary. Crimson held at round end is read plain; defender modifiers don't exist.
- UI must show each player's "you vs X: A vs B crimson, worth N" readout. Without it, cut the sphere.
- Watch: hoarding crimson to win the challenge vs spending it on items. That tension is the point; if it's a non-decision, a winning challenger pays 1 crimson.
