## The Bookie (was Veiled Eye)
Pitch: Bet on where rivals will go.
Verbs: tip (put your tip marker on an empty open space), read (gain per marked space)
Resource: chips.
Tip rules: when a rival places there, the tip pays off: they gain 2 chips, you gain 1, and it flips face-up. Paid tips clear at round end; unpaid tips stay. Your own worker on your tip removes it unpaid.

Scoring — **Payout**: at round end, +1 follower per tip of yours a rival took this round. Max 1 / 2 / 3 in rounds I / II / III.

| Ring | Action | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| I | Tip | Tip an empty open space; +2 chips. |
| I | Odds | +1 chip per marked space on the wheel, minimum 2, maximum 5. |
| I | Hedge | +2 chips, then move one of your tips. |
| II | Spread | Tip two spaces; +1 chip per tip of yours on the wheel (max 4). |
| II | Long Shot | Tip a space in an inner ring; +3 chips. |
| III | Big Book | Tip three spaces. This round, each paid tip also gives you 2 any. |

| Member | Cost | Effect | Extra followers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Runner | 2 | Round start: gain 1 chip. | 0 |
| Tout | 3 | When you place a tip, gain 1 chip. | 0 |
| Watcher | 4 | When a rival takes any marked space, gain 1 chip (max 3 per round). | 0 |
| Fixer | 4 | Once per round, move a tip of yours after a rival places. | 0 |
| Syndicate | 5 | Payout cap is 1 higher for you. | +1 |
| Kingpin | 7 | Round start: tip an empty open space. | +2 |

Notes:
- Round I: every space fills, so tips are lures and the 1-follower cap is nearly free. Rounds II–III: spaces go empty, so a tip is a real read.
- The taker always gains more chips than you do from a payoff; a tip is a gift with a hook, not a trap.
- Long Shot points at inner rings that open later, a bet on the future.
- Cut from b1: Portent (name-a-color, game-end payout problem), round-I zero scoring, Twin Sight stacking.
- Color: b1 Altar and Veiled Eye were both Violet; assign at wheel build.
