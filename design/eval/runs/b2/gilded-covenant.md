## Gold: The Vault
Pitch: Gold is the score you're always tempted to spend.
Verbs: gain, sponsor (mark a space), trade
Scoring: Hoard. At round end, whoever holds the most gold gains I 1 / II 2 / III 3 followers. Ties: every tied player scores.

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | Mint | +2 gold, +1 per round number. |
| I | Sponsor | +2 gold. Mark an empty space; its next taker and you each gain 1 gold. |
| I | Exchange | Trade up to 3 resources for gold one-for-one, then +1 gold. |
| II | Interest | +1 gold per 3 gold you hold (min 2, max 4). |
| II | Covet | +3 gold, +1 per rival holding more gold than you. |
| III | Jackpot | Double your gold (max +8). |

| Member | Cost | Effect (≤14 words) | Extra followers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coin Purse | 1 | At each round start, +1 gold. | 0 |
| Piggy Bank | 2 | On your turn, break it for 3 gold. It leaves the game. | 0 |
| Moneychanger | 3 | You may pay for members and fixtures with gold as any color. | 0 |
| Collector | 4 | Whenever a rival recruits a member, +1 gold (max 2 per round). | 0 |
| Banker | 5 | At each round start, +1 gold per member you have (max 4). | +1 |
| Tycoon | 7 | At game end, +1 follower per 3 gold you hold (max 3). | +1 |

Notes:
- The whole sphere is one tension: gold is your score at round end and your best currency all round. Every buy lowers your Hoard. Moneychanger and Tycoon sharpen both sides of it.
- Interplay is generic: Sponsor lures rivals onto spaces, Covet and Collector feed off rivals' gold and buys, and the Hoard is a public race everyone can see.
- Changes from b1: cut the tie-break-by-fewest-followers rule, all "+1 per 2" counters, and per-action caps that needed trackers. Jackpot is the round-III burst: a hoarder doubles, a spender still gets something real.
- Watch: Jackpot plus Tycoon on a pure hoarder. The +8 cap and the Tycoon cap are the brakes.
