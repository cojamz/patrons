## The Kiln (was Pyre)
Pitch: Buy cheap, burn bright.
Verbs: burn (send one of your members back to its stall deck), stoke (gain heat)
Resource: heat.
Base rule: a burned member's follower stays with you as a cinder. Its ability ends.

Scoring — **Ashes**: at round end, +1 follower per member you burned this round (from any source). Max 1 / 2 / 3 in rounds I / II / III.

| Ring | Action | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| I | Stoke | +1 heat per member you have, minimum 2. |
| I | Scrap | Burn a member for 2 any colors and 1 heat, or gain 2 heat. |
| I | Bellows | +2 heat, plus 1 per member burned this round by anyone (max +3). |
| II | Bonfire | Burn up to 2 members for 2 any colors each, or gain 3 heat. |
| II | Recast | Burn a member: take a stall member costing up to 2 more, free. |
| III | Inferno | Burn any number of members for 3 any colors each. |

| Member | Cost | Effect | Extra followers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kindling | 1 | When burned, gain 1 heat. | 0 |
| Tinker | 2 | When you buy a member costing 2 or less, gain 1 heat. | 0 |
| Scavenger | 3 | Whenever a rival burns a member, gain 1 heat. | 0 |
| Smelter | 4 | Your burns pay 1 extra any color. | 0 |
| Ashwalker | 5 | Ashes cap is 1 higher for you. | +1 |
| Firebrand | 7 | When you burn another member, gain 2 heat; the next player gains 1. | 0 |

Notes:
- The loop: buy cheap members anywhere, burn them for a burst and a follower. Stoke pulls the other way (hold them). That is the whole tension.
- Recast: the free member is your buy this placement, from this quadrant's stall.
- Inferno is uncapped on resources; Ashes still caps followers at 3 (4 with Ashwalker). It's the R3 explosion.
- Cut from b1: Ferryman (cost-as-payment), Saint (permanent tokens), the net-zero refund math.
