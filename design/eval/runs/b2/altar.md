## The Vault (was Altar)
Pitch: Lock it up, cash it out.
Verbs: deposit (move your own resources into your vault), accrue (put coin from the supply into your vault)
Resource: coin.
Vault rules: vault value = resources in it. Vaulted resources can't be spent this round. At round end, after scoring, the vault empties back to you.

Scoring — **Interest**: at round end, if your vault holds at least 4 / 6 / 8 (round I / II / III), gain 1 / 2 / 3 followers.

| Ring | Action | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| I | Deposit | Deposit up to 3 resources; gain 1 any color. |
| I | Accrue | Put 3 coin in your vault; each rival gains 1 coin. |
| I | Dividend | +2 coin; +2 more if your vault already meets this round's line. |
| II | Compound | Put coin in your vault equal to half its value, minimum 2. |
| II | Payday | +3 coin; +2 more if your vault already meets this round's line. |
| III | Windfall | Double your vault. Round end: it may buy one fixture, 1 cheaper. |

| Member | Cost | Effect | Extra followers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clerk | 2 | When you deposit, gain 1 coin. | 0 |
| Pension | 3 | Round start: put 2 coin in your vault. | 0 |
| Broker | 3 | When a rival buys a fixture, put 1 coin in your vault. | 0 |
| Auditor | 4 | Your vault line is 1 lower. | 0 |
| Underwriter | 5 | Round start: deposit up to 3 resources, gaining 1 coin each. | 0 |
| Magnate | 7 | Each round end you meet the line, gain 1 more follower. | +2 |

Notes:
- The tension: every deposit trades this round's buying for the round-end line. Nothing is lost, only delayed.
- Clearing early flips Dividend/Payday into income, so the rest of your round isn't dead.
- Accrue's gift goes to rival hands, never rival vaults (no kingmaking).
- Windfall: doubling is the R3 burst; the fixture buy uses vault resources only and cashes what would otherwise expire.
- Cut from b1: members on the altar, sleeping abilities, the "2 per member" count, Thurifer's post-cap add.
