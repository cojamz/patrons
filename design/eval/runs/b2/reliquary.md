## Ivory: The Auction House (market)
Pitch: Work the market. Buy more than anyone.
Verbs: reserve (hold a stall member for yourself), redeal (refresh a stall), bargain (extra, cheaper buys)
Scoring: The Haul. At round end, whoever bought the most members this round gains 1 / 2 / 3 Favor (printed on rings I / II / III). Ties: each tied player gains 1 less. Reserving doesn't count; buying from your reserve does.

Rules panel:
- **Reserve**: move a stall member to your one reserve slot; the stall refills at once. Only you can buy it, on any later buy. Reserving again sends the old one to the bottom of its deck.
- **Redeal**: put a stall's members under its deck and deal 2 new ones.

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | Appraise | +1 ivory per member you own (at least 2, at most 5). |
| I | Reserve | +1 ivory. Reserve a member from any stall. |
| I | Redeal | Redeal any stall. Everyone gains 1 ivory; your buy may come from it. |
| II | Bargain | Buy an extra member from any stall at 1 less, or gain 2 ivory. |
| II | Connoisseur | +1 ivory per color among your members. |
| III | Windfall | Free buy: any member costing up to 6, from any stall or your reserve. |

| Member | Cost | Effect (≤14 words) | Extra Favor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apprentice | 1 | Counts as two members for Appraise. | 0 |
| Broker | 2 | Buying from your reserve costs 2 less (minimum 1). | 0 |
| Rival Bidder | 3 | When a rival buys a member costing 5 or more, gain 1 ivory. | 0 |
| Haggler | 4 | Your members cost 1 less (minimum 1). | 0 |
| Curator | 5 | Game end: +1 Favor per two members you own. | 0 |
| Second Look | 7 | Once per round, after a buy, make one more buy from that stall. | +1 |

Notes:
- **Why it works.** It sharpens the core loop instead of adding a side game: more buys, cheaper buys, better buys. The Haul makes every rival's purchase a race you can see. Windfall plus a reserved 6-cost is the round-III burst; Bargain + Second Look + Windfall can make three buys in one round.
- **Cut from b1:** Relinquish and the release rule, reserve expiry timers and the 2-slot swap, Seer (a second Haul on the same axis), paying for reserves in ivory.
- **Interplay:** Redeal spills 1 ivory to everyone. Reserve denies one member, briefly and openly. Rival Bidder makes you root for others' big buys.
- **Watch:** Haggler + Bargain + Second Look is the strongest stack in the set; make sure the Haul doesn't snowball to one player every round. If it does, cap Bargain at a member costing up to 4.
