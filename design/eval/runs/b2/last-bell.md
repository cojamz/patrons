## Amber: The Night Shift (tempo)
Pitch: It's not what you take. It's when.
Verbs: hasten (place your next worker now), hold (place your next worker after everyone), echo (repeat a gain)
Scoring: Last Word. At round end, whoever placed the last worker in Amber this round gains 1 / 2 / 3 Favor (printed on rings I / II / III). A Lamp token sits on the most recent Amber worker so the table can see who holds it.

Rules panel:
- **Place now**: your next worker goes immediately. Your skipped snake turn passes; the snake order is unchanged.
- **Hold**: that worker sits in the Night tray. After all other workers are placed, held workers place one at a time, highest Favor first (so the trailer speaks last). A held worker needs an open space and gets its normal buy.
- **Echo**: copy the resource gains of a placement, nothing else. Not a placement; can't be echoed.

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | Hasten | +2 amber. Place your next worker now. |
| I | Last Call | +2 amber. Hold your next worker until everyone else has placed. |
| I | Dusk | +1 amber, plus 1 per 3 workers already placed this round. |
| II | Dawn | +1 amber, plus 1 per worker you have yet to place. |
| II | Encore | Echo your previous placement this round (not a center). Else +3 amber. |
| III | Midnight | +2 amber per worker you have yet to place. Place your next now. |

| Member | Cost | Effect (≤14 words) | Extra Favor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lookout | 1 | When a rival places now or holds, gain 1 amber. | 0 |
| Early Bird | 2 | Your first placement each round gains 1 more of its color. | 0 |
| Night Owl | 3 | Your last placement each round gains 2 more of its color. | 0 |
| Metronome | 4 | Your Hasten and Last Call each give 2 more amber. | 0 |
| Insomniac | 5 | Once per round, you may hold any one of your workers. | 0 |
| Overtime | 7 | Each round, place one extra worker, held, outside Amber. | +1 |

Notes:
- **Why it works.** One axis, two directions: early pays (Hasten, Dawn, Midnight, Early Bird), late pays (Last Call, Dusk, Last Word, Night Owl). Everyone can contest Last Word because anyone can end the round in Amber.
- **Cut from b1:** joining occupied spaces, the joined-occupant sand, Keeper of Hours (counted rivals' unplaced workers), Chronicler's "out of turn" catch-all, last-worker exceptions. Every round-I space still fills; a held worker just takes whatever is left.
- **Burst:** Midnight on your first round-III pick with 4 workers left = 8 amber plus tempo.
- **Interplay:** Lookout watches the table. Hold order is highest Favor first, so the trailer gets the final word.
- **Watch:** round-I Last Call is a gamble (you get the leftover space). That's the fun, but confirm it never reads as a toll.
