## Shadow: The Gatecrashers
Pitch: Every space is open if you don't mind company.
Verbs: share (place on an occupied space), take (from the leader only)
Scoring: Crowds. At round end, +1 follower per space you shared this round, max I 1 / II 2 / III 3.

Sharing: place your worker on a space a rival already occupies and resolve it normally. You still buy from the stall of the quadrant you played. A space holds at most two workers.

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | Slip In | +2 shadow. Your next worker this round may share an occupied space. |
| I | Lurk | +2 shadow, +1 per player ahead of you (max +2). |
| I | Lift | +2 shadow. Take 1 resource from the leader (no single leader: +1 shadow). |
| II | Gatecrash | Share any occupied ring I–II space now and resolve it. +1 shadow. |
| II | Smuggle | +3 shadow. This turn, buy from any stall instead of this one. |
| III | Heist | Share up to two occupied spaces now, resolving both. +2 shadow. |

| Member | Cost | Effect (≤14 words) | Extra followers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lockpick | 1 | On your turn, discard it: your next worker may share an occupied space. | 0 |
| Lookout | 2 | Whenever a rival shares a space you occupy, +2 shadow. | 0 |
| Fence | 3 | Whenever you share a space, +1 resource of any color. | 0 |
| Pickpocket | 4 | Whenever you share a space with the leader, take 1 resource from them. | 0 |
| Smuggler | 5 | You may buy from any stall; your first buy each round costs 1 less. | +1 |
| Ringleader | 7 | Once per round, any of your workers may share an occupied space. | +1 |

Notes:
- Core: crowding onto spaces others already claimed. It turns "that space is gone" into "that space is contested", which is board interaction, not hitting people.
- Leader = most followers at round start, marked on the board all round. Every take points at the leader only and is 1 resource. No sways, no member theft.
- Changes from b1: cut the shadow threshold track and the "beat every leader's shadow" comparison (two hidden-feeling numbers to read), cut Defection's member sway, and stopped takes from anyone merely ahead. Shadow is now a plain spendable resource.
- Lookout makes being shared on a small gift, so getting crowded never feels bad.
- Watch: overlap with Green's echo. Sharing places a worker on a live space now; echo copies a space's effect from history without placing. Keep both only if they feel different at the table.
