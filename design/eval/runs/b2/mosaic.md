## Gold: Mosaic
*Was: radiant-choir (b1). Fix.*

Pitch: Complete the spectrum. Hold one of every color, as many times over as you can.
Verbs: gain (mixed colors), convert, give.

Scoring — SPECTRUM (paid at round end): whoever holds the most full sets (at least 1) gains I 1 / II 2 / III 3 followers. Ties all score. A full set is one resource of each color. Sets are counted, not spent.

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | **Bounty** | +2 gold, +1 per full set you hold. |
| I | **Wander** | +3 resources, each a different color. |
| I | **Alms** | +3 of one color. Each rival gains 1 gold. |
| II | **Exchange** | Swap up to 3 of your resources to other colors, then +2 any. |
| II | **Spectrum** | +1 of every color. |
| III | **Cascade** | +2 of every color, +1 more gold per full set you hold. |

| Member | Cost | Effect (≤14 words) | Extra followers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tinker | 1 | Once per round, convert 1 of your resources to any color. | 0 |
| Wayfarer | 2 | Wander gives 1 more resource, of a color not yet given. | 0 |
| Lookout | 3 | When a rival gains 4+ at once, +1 gold. Once per rival per round. | 0 |
| Patron | 4 | Whenever any action makes rivals gain, +1 gold. Once per action. | 0 |
| Keystone | 5 | Counts as one resource of any color for your full sets. | +1 |
| Masterwork | 7 | Final round end: +1 follower per full set you hold (max 3). | +2 |

Notes:
- Identity: the generalist. Everyone else goes deep in one color; Mosaic goes wide and wins by breadth.
- Fixed: gifts are now gold, not each rival's scarcest color, so your generosity no longer completes rivals' sets in the race you're running.
- Cut: Novice (round-start release), Witness/Missionary double-watchers merged into Lookout + Patron, Pilgrimage's per-2-members math.
- Watch: Keystone + Masterwork may run away for whoever grabs both; first thing to test in mind-play.
