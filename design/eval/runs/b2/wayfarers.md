## Saffron: The Caravan (spread)
Pitch: Be everywhere. Leave a mark.
Verbs: wander (yield per color you're present in), stake (a permanent outpost), share (enter an occupied space)
Scoring: Long Road. At round end, whoever has the most outposts gains 1 / 2 / 3 Favor (printed on rings I / II / III). Ties: each tied player gains 1 less.

Rules panel:
- **Outpost**: your marker on a quadrant's rim. One per quadrant per player. Stays all game.
- **Present**: you have a worker or an outpost in that quadrant.
- **Share**: place on a space a rival holds and resolve it too. Max 2 workers per space. The rival gains 1 of that space's color.

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | Wander | +1 saffron per quadrant where you're present (at least 2). |
| I | Stake | Stake an outpost in a quadrant lacking yours. If none, +3 saffron. |
| I | Fellowship | +2 saffron. Your next worker this round may share an occupied space. |
| II | Pitch Camp | +2 saffron. Stake an outpost where your previous worker this round stands. |
| II | Caravan | +1 of each color where you're present. |
| III | Journey's End | +2 of each color where you're present. |

| Member | Cost | Effect (≤14 words) | Extra Favor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scout | 1 | When you buy this, stake an outpost. | 0 |
| Guide | 2 | Once per round, share a space in a quadrant holding your outpost. | 0 |
| Innkeeper | 3 | Whenever anyone shares a space, you gain 1 saffron. | 0 |
| Pathfinder | 4 | When you place in a quadrant holding your outpost, +1 of its color. | 0 |
| Toll Road | 5 | When a rival places in a quadrant holding your outpost, +1 saffron (max 3/round). | 0 |
| Cartographer | 7 | Round end: +1 Favor per outpost you have beyond two. | 0 |

Notes:
- **Why it works.** Breadth is the engine: outposts are permanent presence, presence feeds Wander, Caravan and Journey's End, and the outpost count is the contest. Journey's End across 4 quadrants = 8 resources in 4 colors, the burst.
- **Cut from b1:** Shift (moving workers, locked spaces, adjacency), Acolyte's release, Shrine-keeper's ring-II restriction. Pitch Camp replaces Shift as the second outpost source and stays one line.
- **Interplay:** sharing always pays the host 1, and it's the only way into a rival's taken center. Toll Road and Innkeeper make you glad when rivals move through your ground. No harm anywhere.
- **Watch:** Cartographer and Long Road both count outposts. Fine as a capstone, but check one player can't lock round III by round II. Ties shaving 1 is the brake.
