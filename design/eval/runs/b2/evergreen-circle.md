## Green: The Encore
Pitch: The best moves are worth making twice.
Verbs: echo (copy a space's effect without placing)
Scoring: Encore. At round end, +1 follower per echo you made this round, max I 1 / II 2 / III 3.

Echo rule (printed on the board edge): an echo copies a space's effect only. You don't occupy it, you still buy from this stall, and it doesn't count for other quadrants' scoring. A space whose effect is an echo can't be echoed.

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | Tend | +2 green, +1 more per echo you've made this round. |
| I | Mirror | Echo a ring I–II space a rival took this round (none: +2 green). |
| I | Sprout | +1 green now, +3 green at the start of next round. |
| II | Reprise | Echo a ring I–II space you took last round. |
| II | Harmony | Echo a space; its last taker gains 1 resource of its color. |
| III | Bloom | Echo two different spaces anyone took this game, ring III excluded. |

| Member | Cost | Effect (≤14 words) | Extra followers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seedling | 1 | At each round start, +1 green. | 0 |
| Cutting | 2 | Your Sprouts return +2 more. | 0 |
| Mirror Twin | 3 | When you echo a rival's space, you gain 2 resources, they gain 1. | 0 |
| Gardener | 4 | Whenever you echo, also gain 1 resource of any color. | 0 |
| Timekeeper | 5 | Once per round, echo a ring I space right after you take it. | +1 |
| World Tree | 7 | Your echoes copy the space's effect twice, once per round. | +2 |

Notes:
- Core: echo. You win by copying the table's best plays and replaying your own. It reads the board every turn and stays strong as later rings open.
- Changes from b1: cut the Plant tray (a timer per player) and Graft (copying member abilities is a rules swamp). Sprout keeps the "plant now, harvest later" feel in one line. Scoring caps now climb 1/2/3 like every other quadrant.
- Ring III center can't be echoed, so the center contest stays real.
- Harmony and Mirror Twin spill a little to whoever you copy: being echoed is a compliment, not a loss.
- Watch: overlap with Shadow's sharing (see that spec), and whether the 1/2/3 Encore cap makes Green a lure every seat dips into. That's fine if Green spaces get contested.
