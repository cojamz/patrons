## Amber: The Night Shift
Pitch: It's not what you take. It's when.
Resource: **oil** (amber).
Verbs: **hasten** (place your next worker now), **hold** (place your next worker after everyone).

Rules panel:
- **Hasten**: your next worker places right away. You skip your next snake turn; the order is unchanged.
- **Hold**: that worker waits in the Night tray. Once all other workers are placed, held workers place one by one, most followers first, so the trailer goes last. It gets its normal buy.
- **No open space?** A held worker gains 3 oil instead and returns.

Scoring — **Night Watch**: at round end, whoever has the most workers in this sphere gains 1 / 2 / 3 followers (printed on rings I / II / III). Ties go to the tied player with the fewest followers; still tied, all of them score. A Lamp token sits with the current holder.

Notes:
- **Night Watch no longer feeds the leader.** b2's "last worker in Amber" went to whoever placed last in snake order, usually the leader. Now it's a majority over 6 spaces, and ties lean to whoever is behind.
- **Held workers never strand.** No open space means 3 oil, not a dead worker. Overtime's extra worker gets no fallback: no open space, no extra worker.
- **One axis, two directions.** Early pays (Hasten, Dawn, Double Shift, Midnight, Early Bird); late pays (Last Call, Dusk, Night Owl, Sleepwalker). Double Shift is the play for Night Watch.
- **Interplay.** Night Porter watches the table's tempo. Hold order puts the trailer last, so the player behind gets the last pick of what's left.
- **Burst.** Midnight on your first round-III pick with 4 workers left: 8 oil, then straight into another placement.
- Changed from b2: "echo" and the Encore action cut (Green owns both words), Last Word replaced by Night Watch, held-worker fallback, Overtime capped by open spaces, Lookout renamed (clashed), 8 members, amber now named oil.
