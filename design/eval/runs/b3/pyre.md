## Ember: The Kiln
*Was: Pyre (b2). Fix.*

Pitch: Buy cheap, burn bright.
Resource: **heat** (ember).
Verbs: burn (send one of your members to the bottom of its deck), stoke (gain heat).

Kiln rules:
- **Cinders:** a burned member's follower stays with you. Its ability ends.
- **Tinder:** the Kiln stall always offers Tinder beside its 2 members. Cost 1 wild, unlimited. A member worth 0 followers that exists only to be burned. Burned Tinder returns to the supply.
- **Fuel:** heat can pay for fixtures, each heat counting as any resource.

Scoring — **Ashes** (round end): +1 follower per member you burned this round. Max I 1 / II 2 / III 3.

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | **Stoke** | +1 heat per member you have, minimum 2. |
| I | **Scrap** | Burn a member for 2 any colors and 1 heat, or gain 2 heat. |
| I | **Bellows** | +2 heat, +1 per member burned this round by anyone (max +3). |
| II | **Bonfire** | Burn up to 2 members for 2 any colors each, or gain 3 heat. |
| II | **Recast** | Burn a member: take a stall member costing up to 2 more, free. |
| III | **Inferno** | Burn any number of members for 3 any colors each. |

| Member | Cost (specific + wild) | Effect (≤14 words) | Extra followers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kindling | 1 wild | When burned, gain 2 heat. | 0 |
| Rummager | 2 wild | When you buy a member costing 2 or less, gain 1 heat. | 0 |
| Stoker | 2 heat + 1 wild | Round start: +1 heat per cinder you hold (max 3). | 0 |
| Scavenger | 1 heat + 2 wild | When a rival burns a member, gain 1 heat. | 0 |
| Smelter | 3 heat + 1 wild | Your burns pay 1 extra any color. | 0 |
| Ashwalker | 3 heat + 2 wild | Your Ashes cap is 1 higher. | +1 |
| Glassblower | 4 heat + 2 wild | When you burn, gain 2 heat; the next player in turn order gains 1. | +1 |
| Firebrand | 5 heat + 2 wild | Round end: if you burned 2+ members this round, +1 follower. | +2 |

Notes:
- The loop: buy cheap members anywhere (Tinder here), burn them for a burst and a follower. Stoke pulls the other way (hold them). That's the whole tension.
- Fixed: round-I burn has fuel. Place anywhere, buy Tinder; your next Scrap burns it. Tinder is your one buy for that placement, so it's never free.
- Fixed: heat has an outlet beyond its own stall. Fuel turns a heat pile into Laurels, Garlands and Diadems, and makes heat a wild card for their mix rules.
- Recast: the free member is your buy this placement, from this sphere's stall (not Tinder).
- Inferno is uncapped on resources; Ashes still caps followers at 3 (4 with Ashwalker). It's the round-III explosion.
- Renamed Tinker to Rummager (clashed with Mosaic). Added Stoker (cinders matter) and Glassblower (spill); Firebrand now carries the late followers.
- Costs: "costing N" (Recast, Rummager) means the total, specific + wild.
- Watch: Fuel + a big Inferno racing the Diadem clock.
