## Teal: Mosaic
*Was: Mosaic (b2, gold). Fix.*

Pitch: Complete the spectrum. Hold one of every color, as many times over as you can.
Resource: **tiles** (teal).
Verbs: gain (mixed colors), swap, give.

Scoring — **Spectrum** (round end): whoever holds the most full sets (at least 1) gains I 1 / II 2 / III 3 followers. Ties all score. A full set is 1 of each resource on the table. Sets are counted, not spent.

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | **Bounty** | +2 tiles, +1 per full set you hold (max +2). |
| I | **Wander** | +3 resources, each a different color. |
| I | **Potluck** | +3 tiles. Each rival gains 1 of the resource they hold most. |
| II | **Swap Meet** | Swap up to 3 of your resources to other colors, then +2 tiles. |
| II | **Prism** | +1 of every color. |
| III | **Cascade** | +2 of every color, +1 tile per full set you hold. |

| Member | Cost (specific + wild) | Effect (≤14 words) | Extra followers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dabbler | 1 wild | Once per round, convert 1 of your resources to any color. | 0 |
| Rambler | 1 tile + 1 wild | Wander gives 1 more resource, of a color not yet given. | 0 |
| Quilter | 2 tiles | Round end: if you hold a full set, +2 tiles. | 0 |

| Go-Between | 2 tiles + 2 wild | Pay the specific part of any member's cost with any resources. | 0 |

| Masterwork | 5 tiles + 2 wild | Final round end: +1 follower per full set you hold (max 3). | +2 |

Notes:
- Identity: the friendly generalist. Everyone else goes deep; Mosaic goes wide and leaks a little to the table.
- Fixed: own resource (tiles), so it no longer shares gold with the Treasury.
- Fixed: Potluck gifts a rival's *most-held* resource. Adding to your biggest pile never raises your smallest, so a gift can never complete a set. Tied for most: the rival picks.
- Fixed: name clashes (Exchange, Tinker, Lookout, Wayfarer, Patron, Spectrum-as-action) renamed. Alms (too churchy) is now Potluck.
- Interplay: Host pays off any sphere's spill-to-rivals action; Go-Between and Patchworker turn breadth into buying power anywhere.
- Costs: totals run 1–7; "costing N" anywhere means the total.
- Watch: Keystone + Masterwork for whoever grabs both.
