## Crimson: Ladder
*Was: Ladder (b2). Fix.*

Pitch: Climb past the player just ahead of you.
Resource: **grit** (crimson).
Verbs: gain (more from behind or in a crowd), give (to whoever trails), take (from the leader only).

Scoring — **Overtake** (round end, before other payouts): each player who placed here this round and holds at least 1 grit challenges the player directly ahead (fewest followers among those ahead of you; tied, you choose). Hold more grit than them: gain I 1 / II 2 / III 3 followers. Ties fail. **Alone in last place:** you win ties and gain 1 extra follower. Nobody ahead of you, no challenge. Followers come from the supply; nobody loses any.

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | **Rally** | +2 grit, +1 per rival already here (max +2). |
| I | **Leg Up** | +4 grit. Each rival with the fewest followers gains 1 grit. |
| I | **Slipstream** | +2 grit, +1 per player ahead of you (max +2). |
| II | **Reel In** | +2 grit. Take 2 grit from the leader; any shortfall comes from supply. |
| II | **Surge** | +3 grit, +1 of any color per player ahead of you. |
| III | **Breakaway** | +5 grit. This round you may challenge the leader instead, for +2 followers. |

| Member | Cost (specific + wild) | Effect (≤14 words) | Extra followers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Understudy | 1 grit | Round end: if your challenge fails, +2 grit. | 0 |
| Photo Finish | 1 grit + 1 wild | You win ties when you challenge. | 0 |

| Spotter | 2 grit + 1 wild | When a rival gains 4+ at once, +1 grit. Once per rival per round. | 0 |
| Pacer | 3 grit + 1 wild | Your first placement here each round: +2 grit; the player furthest behind gains 1. | 0 |
| Ringer | 3 grit + 2 wild | Count 2 extra grit when you challenge. | +1 |
| Second Wind | 4 grit + 2 wild | If your challenge wins, challenge the next player ahead too. | +1 |
| Headliner | 5 grit + 2 wild | Round end: if your challenge wins, +1 more follower. | +2 |

Notes:
- Identity: the underdog. The only sphere whose scoring points up the table; it's the game's built-in catch-up.
- Fixed: a challenge needs at least 1 grit (no free challenges from empty hands).
- Fixed: "ahead" and "leader" now follow core rules (strictly more followers; tied at the top = no leader). The old pick-order tiebreak is gone.
- Fixed: Reel In never fizzles. No leader, a broke leader, or you are the leader: the missing grit comes from supply.
- Fixed: last place gets real catch-up: ties win plus a bonus follower, on top of Leg Up, Late Bloomer and Pacer feeding them grit.
- Breakaway: the "+2" stacks on the ring payout (III 3 + 2 = 5). If you're the leader there's no one to challenge; the grit still counts for buys.
- Late Bloomer costs only wild, so the trailing player can grab it with whatever they have.
- UI must show each player's "you vs X: A vs B grit, worth N" readout. Without it, cut the sphere.
- Watch: hoard grit to win vs spend it on members. If that's a non-decision, a winning challenger pays 1 grit.
