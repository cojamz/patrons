## Teal: The Bazaar (market)
*New in b3. A fresh market attempt that learns from the Auction House's death (see graveyard).*

Pitch: Spend big, recruit everywhere.
Verbs: browse (buy from any stall), haggle (pay less), trade in (upgrade a member).
Resource: **scrip** (teal).
Sphere rule: **scrip pays the specific part of any member's cost**, whichever sphere it comes from. Scrip is the market's money.

Scoring: **Ledger** (paid at round end). Whoever bought the highest total printed cost in members this round (at least 1 member) gains I 1 / II 2 / III 3 followers. Ties all score. Discounts don't lower your total. A trade-in counts the new member's full cost.

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | **Haggle** | +2 scrip. Your buy this turn costs 1 less. |
| I | **Browse** | +2 scrip. Your buy this turn may come from any stall. |
| I | **Commission** | +1 scrip per member anyone bought this round (min 2, max 5). |
| II | **Trade-In** | +2 scrip. Return a member you own; its cost counts toward this buy. |
| II | **Bulk Order** | +2 scrip. This turn, make two buys, from any stalls. |
| III | **Spree** | +4 scrip. This turn, make up to three buys, from any stalls. |

| Member | Cost | Effect (≤14 words) | Extra followers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Porter | 1 wild | Round start: +1 scrip. | 0 |
| Shopkeep | 1 scrip + 1 wild | Your first buy each round costs 1 less. | 0 |
| Peddler | 2 scrip + 1 wild | When a rival buys a member, +1 scrip (max 2 per round). | 0 |
| Hawker | 2 scrip + 1 wild | After you buy a member costing 3 or less, +1 scrip. | 0 |
| Merchant | 2 scrip + 2 wild | Once per round, one buy may come from any stall. | 0 |
| Quartermaster | 3 scrip + 2 wild | Round end: +1 scrip per member you bought this round (max 3). | +1 |
| Outfitter | 4 scrip + 2 wild | Your members cost 1 less (minimum 1). | +1 |
| Mogul | 5 scrip + 2 wild | Final round end: +1 follower per 3 members you own. | +2 |

Notes:
- **How it fixes the Auction House:**
  - *Ties every round:* the Ledger sums printed costs instead of counting buys, so exact ties are rare. When they happen, all tied players score, so a tie is never a wash.
  - *Stall drying:* Trade-In sends members back to their decks, so the Bazaar refills what it drains. Extra buys come only from Bulk Order, Spree and Merchant, not from every space.
  - *Unpayable cross-stall buys:* scrip covers the specific part of any member's cost, and wild covers the rest. Every "any stall" buy can be paid for.
- **Engine arc:** cheap members (Porter, Hawker) feed Commission and Quartermaster in round I. Trade-In upgrades them in round II. Spree plus Outfitter gives three discounted buys in round III, which wins the 3-follower Ledger and pumps Mogul.
- **Interplay:** the Ledger is contested by everyone, since everyone buys members. Commission and Peddler pay you to watch rivals shop. Browse and Spree compete for other spheres' stall members, a public race over shared items.
- **Distinct from Treasury:** Treasury rewards hoarding, and the Bazaar rewards spending. The two pull in opposite directions at the same table.
- **Watch:**
  - Outfitter + Shopkeep + Haggle can make cheap members free. Minimum 1 stays.
  - Spree may empty a rival's stall in round III. That's acceptable, since it's the last round.
  - Mogul at 7 must still beat a Diadem for a member-heavy player. Check it at the tables.
