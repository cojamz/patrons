## Gold: The Treasury
Pitch: Gold is the crown you're always tempted to spend.
Resource: gold.
Verbs: gain, sponsor (mark a space), trade
Scoring: **Crown.** At round end, whoever holds the most gold gains I 1 / II 2 / III 3 followers. Ties: each tied player gains 1 less.

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | Mint | +1 gold, +1 per round number. |
| I | Sponsor | +2 gold. Mark an empty space; its next taker gains 1 gold. |
| I | Cash In | Trade up to 3 resources for gold one-for-one, then +1 gold. |
| II | Ledger | +2 gold, +1 per member you have (max +3). |
| II | Covet | +3 gold, +1 per rival holding more gold than you. |
| III | Jackpot | Double your gold (max +6). |

| Member | Cost (specific + wild) | Effect (≤14 words) | Extra followers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coin Purse | 1 gold | At each round start, +1 gold. | 0 |
| Piggy Bank | 2 gold | On your turn, release it for 4 gold. | 0 |
| Strongbox | 2 gold + 1 wild | Your gold counts +2 when the Crown is judged. | 0 |
| Moneylender | 1 gold + 2 wild | Whenever a rival buys a fixture, +1 gold (max 2 per round). | 0 |
| Collector | 3 gold + 1 wild | Whenever a rival buys a member, +1 gold (max 2 per round). | 0 |
| Banker | 4 gold + 1 wild | At each round start, +1 gold per 2 members you have (max 3). | 0 |
| Spendthrift | 4 gold + 2 wild | Whenever you buy a fixture, +2 gold. | +1 |
| Tycoon | 5 gold + 2 wild | At game end, +1 follower per 3 gold you hold (max 3). | +1 |

Notes:
- One tension: gold wins the Crown, and the gold-heavy specific costs (wild kept small) mean every member you buy drains it. Ledger and Banker pay you back for spending, so the hoarder and the spender both have a line.
- Interplay is generic: Sponsor lures a rival onto a space, Covet feeds off rivals' gold, Collector and Moneylender watch rivals' buys, and the Crown is a public race.
- Changes from b2:
  - Crown ties now shave 1, like other majority races (was: all tied score full).
  - Numbers tightened: Mint 2/3/4 (was 3/4/5), Jackpot max +6 (was +8).
  - Leak plugged: Sponsor's spill is 1 gold to the taker only (was 1 to them and 1 to you).
  - Banker loses its extra follower and needs 2 members per gold.
  - Interest became Ledger (rewards spending, and "Interest" is the Vault's rule name). Exchange became Cash In ("Exchange" is a Mosaic action).
  - Moneychanger cut (dead once fixtures take any resource). Added Strongbox, Moneylender and Spendthrift to reach 8.
- Watch: Strongbox plus Jackpot on a pure hoarder. Also, Mosaic's b2 color was also called "gold". One of them needs a new name if both make the cut.
