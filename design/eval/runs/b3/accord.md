## Rose: The Accord (deals)
*New in b3.*

Pitch: Post offers, shake hands, both win.
Verbs: offer (post a public trade), accept (take one), barter (swap with the bank).
Resource: **seals** (rose).

Deal rules (one panel, shown by the UI):
- **Offer:** set up to 3 of your own resources on an offer card and name one color. You may have at most 2 open offers.
- **Accept:** once on each of your turns, before or after you place, you may accept one rival's open offer. You give the same number of the named color and take the goods. You can't accept your own offer.
- **Deal struck:** the poster and the accepter each gain 1 seal from the bank. That bonus is what makes every deal positive-sum.
- Offers left unaccepted return to their poster at round end. There's no haggling and nobody waits: terms are fixed when posted, and accepting happens only on your own turn.

Scoring: **Handshake** (paid at round end). +1 follower per deal you struck this round, as poster or accepter. Max I 1 / II 2 / III 3.

Notes:
- **Never stalls:** there's no negotiation, nobody gets a reply window, and nobody waits on anyone. An offer is a public card, and others act on it only on their own turns.
- **Positive-sum by construction:** every deal pays both sides a seal plus a Handshake follower. Swapping colors helps both of them buy members. Offers get posted only from Accord spaces (6 a round) and Notary, so the number of deals is bounded and the follower bonus can't spiral.
- **Collusion check:** two players trading back and forth is capped by Handshake's 1/2/3 limit and by the number of offers available. Ambassador rewards dealing with *different* rivals, which pulls against pairing up.
- **Interplay:** anyone who takes an Accord space posts offers, so the whole table deals, not just the Accord player. Chatter, Matchmaker and Charter pay you to watch the market. Nothing harms anyone.
- **Engine arc:** in round I you post and gather seals. In round II you double up and accept more. In round III, Charter turns everyone's deals into your income just as 5 workers per player create the most deals.
- **Generic:** offers trade any colors and never name another sphere.
- **Watch:**
  - Whether rivals actually accept. If offers sit unaccepted, give the accepter +1 more seal, so that accepting is a lure.
  - Guarantor may make every offer an instant accept. That's fine if it is.
