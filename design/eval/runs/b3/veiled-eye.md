## Violet: The Bookie
Pitch: Call where they're headed.
Resource: **chips** (violet).
Verb: **tip** (put your marker on an empty open space).

Tip rules:
- When a rival places on your tip, it pays: they gain 2 chips, you gain 1. Flip it face-up; it stays until round end.
- An unpaid tip clears when you place your next worker. Your last worker's tips last until round end.
- Your own worker on your tip clears it unpaid.
- No empty open space to tip? Gain 2 chips per tip instead.

Scoring — **Payout**: at round end, +1 follower per tip of yours that paid this round. Max 1 / 2 / 3 (printed on rings I / II / III).

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | Tip | Tip an empty open space. +2 chips. |
| I | Odds | +1 chip per tip on the wheel, anyone's. Minimum 2, maximum 5. |
| I | Hedge | +2 chips, +1 per tip of yours paid this round. |
| II | Spread | Tip two empty open spaces. +2 chips. |
| II | Long Shot | Tip a space. If it pays, you gain 4 chips, not 1. |
| III | Big Book | Tip three spaces. This round, each tip of yours that pays gives +1 any. |

| Member | Cost (specific + wild) | Effect (≤14 words) | Extra followers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Runner | 1 chip | Round start: gain 1 chip. | 0 |
| Tout | 2 chips | When you tip, gain 1 chip. | 0 |
| Bagman | 3 wild | When you take a space a rival tipped, gain 1 more chip. | 0 |
| Fixer | 2 chips + 1 wild | Once per round, move one of your unpaid tips. | 0 |
| Watcher | 2 chips + 2 wild | When any tip pays, gain 1 chip. Max 2 per round. | 0 |
| Oddsmaker | 3 chips + 2 wild | Your Payout max is 1 higher. | +1 |
| Tipster | 4 chips + 2 wild | When a tip of yours pays, also gain 1 any. | +1 |
| Kingpin | 4 chips + 3 wild | Tip and Spread each place one more tip. | +2 |

Notes:
- **A read, not a tax.** A tip only lives until your next placement, so it pays only if someone takes that space in the next few picks. In round I every space fills eventually, but not before your next turn. The taker always gains more than you: a lure, never a toll.
- **Not dominant.** Big Book pays +1 per paid tip (was 2 any). Payout counts only this round's tips. One cap raiser (Oddsmaker); Watcher capped at 2. Nothing stacks into the cap twice.
- **Best interplay kept.** Every tip spills chips to whoever takes it. Bagman rewards walking into rivals' tips. Watcher and Odds count everyone's tips.
- **Burst.** Big Book with 3+ workers left: three live reads, up to 3 followers, plus chips and any-color on top.
- Changed from b2: tips expire on your next placement (were permanent until paid), Long Shot no longer inner-ring-only, Hedge scales, fallback for no empty space, 8 members, color fixed as violet (Vault is cobalt).
