## Green: The Encore
Pitch: Your best moves come back for a second bow.
Resource: memory.
Verbs: echo (only your own past spaces), plant
Scoring: **Refrain.** At round end, +1 follower per echo you made this round, max I 1 / II 2 / III 3.

Echo rule (printed on the board edge):
- You may echo only a space **you** took earlier, this round or a past one.
- Core rules apply: the echo copies the yield you got then, it can't echo an echo, and it can't echo a center.
- You don't occupy the echoed space, and you still buy from this stall.

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | Tend | +2 memory, +1 per echo you've made this game (max +3). |
| I | Recall | Echo a space you took earlier this round (none: +3 memory). |
| I | Sprout | +1 memory now, +3 at next round start. Round III: +4 now instead. |
| II | Reprise | Echo a ring I–II space you took last round (round I: +3 memory). |
| II | Chorus | Echo a space you took this game; whoever is last in followers gains 1 memory. |
| III | Finale | Echo up to three different spaces you took this game. |

Notes:
- Core: memory and time. You build a history of good placements, then replay them. Encore reads your own trail, while Gatecrashers reads the live board, so the two no longer blur.
- The Civ arc comes for free: round-I echoes copy small yields, and round-III echoes copy your best round-II plays. Finale is the burst.
- Interplay is generic and light: Old Haunt watches rivals retake your old spaces, and Chorus spills to whoever is last.
- Changes from b2:
  - Echo only your own spaces (Mirror, Harmony, Mirror Twin and Bloom's "anyone" are gone). It no longer depends on a rich partner.
  - Caps kept at 1/2/3.
  - Scoring renamed Refrain ("Encore" is a Night Shift action name).
  - Tend counts echoes this game, so it scales instead of going flat.
  - Sprout gets a round-III mode so it's never dead.
  - The Gardener effect moved to Scrapbook. World Tree became Standing Ovation, now counting as one echo.
  - Added Old Haunt, Rehearsal and Chronicler to reach 8.
- Watch: Finale plus Standing Ovation copying a round-II peak (4 big copies). The core yield-copy rule should keep it from reaching table 8's 8–13 per placement.
