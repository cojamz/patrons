## Cobalt: The Vault
Pitch: Lock it up, cash it out.
Resource: **ingots** (cobalt).
Verb: **vault** (move resources into your vault).

Vault rules:
- Vaulted resources can't be spent this round.
- At round end, after scoring, your vault returns to your supply.

Scoring — **Interest**: at round end, +1 follower per 3 in your vault. Max 1 / 2 / 3 (printed on rings I / II / III).

| Ring | Action | Effect (≤14 words) |
|---|---|---|
| I | Deposit | Vault up to 3 of your resources, then +1 ingot. |
| I | Accrue | Put 3 ingots in your vault. Each rival gains 1 ingot. |
| I | Dividend | +2 ingots, +1 per 3 in your vault. |
| II | Compound | Add ingots to your vault equal to half its size, minimum 2. |
| II | Payday | +3 ingots. Then vault up to 2 of your resources. |
| III | Bonanza | Double your vault with ingots. Your Interest has no max this round. |

| Member | Cost (specific + wild) | Effect (≤14 words) | Extra followers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lockbox | 1 ingot | Round start: put 1 ingot in your vault. | 0 |
| Clerk | 2 ingots | Dividend and Payday give you 1 more ingot. | 0 |
| Pawnbroker | 2 wild | When a rival buys a fixture, put 1 ingot in your vault. | 0 |
| Teller | 3 ingots + 1 wild | Once per round, move up to 2 resources into or out of your vault. | 0 |
| Auditor | 2 ingots + 2 wild | Your vault counts 2 more for Interest. | 0 |
| Underwriter | 3 ingots + 2 wild | Round start: vault up to 3 resources, +1 ingot each. | 0 |
| Heir | 4 ingots + 2 wild | Whenever you vault resources, also put 1 ingot in your vault. | +1 |
| Magnate | 4 ingots + 3 wild | When Interest pays you, +1 more follower. | +2 |

Notes:
- **Less fragile.** Interest pays per 3, so a half-built vault still scores. Two of three round-I spaces reach the round-I line alone (Accrue; Deposit with 3 in hand), and Payday gives ring II its own way in. Losing one space costs a little, not everything.
- **The tension.** Rounds I–II: vaulting delays buying, nothing is lost. Round III: the vault never comes back, so vaulting is a real spend. Bonanza lifts the cap so it can match a Diadem (9 vaulted → 18 → 6 followers), not beat it.
- **Bonanza only doubles.** The b2 round-end fixture buy is gone.
- **Interplay.** Accrue spills ingots to rivals' hands, never their vaults. Pawnbroker watches the fixture race. Vaulting competes with the Diadem clock for the same resources.
- Changed from b2: thresholds 4/6/8 became per-3 (3/6/9 to max out), Bonanza's fixture buy cut, Pension and Broker replaced; Strongbox/Moneylender names left to the Treasury, 8 members, coin renamed ingots (gold belongs to the Treasury).
