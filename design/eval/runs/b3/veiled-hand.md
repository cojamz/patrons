## Shadow: The Gatecrashers
Pitch: Every space is open if you don't mind company.
Resource: shadow.
Verbs: share (crowd onto a rival's occupied space), take (from the leader only)
Scoring: **Crowds.** At round end, +1 follower per space you shared this round, max I 1 / II 2 / III 3.

Sharing rule (printed on the board edge):
- To share, you crowd onto a space a rival occupies right now and resolve its **base effect**. The base effect is the printed effect without any "+1 per…", "double" or other scaling bonus.
- A space holds at most 2 workers.
- You can't share a center, or a space whose effect echoes or shares.
- The rival you share with gains 1 shadow.
- There are two ways to share:
  - **With a worker** (Slip In, Lockpick, Ringleader): your worker stands beside theirs. It counts as placing there, so you buy from that space's stall.
  - **From here** (Gatecrash, Heist): no worker moves, and you buy from the Gatecrashers stall.

Notes:
- Core: crowding. "That space is gone" becomes "that space is contested", and the action happens on the board, not at people. It only feeds off what rivals are doing now. Your own past is Encore's territory.
- Being shared on is a small gift (+1 shadow), so getting crowded never feels bad.
- Changes from b2:
  - The sharer gets the base effect only. This kills the +12 piggyback on rivals' scaling spaces seen at table 6.
  - Centers can't be shared, so the center contest stays real.
  - Gatecrash and Heist have a fallback, so a first pick is never dead.
  - The occupant spill moved from Lookout into the rule ("Lookout" was also a Night Shift and Mosaic name).
  - Smuggler loses its discount. Ringleader is now always-on, since once per round was weaker than Slip In.
  - New members: Wallflower (catch-up), Eavesdropper (watch the table), Afterparty.
- Watch: whether Ringleader plus Fence turns every placement into a share. The base-effect rule should keep each share modest.
