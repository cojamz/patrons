## Interplay graph
- **Gold** emits: gains gold, marks spaces, gives (Sponsor), holds the most gold. Consumes: item buys (Commission, Tithe Box), takes (Vault).
- **Shadow** emits: takes (up only), shares spaces, buys off-stall, holds the most shadow. Consumes: item buys (Informant).
- **Verdant** emits: echoes, gives (Echo Chamber). Consumes: rivals' spaces (Echo), rivals' items (Graft), echoes (Cuttings).
- **Sunlight** emits: gives (Bounty, Zenith, Gift Basket), gains 3 or more at once, sets. Consumes: gains of 3 or more (Beacon), gifts (gifts help everyone's sets).
- **Links (9):**
  - buys → Tithe Box, Commission, Informant;
  - takes → Vault, Veil;
  - any space → Echo;
  - items → Graft;
  - gains of 3 or more → Beacon;
  - gives → Harmony.

  Every quadrant both emits and consumes.

**Swap test:**
- Remove Shadow and **Vault dangles**: no one is left to take anything. Defensive items need a second use.
- Echo is only emitted by Verdant, so Cuttings works only when Verdant is present. That's fine, since it's Verdant's own item.
- Remove any other quadrant and nothing else dangles.
