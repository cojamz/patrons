# Batch 3 synthesis (8 tables, 11 spheres)

## Problems across the whole system
1. **The member economy is the weak point.**
   - Decks run dry by round III at 4 of 8 tables.
   - 1-cost members are the best buy in the game.
   - Round III collapses into a fixture race.
   - This matches Cory's read: members aren't numerous or central enough.
2. **Fixtures still swing too much.** Their share of all followers ran from 23% to 57% depending on the table.
3. **Rules that keep failing:**
   - Ties at the top make "leader" effects go dead.
   - Echoes of payments and delayed effects aren't defined.
   - Last-worker tempo actions do nothing.
   - The Diadem clock never fires.
   - There's no final tiebreak.
