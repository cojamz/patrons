# Batch 3, table 2: Ladder / Kiln / Vault / Bookie
**Final followers:**
- Opportunist (Kiln) 27
- Newcomer (Ladder) 26
- Builder (Vault) 24
- Spoiler (Bookie) 22

Followers came about one-third each from members, fixtures and sphere scoring.
Interplay: 3/5.

**Spheres:**
- **Ladder:** fun, a real catch-up, and needs no partner.
- **Kiln:** too strong. Kindling at 1 wild is the best buy in the game. Fuel turns a pile of heat into fixtures, and Inferno can buy a whole Diadem.
- **Vault:** sluggish early, then too strong late. Bonanza plus Compound, Heir and Auditor can outdo a Diadem.
- **Bookie:** the best interplay, at fair strength. But a tip vanishes on the snake's double-pick, which punishes whoever sits at the ends of the snake.

**Fixes:**
1. A tip survives until a rival has placed.
2. Kiln: heat counts as only one color for fixtures, Inferno is capped at 3 burns, and burned Tinder doesn't score.
3. Members cost at least 2, or a 1-cost member gives no extra follower. Cap Bonanza.
