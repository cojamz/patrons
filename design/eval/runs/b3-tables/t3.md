# Batch 3, table 3: Night Shift / Bazaar / Accord / Treasury
**Final followers:**
- Spoiler 25
- Newcomer 24
- Builder 23
- Opportunist 23

Followers came from members about 40%, sphere scoring 37% and fixtures 23%. Cheap 1-cost members did most of the scoring.
Interplay: 3/5.

**Spheres:**
- **Night Shift:** the most fun to sequence. Dusk, Dawn and Midnight are too strong, and Night Watch went uncontested. Tempo actions do nothing on your last worker.
- **Bazaar:** strongest. Scrip erases every other sphere's specific cost. Spree buying fixtures would break the game.
- **Accord:** the most interactive. Deals aimed at the hoarder were the best moments. Middleman and Chatter are dead when no offers are open.
- **Treasury:** easy to read, but the Crown is a free +6 for a lone hoarder.

**Fixes:**
1. Scrip pays at most 1 of a member's specific cost. Extra buys are members only. Every cost has a floor of 1.
2. Tempo actions on your last worker give +2 oil instead. Cap Dusk and Dawn. Contests need 2 or more players competing.
3. Offers must be at least 2 resources, for a different color. Add fallbacks. Rename Bazaar's Ledger and Porter.
