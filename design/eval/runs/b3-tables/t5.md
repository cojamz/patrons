# Batch 3, table 5: Encore / Vault / Night Shift / Ladder
**Final followers:**
- Builder 32
- Opportunist 31
- Spoiler 28
- Newcomer 26

Close all game, with the burst in round III. Followers came about one-third each from fixtures, members and sphere scoring.
Interplay: 3/5.

**Spheres:**
- **Encore:** distinct and fun, and needs no partner. Nearly solitaire.
- **Vault:** Accrue is the best round-I space, and Bonanza is about right.
- **Night Shift:** strongest, and a problem. A chain of speed-up actions lets one player take 4 placements in a single turn and empty the sphere.
- **Ladder:** good tension, but it does nothing when players are tied. Because targets are set at round end, buying followers can turn a challenge against you, so players avoid buying.

**Fixes:**
1. A worker that was sped up can't speed up again. Double Shift goes outside this sphere.
2. Set Ladder's targets at round start.
3. An echo copies only resources gained, not vault moves or delayed effects. Finale counts as 3 echoes. Give Encore one spill to other players.
