# Batch 3, table 1: Treasury / Gatecrashers / Encore / Mosaic
**Final followers:**
- Opportunist (Mosaic) 41
- Builder (Encore) 37
- Newcomer (Gold) 28
- Spoiler (Shadow) 27

Followers came from fixtures 57%, members 21% and sphere scoring 18%. The leader got 33 of their 41 from fixtures.
Interplay: 3/5.

**Spheres:**
- **Gold:** reads well, but a hand of pure gold can't buy a Garland or Diadem, which need mixed colors. It's a trap for new players.
- **Gatecrashers:** the most fun. Sharing Prism is the best share. Its first picks in round III hit the fallback.
- **Encore:** clean, but strong and near-solitaire.
- **Mosaic:** strongest. Its spread of colors makes the Diadem easy.

**Feel-bad:** taking 1 resource broke someone's only full set, which cost them several followers' worth.

**Fixes:**
1. Make fixtures clearly worse than members (Diadem 12 for 6, or 1 fixture per round).
2. An echo copies only what was gained, not what was paid. A take can't remove someone's last resource of a color.
3. Gold can trade out to reach the mixed-color fixtures.
