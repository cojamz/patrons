# Batch 3, table 6: Mosaic / Bookie / Bazaar / Gatecrashers
**Final followers:**
- Newcomer (Mosaic) 37
- Builder 32
- Spoiler 32
- Opportunist 30

Followers came from fixtures 37%, members 36% and sphere scoring 24%.
Interplay: 4/5.

**Spheres:**
- **Mosaic:** fun and distinct, strong late. Its Spectrum contest usually goes uncontested.
- **Bookie:** the most fun, and the best lure design.
- **Bazaar:** fun, and Ledger is a real contest. But Spree wins Ledger in round III on its own.
- **Gatecrashers:** distinct and never feels bad. Weak early: a first-pick Heist in round III was dead.

**Problems:**
- Stalls were dry by round III, so late buyers had only fixtures left. That confirms "too few members".
- The game has too many ways to buy from any stall. That makes Go-Between and Smuggler near-dead text.

**Fixes:**
1. Spree gets 2 buys.
2. Bigger member decks (10 or more), or recycle unbought cards.
3. Consolidate the any-stall buying.
