# W3 decipher pass: every space's panel and hover, read cold (2026-10-15)

The words designer's pass after wave 3's blind testers (design/eval/wave3/tester-a and tester-b; A/B numbers below are
theirs). For every space of every sphere I read only the panel's lines as a newcomer would, wrote down what I thought it
does, and compared that with the rule. Then I checked each panel on the real screen: five tables (classic; magenta,
white, purple, silver; yellow, magenta, white, green; gold, purple, silver, blue; the plain blue and green with yellow
and red), rounds I, II and III, at 1270×700, with the text-clip check and every hover read back
(scratchpad script: real mid-game saves, seat 0 to place). No rule, number or ability changed; only words.

Values are round II. ● is the resource dot, 👤 the follower mark. "Line 3" is the small print.

## What the screen does with the lines (measured, and why some words moved)
- **Line 3 almost never shows at 1270×700.** A quadrant's ring shows its small print only when every panel there has
  room, and in 15 table-rounds that held only for a few rings. So line 3 is hover-sized help, never the only home of
  anything that decides the gain.
- **Line 2 drops whole when it doesn't fit**, and with it nothing but line 1 is left. Ring II verb panels drop it at
  some turns of the wheel, and the center ring always does. So line 2 stays within about 16 letters a line, and a
  center or ring II verb is whole in line 1.
- Joining "more each round" onto a full line 2 made four ring I panels drop line 2 entirely; it now takes line 2 only
  where line 2 is empty, else line 3.

## "You'd get" (B20)
The dry run now keeps which branch is worth most to you (best) and which one is likely (you pick what's worth most to
you, a rival picks what's worth most to them), on a rough ruler that's never shown (resource 1, follower 2, member 4,
extra turn 3). The line says, in numbers:
- a rival decides: "+4● likely · +1 member, +2👤 at best" (Shake Down; Greatest Hits copying one)
- your pick: your best pick's numbers, "your best pick" (Sell High "+6●, −2👤"; All In "+18👤, −18 resources";
  Poach "+1 member, +2👤"; Echo's best copy; Snap Up's best recruit)
- every pick worth the same, only the mix differs: "+2👤 or +4●" (Your Call), "+2●, +2 of a color you pick" (Goody
  Bags), "+4● · swaps are your pick" (Sweet Talk, Trade Seeds)
- a swap says "1 member swapped", never "nothing"; a discount used up no longer prints "next member −-6"; "about" never
  sits before "nothing".
The rival's own choice no longer adds "a rival decides part of it" when it doesn't change what you get.
