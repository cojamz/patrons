## Game 1 — table Gold / Yellow / Magenta (steals) / Blue, 3 Normal bots, speed Normal. I'm the octopus (seat 1).
Setup notes: members cost ~6 resources for 1 follower (Crew: 2 res → 2 followers, no ability). Follower buys 2→1, 4→3, 8→6, 10→10.
Plan: gold engine. Penny Pincher (4 gold: gold you spend on members counts double) + Kickback (+3 gold) + Most Gold contest.
Round I order was P2, P4, me, P3 (snake).
- Setup: Gold + Gold. Why: Penny Pincher costs 4 gold; Kickback gives +3 gold → buy it on turn 1.
- R1 m1: Kickback (+3 gold, +1 gold to a rival; gave it to P3 who had none). Buy Penny Pincher (4 gold). Why: gold engine.
- R1 m2: Golden Years (+2 gold in round I). Buy Prospector (2 gold + 2 any — paid 2 gold thanks to Penny Pincher). Why: +1 gold whenever an action gains gold.
- R1 m3: Redistribute (take 1 from each rival richer than me; P2 was "safe" from Lie Low). Took the gold from P3 and P4 to win Most Gold. Bought Front-runner (2 any: always pick first). Why: same price as 2→1 followers but with an ability — strictly better than the follower buy.
