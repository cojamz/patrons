# Neutral members in the Straggler mold (Claude's proposals, 2026-10-14; nothing approved)

Cory "really likes" Straggler ("Once per round, after the last minion is placed: move one of your minions to an unused space and do that action."). These bend a placement or turn-order rule instead of paying out, like the guests in content/benders.js. Like the guests, each leans to a sphere for its color.

Prices are by comparison with the measured guests (member-value sim: Straggler 8, Squatter 8, Extra Hand 10, Insider 6, Big Spender 4, Freelancer 4, Front-runner 2). Every one gets a member-value run before it goes in the deck.

1. Line Cutter (6, leans purple): Once per round, right after a rival places a minion: place one of yours, out of turn.
2. Doorman (4, leans red): Once per round: hold an open space. Rivals can't take it until your next turn.
3. Drifter (4, leans purple): Once per round, after the last minion is placed: move one of your minions to an empty space beside it and do that space.
4. Gatecrasher (4, leans purple): Once per game: place a minion on a ring that hasn't opened yet.
5. Usher (4, leans purple): At round start, you choose your seat in the turn order.
6. Ringleader (8, leans gold): Your minion on the inner ring does its space twice.
7. Double Booker (10, leans gold): Once per round: place a minion on a space you already hold; it pays you again.
8. Fortune Teller (2, leans blue): At round start, look at the top 3 of the pile and put them back in any order.

Claude's picks: 1, 2 and 3. They sharpen "will my spot still be open?" the way Straggler does, and each says what it does in one line.
Notes: Drifter is Straggler's little sibling (a neighbouring space only, so cheaper). Usher is a stronger Front-runner. Fortune Teller pairs with Insider (recruit from the top 3 of the pile). Double Booker is the riskiest: it repeats your best space every round.
