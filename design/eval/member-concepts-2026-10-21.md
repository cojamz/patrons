# Member concepts for Cory's cut (Oct 21, overnight)

Made with design/member-process.md. Every new card and text change below is Claude's fill-in until Cory says yes. No prices here: sims set them after his cut. Each color is listed strongest to weakest (Claude's read before any sim), counters marked.

Light by rule: every card he praised stays as written; changes follow his own notes, quoted; new cards only fill a job (a counter, a cheap body, the top of the range, or a slot whose card he cut).

Sims tonight cover the colors whose actions are settled (Magenta, Blue) and the guests. Gold, Purple, Silver, Yellow and White still have their old actions, which give far more than today's economy (Golden Years +6, Toast +9, Clock In +6), so their member prices would move once he reworks those actions. Their concepts are here for his cut; their sims wait for the actions.

## Blue members (actions settled, v0.8.19)
Today: Stylist, Connoisseur, Planner, Underbidder, Minimalist, Sommelier, Patroness, Snob, Recluse (Social Climber cut). Planner, Underbidder and Sommelier are cost cuts, which he dropped Oct 18, so their slots need new cards.
Strongest to weakest: Shopaholic, Snob, Recluse (counter), Connoisseur, Patroness, Window Dresser, Plus-One, Minimalist (counter), the body, Stylist, Doorman (counter).
1. Keep as written: Snob, Recluse, Connoisseur, Patroness, Minimalist, Stylist. [yes]
2. Shopaholic, his words from Oct 20: "You can always buy twice." The deck's top card. [yes]
3. Planner's slot: Window Dresser, "When recruited: send up to 3 shop members to the bottom, then refill." His "rearrange the shop and then buy a bunch" (#35) on a card: buy it first, then spend the next buy on the fresh row. [yes]
4. Underbidder's slot: Plus-One, "When recruited: buy again this turn." The weak twin of Shopaholic, once. [yes]
5. Sommelier's slot, a counter to the Blue player: Doorman, "Whenever a rival recruits a second member in one turn, +1 blue." It pays on the Blue player's big turns and feeds your held blue against theirs. [yes]
6. Social Climber's slot: a plain body worth 2, so Blue has a cheap pickup like Twins and Crew. [yes]
Counters, as read: Recluse and Minimalist already pay you for having fewer members than the wide cult Blue builds; Doorman taxes its big turns.
