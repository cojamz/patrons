# Action names: calibration batch (Gold, Red, Magenta)

For Cory to grade before the other spheres get renamed. Each line reads: current name (what it does): **a** plain leader's verb / **b** wittier / **c** wildcard, then my pick. Every candidate is 1–2 words, at most 14 characters, and clashes with no current action or member name (checked by script).

## Gold: the following's money
1. Joint Venture (+3 gold, and +1 gold to anyone): Share Profits / Kickback / Handshake. Pick: **Kickback**
2. Rush (+2 gold per round: 2/4/6): Raise Funds / Golden Years / Compound. Pick: **Golden Years**
3. Discount (your next member costs 4 less): Waive Fees / Intro Offer / Scholarship. Pick: **Intro Offer**
4. Steal (take up to 3 gold from rivals): Collect Debts / Pay Up / Levy. Pick: **Collect Debts**
5. Windfall (+1 gold per member you have): Collect Dues / Tithe / Head Tax. Pick: **Tithe**
6. Strike (double your gold): Double Up / Double Down / Bonanza. Pick: **Double Down**

Gold set: Kickback, Golden Years, Intro Offer / Collect Debts, Tithe / Double Down. It all sounds like the cult's books: dues, cuts, debts and one big bet. Golden Years says "better later" (2/4/6). Tithe is "per member", said in one word.

## Red: drawing crowds
1. Scout (+2 red, and bury one face-up member): Turn Away / Not Tonight / Velvet Rope. Pick: **Turn Away**
2. Turnout (+1 red per member recruited this round, by anyone): Count Arrivals / Bandwagon / Welcome Wagon. Pick: **Bandwagon**
3. Hire (the cheapest face-up member, free): Enlist / Open Door / Walk-in. Pick: **Open Door**
4. Hire Up (+2 red, and the cheapest face-up member, free): Canvass / Open House / Job Fair. Pick: **Open House**
5. Trade (swap a member for a rival's, up to +2 cost): Swap Members / Swap Sides / Even Swap. Pick: **Swap Sides**
6. Take (any face-up member, free): Hand-pick / Cherry-pick / Chosen One. Pick: **Hand-pick**

Red set: Turn Away, Bandwagon, Open Door / Open House, Swap Sides / Hand-pick. You are the leader at the door, deciding who comes in. Open Door and Open House are a pair, so the bigger version still reads as kin, the way Hire and Hire Up did. Bandwagon tells you to wait until the crowd has joined. I passed on Welcome Wagon because a welcome wagon brings gifts *to* newcomers, which is backwards here.

## Magenta: the light-fingered cult
1. Pickpocket (take 2/3/4 from rivals, split freely): Pick Pockets / Help Yourself / Mingle. Pick: **Help Yourself**
2. Tip-off (take 1 from each player richer than you): Redistribute / Fair Share / Envy. Pick: **Redistribute**
3. Hideout (take 1; theft-proof this round): Stash Loot / Lie Low / Safe House. Pick: **Lie Low**
4. Rob (take 3, at least 1 per rival): Rob Everyone / Crime Wave / House Calls. Pick: **Crime Wave**
5. Ransom (rival pays a member's cost, or loses it): Shake Down / Protection / Lure Away. Pick: **Shake Down**
6. Heist (take a rival's member; they get a replacement): Poach / Steal Away / Headhunt. Pick: **Poach**

Magenta set: Help Yourself, Redistribute, Lie Low / Crime Wave, Shake Down / Poach. The voice is cheeky, not violent, and it escalates from pockets to people. I passed on Protection because players would read it as defense (Hideout, Watchdog). I passed on Fair Share and kept it free, because it sounds like a giving action Silver may want.

## Naming rules (for the remaining spheres)
An action name is something the leader *does*: a verb phrase or an event (Turn Away, Open House, Shake Down). It is never a person, because people are members, which is why Chosen One and Loan Shark are out. Use 1–2 plain English words, at most 14 characters, with no "&" and no in-jokes. A new player should guess the core effect from the name and the sphere's color, and the scaling detail can stay in the card text. The wit should come from an everyday idiom whose plain meaning is the effect (Double Down, Help Yourself), never from a pun you have to decode. Every name must be unique across all actions and members. Within one sphere, two picks never share a lead word (so Collect Dues and Collect Debts can't both be picked). The R1 and R2 versions of the same effect share a root (Open Door / Open House). Never use the base rule words (recruit, release, sway, follower, member, buy, favor) or game terms (round, ring, set, shop) as a name, and never use religious words that aren't everyday speech (Tithe yes, Sacrament no, Passover no). Each sphere keeps one register: Gold sounds like the cult's books, Red like the door and the crowd, Magenta like a cheeky crew. Already flagged for the remaining spheres: Bloom is used twice today (the Green stand-in and Yellow), "Collection" would read as Yellow's sets, and "Fair Share" and "Double …" sit close to existing ideas (Silver; Double Time and Double Agent).
