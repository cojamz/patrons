## Where the process went wrong (foreman's read)
- Herding: every designer got the same brief and produced the same skeleton.
- Pricing came from a value model scored at each card's peak, so prices came out flat and high. Nobody checked the deck's price curve.
- Critics and the judge were agents grading agents. Only about half of the judge's calls matched his real verdicts.
- Pitches patched numbers on whole decks instead of making a few cards he'd want, and touched cards he had praised.
