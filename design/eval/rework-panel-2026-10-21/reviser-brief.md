# Reviser brief (rework panel, phase 4)

You take the concept the panel chose for one color and turn it into the version Cory sees. Five critics went at every pitch: design/eval/rework-panel-2026-10-21/critiques/{pricing,feel,judge,skeptic,architect}.md. Read all five for your color, the chosen pitch file, the alternative's pitch file, plus brief.md, member-shape.md, pricing.md, cory-judge.md (rubric v3) and Cory's notes on your color in design/todo.md.

## Rules every revision follows (from the critics, all colors)
1. **Apply every fix the critics agree on** for your concept; where they disagree, decide and say why in one line.
2. **Break the shared skeleton.** The judge found seven decks with the same ten slots (plain body, "rivals can't X" defense, round II action on arrival, "+1 when you take this color's space", "+1 per N held" scorer, once-per-game round III capstone). Cory will read that as agent-forged slight variations. Every card should be one only this color could have. No defense card that only guards against a verb this pitch invented. At most one "+1 follower per N" scorer, and only if it's the color's own shape.
3. **Fewer cards is fine.** If only 7 cards are genuinely good, give 7 and say "3 slots open for you". Never pad.
4. **Caps on relative gains**: any match / tie / raise-to / double / per-member gain in round II prints a cap of about 5, in round III about 10. Member-counting effects count member cards, not bodies (the engine counts a worth-N member as N members), or are capped.
5. **Say how Green copies each space** (one line): a copy replays the original taker's gain unless the space is flagged so the copier does the action themselves. Spaces that take from a shared pile, or re-fire members, must be flagged.
6. **No "you win ties" card** (Cory, Oct 16: "ties all score"; a guest version is already with him). **No re-firing of Clockmaker-style compounding income.** **No out-of-turn buys.** **At most one turn-moving source besides Crunch Time on the whole board.**
7. Name collisions: no live card name reused (check live-cards.txt).
8. Arithmetic: per space, what the taker walks away with in rounds I/II/III at 2 and 4 players. Per member: cost colored+any, followers, one line why that price, against how much of its own color a specialist earns in a game (say the number).
9. All texts and numbers are Claude's fill-ins; say so in the header.

## Output
Write design/eval/rework-panel-2026-10-21/final/<color>.md:
- One paragraph: the identity, and what changes for a player at the table.
- The actions (round III, II ×2, I ×3), each with text, walk-away per round, copy line.
- Contest.
- The deck.
- The alternative direction in 5 lines (what it is, why it ranked second).
- **Picks for Cory**: 3 to 5 numbered questions he can answer by number, one line each, quoting card text in a few words, default in brackets. Only design and feel calls. The first pick is always the direction itself.
Return at most 20 lines: the picks.
