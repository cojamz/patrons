# Guests: the skeptic (2026-10-21)

Scope: pitches/guest-members.md only. Grounded in strategy-read.md and live code: contests pay 2/4/6, a tie pays each tied winner half (core.js `contestTable`, `prize/2`); onRoundEnd members fire *before* contests (core.js `endRound`); turn order is fewest-followers first after round I; packs are 1/5/10 in any colors. No sims. Scores are my seat's read.

## Verdicts

**Launderer (new, 4): FIX, as written it's the strongest card in the pitch.**
- Abuse: buy it with the last buy of round III. Turn order is fewest-first, so the player who places last in round III is usually the *leader*. They see every rival's pile, pay 4, then deal what's left across the contests so they just win or tie each one. With 8–9 resources left at a 3–4p table, that's two or three round III contests at 6 each: 12–18 followers for 4, plus perfect information. It's win-more, and it's a bomb at the end of the game.
- It also takes Yellow's open verb (strategy-read gives Yellow "reshapes"), and it overlaps Freelancer, so you'd have two colorless cards that dodge colored parts.
- Red angle: Recruit a Member (free up to 10) and Poach in round III fire it again for whoever takes it (Oct 18 rule). That makes it Red's best round III target at any table with Red.
- Not simple on screen: "change the colors as you like" needs a full redistribution picker.
- Fix: "When recruited: turn all of one color you hold into another color", and either "not in round III" or a price of 6. One-to-one is easy to read and still lets you snipe one contest or dodge a theft.

**Tiebreaker (new, 5): KEEP, price 6.**
- The pitch undercounts the swing. Each tie moves you from half to the whole prize and takes the tied rival's half, so at 2p it's a 2/4/6 swing per tie between the only two players. A three-way tie gives you the full 6 instead of 3.
- It's strongest at 2p (ties are common, two contests) and weaker at 4p. At 5 it's cheap for 2p.
- It works *with* the core tension. You hold to match, not beat, so you can spend one more. Rivals answer by holding one more, a counter they can see.
- It's the cleanest new card here and isn't a near-copy of anything live (I found no contest-rule card).
- Engine notes: two copies (Doppelganger) split as usual. It must also cover a tied "most members" contest like purple's, not only resource contests. The round-end card has to show it, or the stolen half reads as a bug.

**Spendthrift (new, 6): CUT (or fix hard).**
- It's a knife edge. You score only if your pile is exactly empty after your last buy, which means landing on exactly 1, 5 or 10, or a member's exact cost. Miss by 1 and you get nothing. That's arithmetic every turn, not "interesting but not complicated".
- It's out of your control. It's checked at round end, *after* everyone else's turns and after Straggler. Any rival gift ("+1 to everyone else" from Silver, Kickback's "+1 gold to a rival") or a Straggler move that gains you a resource kills it silently. You can't defend against that, and it reads as a bug.
- It's anti-interactive. Magenta and every contest lose you as a target or a rival, so you've opted out of the board.
- It's live in round I (exact-change buy pays +3 at once, so 4 followers on 6) and in round III, where packs-only players already empty out. So it nudges the buy-followers-only rate up, which the pitch admits.
- If it stays: "Round end: if you hold 2 or fewer resources, +2 followers", price 5. Softer edge, smaller number.

**Front-runner 6 (reprice): KEEP the price. The text is still win-more.** The price fixes "too cheap", not the shape. The leader keeps first pick of the round III spaces and takes the trailer's catch-up away. One line for Cory (his card, so no added limits from us): at 6 it's still the leader's card.

**Freelancer 7 (reprice): KEEP.** It changes nothing on Classic tables (already all-any). It's fine elsewhere. If Launderer survives, the two overlap. One of them should go, and Freelancer is the one Cory has liked.

**Squatter 7, Extra Hand 9, Straggler 5: KEEP.** These are Cory's numbers. Option 7 ("not a round III space") makes Squatter a rounds I–II card. That's fine as a later lever, not now.

**Deal 5 of 8: KEEP the idea, but say what's lost.** Cory's three favorites (Squatter, Straggler, Extra Hand) each miss about 37% of games. At 2p the guests are the only thing that varies, which is the real case for this rule. If Spendthrift is cut, the pool is 7 with 5 dealt, which barely rotates. Either "keep 6, all in" or wait until Cory designs his two open slots.

**Buy twice to Blue: AGREE.** As a guest it'd sit at every 2p Gold+Red table and copy Blue's round II space.

## Must not get through
Launderer as written. "Change the colors as you like" on the leader's last buy of round III turns it into a contest sweep with perfect information.
