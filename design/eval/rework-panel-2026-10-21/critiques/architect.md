# Architect: the whole table (rework panel, 2026-10-21)

Seat: the map, not one card. Read: critic-brief, strategy-read, brief, deck-visibility, member-shape (part), all 11 open pitches, the guest critiques (for Tiebreaker and Launderer), decisions.md on ties and Recluse. No sims. Every card text below is a Claude fill-in from the pitches, not approved.

## 1. Territories claimed, and the collision calls

Territories in the ten action pitches:
- Gold: member money + doubling (gold-anchor A); multiply any pile (gold-anchor B, gold-outsider B); a shared pot (gold-outsider A).
- Purple: your future now, turns and round-start income (purple-anchor A); push rivals' turns (purple-anchor B); contests pay now and again (purple-outsider 1); pay for placing late (purple-outsider 2).
- Silver: give, then match the richest rival, plus giving members away (silver-anchor A); a pot (silver-anchor B); call contests early (silver-outsider A); lift then tie the top (silver-outsider B).
- Yellow: members bring their colors (yellow-anchor A); raise a color to your largest (yellow-anchor B); recolor piles, Pour, Fade (yellow-outsider A); a contest pays double (yellow-outsider B).
- White: take a contest's prize early (white-anchor A); followers on members (white-anchor B); catch-up on the follower gap (white-outsider A); tie the leader (white-outsider B).

Six of the twenty concepts touch contests as their verb (purple-outsider 1, silver-outsider A and B, yellow-outsider B, white-anchor A, white-outsider B). That's the herd the brief warned of.

**Contest timing.** One owner: **White**, in the white-anchor A form ("take now the prize of a contest you lead; it won't score at round end").
- Purple's The Count is cut outright: it pays now *and* again, so it adds up to about 20 followers a game at 4 players through contests. That's contests becoming the meal.
- Silver-outsider A is the same rule as White's. Silver has a kernel Cory loves (Rising Tide, his own notes); White has none ("needs a rework he can't pin down"). The color with nothing to keep takes the new rule.
- Is it right at all? Yes, in one color, because the prize pool stays fixed: no new followers, the contest's share of the score doesn't move. What it does is let a leader cash the prize and *spend the stack on members the same round*. It's a release valve on holding, which pushes toward "members are where followers come from", not away. Contests get more attention, not more weight. Two contest colors would be too many; one is seasoning with a new spice.
- Spotlight (prizes pay double) and every "tie the leader" space are cut for the same reason as The Count: they change the size of contests, not their timing.

**The pot.** At most one; it belongs to Gold as the alternative (gold-outsider A, the Jackpot). It's the only idea that gives a Classic table a shared, visible thing every Gold placement feeds or starves, and Gold is on every Classic table. Silver's pot (silver-anchor B) is dropped: its own author rated it 35% and the Silver player's edge is thin. Neither pot is in the recommended map: a new board object plus Cory's Lottery Winner dislike.

**Ties.** Cory's Oct 16 call: "competitions drop tiebreakers; ties all score". Live rule: a tie pays each half. The guest Tiebreaker already went to him. So no color gets a tie card: cut Miser (gold A), Notary (gold B), Diplomat (yellow A), Referee (silver-outsider), the three Tiebreakers (purple-outsider, yellow-outsider, white-outsider) and white-anchor's round I "Even odds". Wherever one was the cheap body, it becomes "Worth 2".

**Round-start effects now.** **Purple** (Fast Forward, Wake Up, Alarm Clock). It fits "your future, now" next to Crunch Time, and White no longer needs it. Watch Fast Forward with flat-income members from other decks (Banker +3 fired twice).

**Doubling vs pouring.** **Gold** doubles, widened to "Double one of your colors. +1 gold." Yellow loses Pour (yellow-outsider A) and Even Out (yellow-anchor B); both are "make one stack big in round III", the same move as Gold's. Debutante's "double your silver" (silver-outsider) goes too. If Cory keeps Double Down narrow ("double your gold"), Pour is free again for Yellow's alternative.

**Follower-gap catch-up.** **No color owns it.** The base game already has two catch-ups (fewest followers pick first; the trailer takes the best round III anchor), and Purple's Crunch Time is a third. White-outsider A's burst, Last Laugh ("your follower buy this turn gives that many again, up to how many you trail"), and Nest Egg (white spent on followers counts double) both multiply follower packs. That points the color at buying followers, not members. Catch-up stays as one member per deck at most (Late Riser in Purple; Underdog in Gold). Purple's Punch Clock and Silver's Chaperone go.

**Launderer.** Lives only as the guest (it's Cory's idea and already went to him there). Out of gold-outsider's deck; Yellow's Dyer ("When recruited: turn any of your resources into one color") is the same card and goes. Carry the guest skeptic's warning: on the leader's last buy of round III it re-deals a pile across every contest with full sight. That needs a guard before it ships.

**Per-member payouts.** Gold's Tithe ("+1 gold per member you have") and Yellow's Potluck ("each of your members brings you 1 of its color") already coexist live. Once Yellow's identity *is* Potluck, Tithe reads as its sibling. Tithe leaves Gold's board; Half Again ("gain half your gold") moves up to round II. Treasurer stays (Cory: "really good").

**Recluse** (todo: "Recluse moves out of Blue"). Recommend **Silver**, in Chaperone's slot: Regift gives your members to rivals, which shrinks your cult and grows theirs, and Recluse pays +2 per rival with more members. One card that sings with the color's verb, and it still stands alone.

## 2. The map

| Color | Recommended | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Gold | Money for members, and it doubles (gold-anchor A), with round III widened from gold-anchor B, Tithe swapped for Half Again, Miser as a plain body | The Jackpot (gold-outsider A), Collect Debts kept as Classic's only pile check |
| Purple | Your future, now (purple-anchor A): Crunch Time, Head Start, Fast Forward, Catch Up as Cory's own 1/2/3 | Own the order (purple-anchor B): Hold Up pushes a rival's next turn last |
| Silver | The Tide (silver-anchor A): give first, then gain off the richest rival; Regift; High Tide. Plus Recluse; minus Warm Welcome | Call contests early (silver-outsider A), only if White takes its alternative |
| Yellow | Your members bring their colors (yellow-anchor A): Potluck, Feast, Bumper Crop. Diplomat cut | Dye without Pour or Dyer (yellow-outsider A): Fade, Trade Seeds' growing swap, Top Up |
| White | Take the prize early (white-anchor A), Even odds swapped for "+1 white per contest you lead" | Catch-up (white-outsider A), with Last Laugh aimed off follower packs |

Why each assignment beats the others:
- **Gold.** It's on every Classic table, so it must be the plainest and the most Cory's: his "money for buying members" and his Double Down, now pulling gold *into* members (Oct 20). Widening fixes the flat spot where the anchor went to the gold leader by default. The Jackpot interacts better but adds a board object and a windfall feel he's turned down.
- **Purple.** It keeps both of his Purple calls (Crunch Time; Catch Up "1, 2 or 3 for your first, second or third minion") and gives "round-start effects now" a home on the color that's already about *when*. The Count is the only rival concept with a new idea, and it inflates contests.
- **Silver.** It builds the burst from the one Silver card he loves ("Rising Tide… I like this one a lot") and from three of his own notes (everyone holds silver, get rid of members, Patron Saint). The counterweight is the held-silver contest your gifts feed. Calling contests is the better idea in the abstract, but it's White's to carry.
- **Yellow.** One mechanic answers all four of his Yellow notes: it wants members, it plays the same at 2 and 4 players (it counts members, not table colors), Top Up scales, and Chefs' bodies pay. Dye's best move, Pour, collides with Gold, and its conversion overlaps Launderer and Freelancer.
- **White.** It's the one concept that works on the hold-or-spend tension itself, adds no followers to the game, and turns a held stack into member buys. White has nothing to keep, so it can take the new rule. **Close call:** his own Oct 16 note says White "likes fewer followers as a catch-up", which is the alternative. I put it second because its burst multiplies follower packs and stacks a fourth catch-up on the snake.

The five verbs: Gold *grows and spends money*, Purple *moves when*, Silver *gives*, Yellow *reads your cult*, White *settles contests*. Only White leans on contests. None takes (Magenta), copies actions (Green), moves members (Red; Regift is a gift, not a swap), or changes the row or the number of buys (Blue).

## 3. Two tables

**2 players, Classic (Gold + Red).** Round III anchors: Double Down widened and Recruit a Member ("free member up to 10").
- The Red player holds about 5 to 8 red, so doubling it is +5 to +8 plus the red contest: they want Gold's anchor. The Gold player wants a free 8- or 9-cost Gold capstone: they want Red's. **Both anchors are wanted by both seats**, so the trailer's first pick is a real choice. Unwidened, Double Down would be the gold leader's by default.
- Gold's ring I and II spaces in round III: Golden Years 4, Kickback 3, Splurge (both players hold gold at 2 players), Half Again 2 to 6. None dead.
- **Buy-followers-only:** Splurge only pays on members, and Half Again and Double Down only pay a holder, so the pack buyer gets the +1s. **One line to watch:** hold all game for the contests, double in round III, then buy 10-packs. The 10 cap and about 5 round III buys limit it. If bots show it, the lever is "double (at most +10)". Not proposed by default.

**4 players: Gold, Purple, Silver, Yellow.** Round III anchors: Double Down, Crunch Time, High Tide, Bumper Crop.
- Each is wanted beyond its specialist. Double Down: any stack. High Tide: any rival of a silver hoarder (the hoard is the prize). Bumper Crop: any cult of 5 or more members. Crunch Time: whoever picks first.
- **Biggest risk on this table:** with no Recruit a Member or Spree, Crunch Time lets the trailer take 3 of the 4 anchors (Crunch Time plus two more before anyone answers), and the other three players split one. That's a huge catch-up swing on a table that already gives the trailer first pick. Flag for Cory, and play it before tuning it.
- **Bumper Crop** ("each of your members brings you 2 of its color") counts bodies. A worth-5 member is +10 on its own, so a big-body cult runs 15 to 20. Count member cards, or cap the space at 10 (the member ceiling).
- **Buy-followers-only:** Silver's "everyone else" gifts hand the pack buyer free resources: +1 a round from Toast, +2 from Rising Tide, +3 from High Tide. That's about 6 a game, mild. Warm Welcome ("gain 1 follower × round") is followers straight off a space, which the pack buyer loves, so I cut it. Splurge, Potluck, Feast, Fast Forward and Bumper Crop all pay for members. Net: the table leans toward members. I'd expect pack-only below 25% (an expectation, not a measurement).

**Pairs to watch:**
- White plus Gold: double a stack, cash its lead, spend it. That's two placements and fair.
- Purple plus Yellow: Fast Forward fires Orchard ("at round start, members bring their colors"), which is Potluck again.
- Silver plus White: aimed gifts make or break leads right before a cash. That's good interaction.

## 4. Blue members

**Deck A, "every extra buy pays".** It's built on the settled actions (Buy twice, Spree) and on Cory's "you can always buy twice" (Shopaholic), and it doesn't depend on which colors show up in the row. Fixes:
- Bulk Buyer becomes "+1 follower per *member* bought after your first". As written it pays on follower packs, which feeds the pack buyer.
- Doorbuster (a buy at round start, not on your turn) gives way to B's Window Dresser ("Recruit: send up to 3 shop members to the bottom", worth 2).
- Insider ("only you may buy it") echoes Claim (❌). Show it to Cory as his peek-recruit fix, with that echo named.
- Recluse leaves for Silver. A takes Window Dresser or "Last Call" in that slot.

**Contest: held blue,** flagged as a recommendation. Every other reworked color moved to held resources. "Most blue members" pays the shopper twice for one buy and rides on what the row shows. Blue earns only about 7 to 8 blue a game, so colored parts of 1 to 2 make every Blue buy a contest cost: the shopper's dilemma.

No Blue member should touch contests: that's White's.
