## 0. Five things that decide most verdicts tonight

**1. He told us which colors he's "not married to": Silver, Gold, White. Not Purple, not Yellow.**
Purple: "#80 Purple kernel is there, but needs rework"; Crunch Time "keep as is". Yellow is variety/sets, "basically doesn't want members", "weak at low player counts". So a ground-up identity swap is welcome for Silver, Gold and White and is a headwind for Purple and Yellow. That favors purple-anchor over The Count, and yellow-anchor over Dye and Spotlight.

**2. Changes to how contests score: the hardest no on the board.**
Ledger, 2026-10-15: "Prizes reward what each sphere builds (own scoring shapes) · ❌ · 'Scoring defaults to most of your resource held: it makes you weigh holding against spending'." Decisions, 2026-10-16: "(Cory) Competitions drop tiebreakers; ties all score. Why: frees words and adds game theory." The later #112 call made a tie "each get half". Design principles: "Ring contests are seasoning, not the meal." Ledger 2026-10-09: "too much redesign" (core-redesign 0✅ 1❌).
Pitches that change when or how a contest pays:
- purple-outsider 1 (The Count: contests pay now and again);
- silver-outsider A (score a lead now, contest over);
- white-anchor A (take the prize early);
- yellow-outsider B (a contest pays double).

Members and spaces that bend the payout:
- tie cards: Miser, Notary, Referee, Diplomat, three Tiebreakers, Even odds;
- payout benders: Runner-up, Front Row, Chameleon, Arbiter, Spoilsport, Head Count's "per contest you lead".

Predict no on the colors built on this, and no or "not now" on most of the cards. The three-seat herd on contest timing came from the brief stressing contests, not from him.

**3. One skeleton stamped on seven decks reads as agent-forged.**
Every deck is: a "Worth N" body, a "rivals can't X" defense worth 2, a "do the round II space on arrival, worth 2" card, a "+1 when you take this color's space" drip, a "+1 follower per 3 X at game end" scorer, and a "Once per game: do the round III space" capstone. The same skeleton shows up in purple, silver, white, yellow and gold. That's member-shape.md, Claude's audit, not his taste (rule 20). Ledger: agent-forged 0✅ 5❌, "mostly sucked" (Oct 9), and "members that are slight variations of each other" (Oct 15).
Seen side by side, he will read it as one deck in seven colors. Each deck should keep at most two template cards, and the rest should be his cards or cards only that color could have.

**4. Defense cards against a verb that doesn't harm.**
He asked for Loyalists and Bodyguard because Red and Magenta take things. Colorfast (vs Fade), Holdout and Hecklers (vs early calls), Runaway (vs ties), Untouchable, Night Watch (vs Hold Up), Sanctuary and Scarecrow defend against Claude's own new verbs. Each is a card that exists to answer another card Claude invented. Expect "why is this here".

**5. Duplicates of things already sent or already rejected.**
- Launderer is Cory's idea and has gone to him as a guest. It reappears in gold-outsider's deck, and as yellow-outsider's Dyer ("When recruited: turn any of your resources into one color"). Cut both.
- Tiebreaker went to him in the guest file. Every other copy is a duplicate.
- Insider (blue) is Claim on a card. Ledger 2026-10-20: "Blue round II claim: only you can recruit a marked shop member · ❌ · 'promote and ransom are just straight up way better'".
- Recluse kept in Blue. His #17: "Recluse not in blue".
