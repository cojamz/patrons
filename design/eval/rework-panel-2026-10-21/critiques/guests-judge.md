## Two facts the pitch gets wrong or misses
- **Packs are 1-for-1 and unlimited** (rulebook line 27: "1 for 1 follower, 5 for 5, or 10 for 10. All three buys are unlimited"). Anyone can land on zero at will by buying 1-packs with the leftovers. The pitch's "hitting zero usually means buying a member at the right cost" is false. This breaks Spendthrift (below).
- **Cory dropped tiebreakers on purpose** (Oct 16: "Competitions drop tiebreakers; ties all score. Why: frees words and adds game theory"). Tiebreaker hands one player back a tiebreak. It's a member, so it's a rule-bender, not a base-rule change, but the pitch never names that it cuts against his own call. He will notice.
- Making the guests colorless at the same totals is not a design pick: it's his own rule ("neutral cards stay neutral", Oct 18) and his open questions #75 / #85 ("Front runner purple not neutral?", "Neutrals got shuffled into other colors?"). Foreman can do it under job 1 as a fix, logged as such, with the two reprices as gut calls (he said "too cheap" / "maybe more expensive", which is Claude's to price per working-with-cory.md).
