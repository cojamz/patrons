Each action file has two concepts (a whole color: actions plus a 10-member deck); each member file has two decks. Your job is to attack them from one seat so only the good survive to Cory. Be specific and blunt; praise only what earns it.

Read first: brief.md, strategy-read.md, member-shape.md, pricing.md, deck-visibility.md, cory-judge.md (rubric v3 at the end), all in design/eval/rework-panel-2026-10-21/, and design/strategy.md. Then read every pitch file.

For every concept (label it like `gold-anchor A`), give:
- a score 1–5 from your seat, one line why;
- the 1–3 specific fixes that would raise it (a card, a number, a wording), or "cut" with why;
- any card that is broken, dead, or a near-copy of a live card in another color (quote it).
Then: your top concept per scope (gold, purple, silver, yellow, white, blue members, magenta members, guests) and the single most important thing the foreman must not let through.

Rules: no sims, no game runs, no code. Write only your own file, design/eval/rework-panel-2026-10-21/critiques/<your-seat>.md. Return at most 30 lines: your top pick per scope with its score, and your blocking objections.
