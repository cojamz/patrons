2. Help Yourself's panel and short face say "Steal 1, +1 follower", which reads as a follower from the bank; since Cory's Oct 22 change it steals the follower from the same rival. Fix (Claude): "Steal 1 and 1 follower".
3. Redistribute's panel still says "already holding a minion"; Cory dropped "already" (it counts the minion you just placed). Fix (Claude): "Steal 1 per magenta action with a minion".
4. Exchange's rule says "2 into 4 in round II" (exactly); its hover and panel say "up to 2 resources". The game lets you stop early, so the hover is right and the rule undersells it. Call for Cory: rule to "Turn up to 1 resource… up to 2… up to 3 into 2 followers each"? Also its +1 white arrives first (you can turn it in) though the rule prints it last.

## Hidden: the board never shows a number or an effect
