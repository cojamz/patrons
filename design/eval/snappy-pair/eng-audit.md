# Engineering audit: motion, render, input (2026-10-26, cut short by time limit)

Read-only. Code read: motion.js, director.js, flight.js, stage.js, trail.js, hoverIntent.js, hoverDelay.js, previewWorker.js, seatMemo.js, GameScreen.jsx (useTable, onPick, flight effect), Wheel.jsx (spin), HoverCards.jsx, session/local.js, plus the perf docs. No probes were run, so no new timings. Everything below comes from reading the code. Not covered: RoundEnd.jsx, ShopCards beyond line 344, lamp.css, the e2e probes beyond snappy.mjs's header.

## 1. How a move becomes pixels (short map)
1. **Engine.** `session.submit` (or a bot's `setTimeout` in local.js:153-169) runs `step` → `applyMove`, which `structuredClone`s the whole state (core.js:101). For bots, `chooseBotMove` runs first, on the main thread: about 140ms at 4x for Normal, up to the 250ms wall cap for Hard (thinker.js:19). The emit runs every subscriber synchronously in that same task.
2. **Session → useTable** (GameScreen.jsx:278). The state is queued and `pump()` runs synchronously in the bot's task. pump calls `foldBuys`, `planChain` (a pure plan) and `show()`. `beforeShow` may call `snapshotShop`/`snapshotChips` (layout reads plus `outerHTML`). Then `startTransition(setTable)`.
3. **React.** The transition renders the **end state** (`shown` = physical). `interaction` (pending, current, order) stays on the old state (`tableView`, order.js:34).
4. **Commit → useLayoutEffect** (GameScreen.jsx:886). It runs `clearFlights` (a querySelectorAll), `snapshotMinions` and `playChain` (director.js:924). playChain does a read pass (querySelector plus getBoundingClientRect on the freshly committed DOM, which forces layout inside the commit), puts holds on every stack's number (`data-hold`), then schedules atoms on the one rAF clock (director.js:615).
5. **Atoms.** Each one makes `document.body.appendChild` ghosts plus 2-4 `Element.animate` calls (flyPath, flight.js:490ff). Landings come from each animation's `finish` event, which is main-thread delivered. Counts roll in a **separate rAF loop per stack** (holdStack, flight.js:414), and Tick runs its own.
6. **End.** `endChain` (director.js:653) → `stageRelease()` (hold) → waits until `!motionPending() && !stageBusy()`. Only then does `finishPresentation` set `interaction = shown`. That is when your options light up, and when the next queued state is allowed to show.

**Main-thread work during an animation:** bot thinking and engine steps (step 1, independent of the table's pace, so they land mid-chain), the React transition render of the next step, layout effects with forced layouts (step 4), lazy `where()` and `shown()` rect reads inside rAF atoms (tug, takeTag, linger, stageOut and pulse places not measured up front: director.js:695, 1253, 1271, 1308), several rAF loops, `finish` callbacks, non-composited keyframes (filter, box-shadow), and the preview worker's results arriving.

**What blocks input:** any task above (a bot think is the longest predictable one). The bigger issue is by design: **clicks during a presentation are dropped** (item S1).

## 2. Quick wins (under an hour each)
- **Q1. Hover closes on every rival step.** HoverCards.jsx:219: `useEffect(() => { if (c && !c.pinned) hover.close(); }, [state.moves])`. During rival turns, a card you are reading closes at every bot step. It only reopens after the pointer moves plus the 200ms delay. This is likely one of the "laggy or flickery hover" reports that probes miss, because probes hover on a quiet table. Fix: close only when the hovered target is gone or changed (its el disconnected, the space taken, the card left the shop). Otherwise keep the card and let it re-render. About 30 min. Low risk.
- **Q2. Pulses animate `filter: brightness`.** flight.js:557. `filter` is not composited, so every pulse repaints the space or member each frame on the main thread, mid-chain. The press ring (flight.js:543ff), the card ring (director.js:1100) and the chip glow all animate `box-shadow`. Fix: use opacity on a pre-painted glow pseudo-layer, or scale only. About 45 min. Low risk (looks the same).
- **Q3. Rect reads inside rAF atoms.** `tug` (flight.js `getBoundingClientRect` on both ends), `takeTag` (director.js:1255), `linger` (1273), `stageOut` (1311, 1337) and the lazy `where()` in pulse, hop and point all read layout after ghosts were appended in the same frame. That forces a style and layout pass per atom. Fix: resolve every `where()` place and rect in playChain's read pass (it already has `found`/`rects` maps, so just prefill them for the pulse, hop, tug, stage and count places). About 45 min. Low risk.
- **Q4. FollowerRates tips remount every move.** ShopCards.jsx:344: `<Tip key={state.moves}>`. All three tips unmount and remount on every step (already noted in lag-2026-10-21.md, never fixed). Fix: close them through an effect on moves, with a stable key. About 15 min.
- **Q5. One rAF loop per rolling stack.** holdStack (flight.js:414) runs its own loop next to the chain clock and Tick. Fix: drive rolls from the director's `tick` (one rAF), registering a roll callback. About 1h. Low-medium risk.
- **Q6. The skip can eat the click that triggered it.** installChainSkip (director.js:1348) skips on capture `pointerdown`. finishPresentation's `setTable` is then a default-lane render. The following `click` reaches `onPick` with the old closure, where `interactionState !== physicalState` (GameScreen.jsx:1069), and is silently dropped if React hasn't re-rendered by then. That is likely at 4x CPU. Fix: wrap the finishPresentation update in `flushSync` when it comes from a skip, or route the click through S1's buffer. About 30 min.

## 3. Structural items (top 3, ranked by how much a player feels them)

### S1. Your options wait for the whole chain to drain, and clicks meanwhile are dropped (biggest)
- **Evidence.** `interaction` only advances in `finishPresentation` (GameScreen.jsx:225-232), which runs on `onChainEnd`. `endChain` (director.js:653-676) waits for every tracked animation (settles, bumps, count rolls) **and** for the stage to empty: `stageRelease()` holds `MO.stageHold`, then the stage pieces fly home over `MO.stageOut`. At Relaxed that is 180×3.75 ≈ 675ms of hold plus 240×3.75 = 900ms of return, after the last piece already landed. Your pending options come from `interaction` (order.js:34), so spaces and buys stay unlit. `onPick`, `onSeatPick` and pay picks return early while `interactionState !== physicalState` (GameScreen.jsx:1069, 1288, 1316). The `queuedClick` buffer is only reached after that check. So after your own placement, or the rival step that hands you the turn, you wait about 1.5s or more (Relaxed) or about 0.7s (Steady) beyond the visible cause and effect. A click in that window either skips the chain (on a non-option target) or is lost. This breaks "motion never makes the player wait to act". The designer would feel it as lag, and no gate measures it.
- **Redesign.**
  1. Split "presentation done" from "decision available". Advance `interaction` at the chain's **last landing**, i.e. `plan.handoff`/total. Stage return flights, settles and count rolls become decoration that never gates input (stageBusy and motionPending stay for pacing the *next rival* step only).
  2. Buffer input. While `interaction !== physical` and the click targets an option legal in `physical.pending`, record it, skip the chain, and replay it on the next commit. This reuses `queuedClick`, moved ahead of the guard.
  3. Add a gate (see §4 G1).
- **Cost.** About 4-6h. Risk medium: the pacing tests (pacing.test.js H2/H3, motion.mjs m4) pin today's timing and need re-intent, not weakening.

### S2. Bots and engine steps run on the main thread, untimed against the table
- **Evidence.** local.js:153-169: `chooseBotMove` plus `step` (a structuredClone of the full state, core.js:101) plus the synchronous emit → pump → beforeShow layout reads, all in one timer task. It fires `BOT.think` (120-400ms) after the previous state, so it lands **during** that state's chain and during your hovering. Normal is about 140ms at 4x (lag-2026-10-21.md), and Hard is capped at 250ms of *wall* time (thinker.js:19), which on a slow laptop is one long task of 250ms or more. While it runs, keydown spin starts, hover opens, rAF chain ticks and holdStack rolls all stall. WAAPI transforms keep moving, but the next atom launches late. Online catch-up has the same shape: `trail.feed` → `bridge` replays up to 400 moves with `applyMove` during render (trail.js:155-168, called from GameScreen.jsx:775 inside useMemo).
- **Redesign.**
  1. Run bots (choose plus step) in a Web Worker. The engine is pure JSON, and previewWorker.js already proves the pattern. The session posts the state and gets back `{ move, state }`.
  2. Make the bot clock *pull*: it thinks for the next step only while the table is idle or at chain handoff (`requestIdleCallback`-gated), never mid-beat.
  3. Move `bridge` to the worker too, or do it in idle time.
- **Cost.** About 5-7h (the worker, determinism tests for seeded bots, fallback). Risk medium: the RNG and `rand` sequence must stay identical, and fuzz and records tests catch drift.

### S3. Many clocks and many DOM writers mid-chain: no single animation scheduler or compositor discipline
- **Evidence.** The chain rAF clock (director.js:615) and the per-stack holdStack rAF (flight.js:414) run alongside Tick's rAF and many `setTimeout`s: the chosen-class removal (director.js:1021), the staged chip hand-off (1154), roll releases (1005), `linger`/`takeTag` lifetimes, and the ghost safety timers in flyPath and ghostFly. On top of that, every landing waits on the `finish` event (main-thread delivered), every ghost is a fresh `body.appendChild` with innerHTML SVG (flyPath: 1 + trail nodes each, so a 12-coin stream is about 12 outer and 12 inner nodes with up to 4 animations each), and the box-shadow and filter keyframes paint (Q2). When a rival's next state commits mid-chain (S2), its transition commit plus layout effects land between ticks, and forced layouts in the commit (playChain's read pass after the commit, snapshotMinions, layColumn's offsetHeight reads at GameScreen.jsx:650-694) make long frames exactly while pieces are in the air.
- **Redesign.**
  1. One scheduler. The director's clock owns all show timing (rolls, removals, lifetimes), and timeouts become clock items, so a skip and a speed change see everything.
  2. A ghost pool. A pre-created fixed `ghost-layer` container with `contain: strict` holds recycled piece nodes (no body appends, no innerHTML parse per piece). Glyph SVGs come from a `<use href>` sprite.
  3. Composite-only keyframes (transform and opacity), as a lint rule in render-budget.test.jsx, like the existing svg-transform rule.
  4. `content-visibility`/`contain: layout paint` on seats, shop and wheel regions, so a ghost append or a seat change doesn't dirty the whole page.
  5. Defer the next queued state's commit to the chain's handoff boundary (it already waits) **and** run its layout reads in the same read pass, never between atom frames.
- **Cost.** About 8-10h in stages: Q2, Q3 and Q5 are its first slices. Risk medium-high. Motion tests (motion.mjs, filmstrip) are the safety net.

(Further structural items were not reached: for example, the end state is rendered under masks rather than the displayed state being decoupled from the engine state, and planChain is recomputed during render at GameScreen.jsx:1226.)

## 4. Measurement gaps and the gate that would catch each
- **G1. Time to "you can act".** No gate measures the time from the visible last landing (or the rival's last step) to your options lit and clickable. The eaten clicks in S1/Q6 are invisible to snappy s1-s3, which act on a quiet, settled table. Gate: at Steady and Relaxed, after your placement and after a rival hands you the turn, time from the last `patrons:land` to `data-presentation-moves === data-moves` with a lit `[data-option]`, with a 150ms bar. Also click a buy option 100ms after the last landing and assert it was applied.
- **G2. Hover during rival turns.** The snappy s2 and info gates hover on an idle table. Gate: hold the pointer on a shop card and a seat through 3 bot steps, and assert the card stays open (catches Q1) and that `long-animation-frame` during the hold stays ≤50ms p95.
- **G3. Long animation frames during chains**, recorded with LoAF over a whole rival turn at 4x CPU and DPR 2 (spin-busy has `--dpr=2`, but chains aren't judged). Bar: frame p95 ≤ 33ms and no frame over 100ms while a chain is in the air. Read the LoAF `scripts` to blame bot think and forced layout.
- **G4. Hard bots.** No probe runs Hard bots, whose think is the worst long task. Run G3 with `level=hard`.
- **G5. Variance, not means.** Report each probe's p95 and max frame gap per beat, not only "landed within 400ms".
