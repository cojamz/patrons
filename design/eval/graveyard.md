# Graveyard
Quadrants and ideas cut from the pool. One line each on why.
- vigil (Ivory, sealing members): killed in the b2 screen. A second state on member cards that mostly repeats what followers already do. Its score is an uncontested personal count, and its release half fights its sealing half.
- caravan (Saffron, presence/outposts): killed after the b2 tables. Flat at 3 tables, its scoring went uncontested because nobody else makes outposts, and its income was too low for its members.
- auction-house (Ivory, market): killed after the b2 tables. Haul tied every round, its stall emptied by round II, and its "any stall" buys couldn't be paid for. A market sphere is still a good idea for a fresh attempt.
