# Magenta member prices from sims (Oct 21)

Instrument: Normal bots, paired games. Each game is played twice from the start of round II, once as is and once with one seat forced to buy the card at a set price. The fair price is where that seat's lead over the best rival comes out even. 300 games per price, prices tried from 4 below to 4 above today's, at 2, 3 and 4 players (table: Magenta, Red, Green, Blue). The 90% range on each fair price is about ±1.5 to ±3, so a difference of 1 is noise.

Notes
- Bodyguard is the clear outlier: the seat that owns it ends about 19 followers further ahead in a 4-player game. In a 30-game check, thefts from its owner after round II fell from about 11 to 4 resources, and Poach and Shake Down can't take its members. Bots steal a lot (about 53 resources a game across the table), so humans may steal less, but the direction is clear.
- Double Agent reads weak at 3 and 4 players. Bots pick the member to take by a rough score, and in round II rivals own few strong members, so this may be the bots, not the card.
- Extortionist and Sticky Fingers read far below today's 10 and 8.
- Player count moves several cards by 3 or more (Grifter, Double Agent, Cutpurse, Fence): those are noisy or truly table-size cards; suggested prices lean on 3 and 4 players.
- Colored part: the suggested totals keep each card's magenta/any split in today's proportion. Worth knowing: a Magenta player earns about 5 magenta a game, and magenta paid for members is magenta not held for the contest.
