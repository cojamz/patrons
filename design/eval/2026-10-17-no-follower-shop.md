# No follower shop: paired Normal-bot experiment

2026-10-17. Instrument: four Normal bots, seeds 1–12, paired with/without follower-shop choices on two tables; 48 games total. Current working-tree engine loaded once at run start (HEAD `8097779a`, plus pending batch changes). No live rule or UI option was added.

Classic: gold/red/blue/green. Experimental: silver/thieves/yellow/purple. Seeded decision randomness is reset identically for each paired game. In the no-shop arm, only `tier:*` options are removed from the bot decision input; the selected move is applied to the unchanged authoritative engine. All other choices remain available. One worker, low priority; no browser/build.

## Reading and limits

In this instrument, removing follower purchases shifted spending toward more member cards and increased passing. Both tables still completed. These small bot samples do not establish human pacing, enjoyment, strategic diversity or a balance recommendation. The Normal evaluator still values future resources under the ordinary economy, so filtering current follower choices does not retrain its strategy for the changed economy. Same-seed arms diverge after decisions, so they share starting conditions rather than identical later randomness. “No affordable member” is an option-list observation, not a stalemate or proof that passing was forced.
