Mid laptop = Chromium at 1270x700 with 4x CPU throttling. Builds: `npx vite build` of lane11, served by `vite preview`.

## Read this first: the box
The whole-game run before (before-lag.md) ran at load 3.0 → 3.7; the run after (after-lag.md) at load 10 → 9.9 on a 4-CPU
box (another lane's tests). The after run is therefore not comparable and reads worse across the board (frames of 650ms
spent in render+layout with no script, long tasks of 34s total vs 19s). Do not use after-lag.md as a verdict; re-run it
on a quiet box (`node scripts/v4-perf.mjs <url> after-lag --cpu=4`). The numbers below are the controlled ones: the same
saved game, one action at a time, before/after builds measured back to back.

## What was found and not fixed (scope moved to React rendering only mid-pass)
- The round-II commit itself: a ~185-207ms uninterruptible React commit at 4x with three forced layouts inside it (the
  deal's deck read in MemberDeck useDeal: 134ms, the chain's deck read in playChain, minions home). The first is the page's
  real layout; the others are ~25-60ms of thrash from useDeal adding ghosts before playChain reads.
- The spin's first frame (~170ms at 4x) is mostly the frame's own render and commit; the spin is backdated to the key, so
  most of the turn is already done by the first painted frame (reads as a quick snap rather than a 300ms turn).
- A Chrome hit test (114ms over a spin) from synthetic mouse moves while layers move under a resting pointer.
- Bots think on the main thread (chooseNormalMove ~140ms at 4x during the round-start chain).
- Hover: filmed frame by frame (hover-open-frames.jpg, hover-swap-frames.jpg): the card opens whole in one frame; on a
  swap the panel's hover tint leaves ~100ms before the card swaps (the 450ms gutter grace). That mismatch is the likeliest
  "tiny bit wonky"; left as is (behaviour).
- FollowerRates remounts its three tips every move (`key={state.moves}`, intended to close them); SinceStrip and TurnCard
  re-render on every state.
