## After the game

**Ava:** I won 102 to 80 by hoarding purple, since Pocket Watch plus Sleep In, Hold Up and Bouncer kept turning skips into purple, and Clockmaker compounded it at each round start. The deciding stretch was round III: the back-to-back turns with Greatest Hits copying Exchange and Allowance, then Hot Streak for +6, took me from 47 to 72 in one go and made the purple and white +10s safe. Last Call felt too strong in round II: +2 followers per minion left, and Echo directly behind it copied it for another +6, so the one-use cost of going last barely bites. Exchange (2 followers per resource) beats buying followers at the same price and gets doubled again by Reprise and Greatest Hits, so copy spaces seem too strong once a player owns a couple of cheap actions. Dee's Cover into Reprise into Crowd In loop gave two extra minions and 10 purple, which looked like a bug-adjacent combo. Sleep In and Hold Up are great with Pocket Watch, but skipping rivals with Bouncer felt a bit unfun for the victims. Purple and white were uncontested by round III, so the contests I won gave me 20 followers for almost no fight.

**Ben:** I led after round II, then lost round III by taking Last Call first: I got +8 but sat out while Ava and Dee used the whole wheel, and Cal's Echo copied it for free. The deciding moment was Ava's round III chain of Exchange, Allowance, Hot Streak and Greatest Hits copying Allowance and Exchange, plus Pocket Watch purple from every skip, which bought 10 followers over and over and put her around 80 before I moved again. Dee's Cover into Reprise into Crowd In loop gave her extra minions and extra purple the same way. Cover, Reprise and Greatest Hits felt too strong in round III, and Last Call plus an Echo behind it feels like a trap for the taker. Window Dresser and Trendsetter at 3 followers for 4 were very strong in round I, and Bury and Out of Style piled blue fast with Bargain Bin. Con Men stealing white at the end of round II swung a contest, and Pocket Watch with Sleep In/Hold Up/Bouncer snowballs because purple is both the contest and the currency.

**Cal:** I finished last on 56 because I took Echo on Ben's Last Call for +8 and then sat out the whole wheel, placing my last four minions on leftover scraps while Ava and Dee chained turns. The decisive moment was round III: Ava's Exchange, Allowance, Greatest Hits and Hot Streak turn, plus Dee's Cover into Reprise into Crowd In loop, turned about 10 resources into followers at better than 1:1 several times over. Last Call is too strong: it pays 6 to 8 followers in rounds II and III and a copy by the next seat doubles it, and in round III the cost of going last was hidden because the extra minions made players place everything first. Exchange (2 resources for 4 followers in round II, 3 for 6 in round III) beats buying followers and shop members, and copies of it via Reprise, Cover and Greatest Hits looked broken. The 10-follower round III contests with Pocket Watch purple and Money Changer white made Ava and Dee uncatchable, while Chronicler, Busker and Stunt Double were solid but small, and Raiders never looked usable because paying white costs a lead.

**Dee:** I lost to Ava by 22, finishing second at 80. The deciding moment was round III, where Ava's Greatest Hits copied Allowance and Exchange in one turn and put her about 17 ahead, and she then held both purple and white for +20; my Cover into Reprise into Crowd In loop got me back into second but not past her. Early on, Echo copying Sleep In cost me a skip and then Hold Up plus Bouncer skipped me three more times while feeding Ava's Pocket Watch, which left me 1 follower after round I. Last Call (+2 per minion left) is too strong when taken first and copied by Echo the next seat; the Cover/Reprise/Crowd In loop and Greatest Hits chains also feel too strong, since they cost no extra minion. Money Changer felt weak: buying followers never triggered it. Recluse after I cast out Con Men was a cheap +6, and Pocket Watch plus skip cards snowballed purple in Ava's favor.
