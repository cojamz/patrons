# Expert games and bot batches: balance read (Oct 29, overnight)

Instruments: five games played by four Sonnet-model "expert" players each (practice3, game1 to game4; 4-color random 2.0 tables; build 5f82bb33 plus guests), each player writing a move-by-move reason and a post-game note; Normal bots 600 games and Hard bots 120 games on the build before the 15 guests (so guests there are partly stale). Raw: data/. Expert games are 5 samples, so read them for mechanisms, not rates.

## Where both instruments agree (act on these first)
1. **Exchange (White, round I/II "turn up to N resources into 2 followers each") is the runaway conversion.** All five games named it. Copies (Echo, Reprise, Greatest Hits, Cover) repeat it. Bots: Exchange users win *less* than average (0.46 vs 0.58 uses per seat), so bots misuse it; experts did not.
2. **Copy spaces on big flat gains.** Echo right behind Last Call (+2 followers per minion left), Sleep In or Crowd In is a free double; Greatest Hits and Reprise on Crowd In (+5 purple and a minion) gave game 4's winner 7-8 turns. Bots: Greatest Hits winners use it 0.69 vs 0.11 per seat (Normal), 0.72 vs 0.10 (Hard); Last Call and Crowd In are used about 1.7x as often by winners.
3. **Recruiter (Red artifact) is too strong.** Bots: owners win 39% (Normal, 358 seats) and 43% (Hard, 82), every other artifact 12-27%. Expert game 2 (103 vs 76) and game 3 (Ben, 7 members) built on it. Gather + Walk-In loop it.
4. **Money Changer (White artifact) is weak and confusing.** Bots: 13% / 12%. Experts: 4 of 5 games said it "didn't refund on follower buys"; the card says "turn", which only White's spaces do. Wording problem and a power problem.
5. **Impressionist (Green artifact) is weak:** 17% / 21%; players pick it last.
6. **Last-turn On Recruit steals decide games** (Accomplice cost 2, Con Men 6, Fixer re-firing Accomplice, Poach on Con Men): game 1 and game 2 were decided by a 20-follower swing from one. Bots: Accomplice is bought every time it shows (115% of appearances).
7. **Lie Low** on a first pick makes the leader's whole pile untouchable (named in games 1, 2, 3, 4; also leads to a safe +10).
8. **Buying followers beats most members.** Bots: buy-followers-only seat wins 31% (Normal) and 10% (Hard) vs a 25% fair share; expert Cal: "buying followers always beat 3 to 5 cost one-follower bodies". Small engine members (Busker, Clockmaker, Cutpurse, Front-runner) were called too weak in four games. Members owned at game end: Normal win share is 29% at 8+ members, 15-19% at 2-6.

## Single-instrument signals (lower confidence)
- Pocket Watch (Purple artifact): experts say it snowballs with Sleep In, Hold Up, Bouncer and Last Call skips (games 3, 4, practice); bots only 24-27%, so it needs the skip cards, which experts chain deliberately.
- Bargain Bin + Bury / Out of Style: game 3 winner got 20 followers in round I off Bury on a 13-cost card. Bots: 26%, average.
- Estate Sale / Farewell turning a spent member into resources: "quietly strong".
- Sleep In, Right Away, Gather, Cover: winners use them *less* than losers among bots (weak spaces). Cover was a combo piece for experts (Cover, Reprise, Crowd In).
- Mob and Hot Streak do well when owned (46% and 40% owners win); Shoplifter, Extra Shift, Shopaholic, Extra Hand, Bodyguard owners win 48-61% but are bought only 4-8% of the time they show (small samples, 19-28 owners).
- Walk-In's "you'd get" preview shows how many followers/members the unseen top card is worth (game 2: "+2 followers, +2 members"); a player used it to pick Walk-In. Likely also true in the real game screen.

Claude's educated guesses, none applied. Per Cory's rule: lift weak cards with followers before cutting price; floor of 3 (2 "very rare").

## Functional ideas for Cory's call (not changed, per "don't change functionality")
- Exchange: 1 follower per resource, or "once per round" copies.
- Copy spaces can't copy a space that gives followers, extra minions or extra turns (Dee, game 4).
- Recruiter: +1 red on recruits that aren't follower buys.
- Lie Low: not usable by the leader, or protects only one color.
- Last Call: Echo can't copy it, or it doesn't skip when copied.
- Money Changer: say "buy" if it should count follower buys.

## About the players
Postgame notes name real mistakes (a miscounted purple pile that cost a +10, an extra-turn effect with no minions left, going last for +8 and watching). Some of each game's outcome is play, not balance.
