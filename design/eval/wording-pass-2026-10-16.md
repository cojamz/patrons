# Wording pass — 2026-10-16

Source: `design/play-feedback-2026-10-16-original.txt`, especially items 3, 5, 6, 12, 31 and 57 in the feedback audit. This is a wording correction, with no changes to names, prices, payouts or effects.

## Changed

- **Snap Up:** the hover now explicitly says the recruit is paid, optional, accepts any colors, and is additional to the normal buy. The wheel says “may pay for a member” instead of implying an automatic/free recruit.
- **Night Owl:** “Rounds II–III: after placing your last minion, take 1 extra turn if there’s room. Once per round.” Timing, round restriction and space requirement are explicit. Existing logs explain recruiting it too late to trigger this round.
- **Crescendo / Golden Years:** current-round payout remains the main number; “+2 each new round” replaces the ambiguous “more each round.” Full +2/+4/+6 schedule remains in the hover. A new schedule graphic is not claimed.
- **Trade Seeds:** corrected stale wheel and short labels from “swap then +1 yellow” to the actual existing rule: “+2 yellow, then swap up to 3.” This was a display/rule mismatch.
- **Top Up:** short label now says the fewest-held colors, rather than only missing colors.
- **Roll Call:** short label specifies your placed minions rather than suggesting everyone’s.
- **Your Call:** the wheel and short label now show that each follower can individually become 2 white; previously “or +6 white” in round III implied all-or-nothing.
