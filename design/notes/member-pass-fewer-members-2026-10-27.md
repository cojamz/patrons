# Member pass: which cards push toward many members, Oct 27

Claude's sort of the live 2.0 decks (Red, Green, Magenta, Purple, Blue, White) against Cory's direction: fewer members by game end, more strategy, about a 30% nudge. Nothing here is a call; Cory designs the cards.

## Extortionist first
"Whenever a rival buys 5 or more followers, steal 2 resources from that rival." (4 magenta + 2 any)
- It taxes buying followers, which is the one way to score that doesn't add members. So it pushes the whole table toward more members, the opposite of the direction.
- It's passive: you buy it and wait; nothing about it asks you to plan. Owners won 55% in Hard bot games at the old price (Oct 25), so it's also the strongest card for doing nothing.
- Direction for a rework (Claude's): a counter that bites a rival for piling up members or for a big swing, not for the follower buy.

## Pays for how many members you hold (any kind)
- Patriarch: "Game end: +1 follower per 2 members."
- Host: "+1 red per 2 members" on recruit and each round start.
- Grifter: "steal 1 resource per 2 members you have."
- Heiress: "Once per game: +1 white per member you have."
- Mob: "Game end: +1 follower per red member." and counts as 5 members.
These reward count, not fit. Nudge: pay for a kind of member, or for what your members did.

## Bodies that count as several members
Twins, Crew, Shift Crew ("Worth 2 followers."), Big Family, Panel (3), Loyalists (2), Mob (5), plus Volunteer, Intern, Benchwarmer ("Worth 1 follower."). Cheap filler that inflates your count and feeds every card above. Your Oct 18 rule ("a member worth several followers counts as that many members") is what makes them stack.

## Extra recruits
Plus-One ("recruit again this turn"), Shopaholic ("You can always recruit twice."), Mentor & Protégé ("recruit the cheapest member in the shop"), Proselytizer ("Whenever you recruit, +1 follower"). More bodies per turn, with no plan attached.

## Small reactive +1s nobody builds around
Muse, Doorman, Critic, Stylist, Snitch, Regular, Connoisseur. Each pays a little when something happens. You called this kind weak on Oct 15. First to go if decks shrink.

## Already pointing the right way
Martyr and Undertaker (pay you for a member leaving: fewer members), Tribute Band, Night Owl, Hot Streak, Desperado, Sticky Fingers, Courier, Exporter, Hoarder, Connector. Each is a piece you build turns around. More cards like these, fewer of the kinds above.

## Table check (Cory, Oct 27, 8:48pm Eastern)
"at a table it would be too hard to keep track of and remember what 22 of your members do". Every pitch above is checked against it: a player can hold their whole cult in their head. Bodies (Twins, Crew) add count but nothing to remember; the cards to thin are the ones with abilities.
