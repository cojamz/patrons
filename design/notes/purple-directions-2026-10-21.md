# Purple: two directions (Claude's pitch, Oct 21, nothing approved)

Broad shapes only. Card text and numbers here are placeholders for the idea; the real ones are Cory's.
Every direction keeps the Oct 20 economy: each purple space gives +1 purple, sometimes more, and the contest scores held purple.

## 1. Purple waits [Claude's pick]
Purple is the color of "how long do I dare wait?". Its spaces grow as the round fills, and anyone can grab one early to spoil it.
- Round III anchor: a space that pays by how full the board is, something like "+1 follower per 2 minions on the board". At 4 players round III ends with 20 minions, so the first pick gets almost nothing and the last pick up to 10, the same as a top follower pack but without the buy. Every rival sees it ripen and has to decide when to take it just to deny it.
- Round II: Crunch Time ("Take your next 2 turns right away") moves here, as you once said it should (Oct 15: "a good, interesting trade-off that doesn't need a round III slot"). In a waiting color it's the jump: cash in two ripe spaces before anyone answers.
- Round I: your own Catch Up (1 / 2 / 3 purple for your first / second / third minion), plus spaces with the same early-or-late pull.
- Why: blocking is the heart of the game, and this puts a ticking price on it. It's your Catch Up and the Bandwagon growth you liked, made into a whole color. No other color cares about when in the round you place.
- Touches others: anyone can spoil a ripe space; a copy color copying the ripe space late gets the big number too (worth a rule).
- Risk: big follower gains without a buy help the buy-followers-only player; numbers have to keep the last pick near a top pack, not above it.

## 2. Purple owns turn order
Purple is the color of going first. Crunch Time stays the round III burst, and the rest of the color is about where you sit in the snake.
- Round III: Crunch Time as is. Two anchors before anyone answers, about +4 over one normal pick, plus about 6 denied to each of two rivals.
- Round II: something like "Next round you place first". Taking first seat in round III means first pick of the anchors, and Crunch Time right after.
- Round I: Catch Up 1 / 2 / 3 as above.
- Why: the snake is the only comeback engine, and the kernel you said was there (#80) is timing.
- Weak spot: round II. "Go first next round" changes where you sit, not what you own, so it's below the Promote / Shake Down bar. It also lets the leader take first seat away from the trailer, which cuts against the catch-up order.
