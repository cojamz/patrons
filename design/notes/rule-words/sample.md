# Rule words: ten before and after

No rule changes, only words. Style guide: design/rule-style.md on the branch.

1. Midas
   before: Gains from actions come as gold instead. You can switch it off or on when you buy.
   after:  Spaces give you gold in place of other colors. Switch it on or off during your buy.

2. Help Yourself (Magenta space)
   before: Take 2 resources from rivals in round I, 3 in round II, 4 in round III, split as you like (they arrive as magenta).
   after:  Steal 2 resources in round I, 3 in round II, 4 in round III, from any rivals, as magenta.

3. Crime Wave (Magenta space)
   before: Take 3 resources from rivals, at least 1 from each; any more from whoever you like (they arrive as magenta).
   after:  Steal 3 resources, at least 1 from each rival, as magenta.

4. Dyer
   before: At round end, +1 yellow per color you hold.
   after:  Round end: +1 yellow per color you hold.
   (also fixes its shop tag, which read "Always")

5. Lender
   before: When it joins you: +5 white. Game end: lose 2 followers.
   after:  When recruited: +5 white. Game end: −2 followers.

6. Pied Piper
   before: Recruit 2+ members in one turn: +2 red.
   after:  Whenever you recruit 2 or more members in one turn, +2 red.

7. Hand-pick (Red center space)
   before: Take any face-up member, free.
   after:  Recruit any member in the shop, free.

8. Good Deed (Silver space)
   before: +4 silver, and a rival you choose gets 2 of a color you choose.
   after:  +4 silver. Choose a rival and a color: they gain 2 of it.

9. Toast (Silver space)
   before: +3 silver in round I, +6 in round II, +9 in round III, and everyone else +1 silver.
   after:  +3 silver in round I, +6 in round II, +9 in round III. Each rival +1 silver.

10. Extra turn (glossary hover)
   before: Another turn right after this one: you place one more minion and may buy again. It only happens while the board has a space to spare; with 4 players, round I has none.
   after:  A turn right after this one: place 1 more minion and buy again. Only while a space is open; with 4 players, round I has none.

Prompt, same idea:
   before: Goody Bags: which color? You get 2, everyone else gets 1.
   after:  Goody Bags: choose a color. You +2, each rival +1.
