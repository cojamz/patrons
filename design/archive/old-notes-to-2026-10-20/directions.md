# v4 Directions — three sketches

Today a new player learns about 26 things. Each direction below keeps the soul: pick a spot, gain stuff, buy things that make your next picks better, and guess what the others want.

## 1. Shrines — build the board you fight over
- Pitch: everything you buy goes onto the shared board as a shrine on one action space, so the board becomes yours as you play.
- Loop: place a worker, take what the space gives. Spend resources to put a shrine on a space. Your shrine gives you a bonus there, and anyone else who uses it pays you a toll of 1.
- Kept: 4 gods, 4 resources, Favor, 3 rounds, more workers each round, blocking.
- Cut: shops, power cards, champions, Favor conditions, action tiers. Shrines replace all of them.
- Hook: you see your engine on the table. Everyone can read your plan, and "do I pay their toll or take a worse spot?" is a new choice every turn.
- New player learns: about 8 things.
- Biggest risk: early shrines on the best spaces snowball. We'd need a limit like one shrine per space, or a cheaper way to build over someone else's.

## 2. Patron Hand — your cards are your workers
- Pitch: each worker is a card in your hand, and the card you play decides which gods you're allowed to visit (like Dune Imperium).
- Loop: play a card, put a worker on a space that matches its god, gain the space plus the card's small bonus. Buy new cards to add to your deck. Reshuffle each round.
- Kept: 4 gods, resources, Favor, blocking, power cards (they become the deck you buy from).
- Cut: shops, champions, Favor conditions, tiers, worker counts. Your hand size sets your workers.
- Hook: two choices fused into one, which spot and which card. You can count what an opponent has left and guess their move.
- New player learns: about 10 things.
- Biggest risk: shuffling brings back the luck Cory wants gone, and deckbuilding is a second game on top. Longer turns too.

## 3. Piles & Pledges — greed on a timer
- Pitch: unused spaces pile up resources every round (like Agricola), and you spend resources on public pledges worth Favor.
- Loop: place a worker and take the whole pile. Unused spaces grow. Spend resources to finish a pledge from a shared row (like Lords of Waterdeep quests). Each pledge also gives you a permanent perk.
- Kept: 4 gods, resources, Favor, blocking, round-by-round build-up.
- Cut: shops, champions, power cards (they become pledge perks), Favor conditions, steal/repeat effects.
- Hook: a growing pile is a visible trap. "Will they take it before me?" gets louder every turn. Pledges show what each player is chasing.
- New player learns: about 7 things.
- Biggest risk: thin engine-building, and cutting stealing makes it gentler than Cory's confrontational pillar.

## My pick
Shrines. It keeps the "buy things that improve your picks" loop and makes it something you can see and touch on the board, and the tolls give us conflict without stealing rules.
It also cuts the most: shops, cards and champions all turn into one thing.
Snowball is its weak spot, but a sim can test it in an afternoon. If tolls feel too mean, we can borrow the growing piles from #3 to help whoever's behind.
