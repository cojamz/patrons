# Cults v0.2: what's new

- **A new name.** Patrons is now Cults: you lead a cult, recruit members and win followers.
- **A new look.** A lamplit wooden table, cream cards, and pixel art for every space and member.
- **A 20-second intro** shows how a turn goes. Replay it any time from the menu.
- **Your own emoji.** Your cult leader wears it, and your minions (the old workers) are little copies of it. A minion sits right on the space it took.
- **A shop of 10.** Ten members face up, refilled from a face-down deck that shows how many are left.
- **The rims tell you who's winning.** Each quadrant's rim shows its rule, the prize and the tiebreak, with the leader's emoji right in the line.
- **The Reckoning.** At round end the quadrants pay out one at a time, and followers fly to each winner.
- **Hovers everywhere.** Point at anything (long-press on a phone) to see what it is, where a number came from, or what a space would give you.
- **Bot levels:** Easy, Normal or Hard.
- **Online with friends.** Host a game, share the link, and everyone plays on their own device. Reopen the link to get your seat back; a bot can cover for anyone who leaves.
- **New buy prices.** Most members cost twice what they did. Followers: 2 for 1, 4 for 3, 8 for 6 (2 per player), and 10 for 10 as often as you can pay.
- **Always three rounds.** Nothing ends the game early.
- **Release gives back less:** only the colored part of a member's cost, and never in the round you recruited it.
- **Turn order:** round I is random; after that, whoever has the fewest followers goes first.
- **New names and a new default table.** Many spaces and members were renamed (members are all people or groups now), and the default table is Gold, Red, Blue (bends the shop) and Green (copies spaces).
