The Patrons build harness

How a change goes from idea to a link in your hands. Every pass runs the whole loop; nothing reaches you until the blind panel says it ships.

You → Claude direction

You set the design and the feel. Claude (the lead) turns that into work, splits it across agents, and owns the result. Every call you make is logged so nothing gets lost between sessions.

build

Build parallel agents, one job each

EngineThe rules, and only the rules. Same seed + same moves = same game, every time.
MultiplayerHost runs the game; others send moves. Survives drops, rejoins, host leaving.
Board UIDraws the game and turns clicks into moves. No rules of its own, so it can't disagree with the engine.
gate 1 · machines check everything, every change

Automatic checks one command · minutes

Rule testsEvery action and member checked to the exact number.
690 simulated gamesBots and random players. Game state checked after every single move.
Online testsSeveral players, dropped host, rejoin, two people grabbing a seat at once.
Real browserA full game played start to finish in Chromium. Any error fails it.
gate 2 · Claude looks

Eyes on the screen screenshots at laptop and phone size

Claude reads the real screens as images and fixes what's obviously off before spending a tester on it.

gate 3 · strangers play it

The blind panel fresh agents every round · never reused

Each tester has never seen the game. They get the link and the rulebook, nothing else, and play by sight: look at the screen, decide, click the pixel.

The newcomerLaptop. Plays a full game properly and learns from the rulebook.
The phone playerSimulated iPhone, real taps. Checks finger-sized targets, no hover tricks.
The saboteurMisclicks, spams, resizes, keyboard only. Tries to break it.

Each one grades flow, feel, learnability, legibility, craft and robustness from A to F, and lists every misread: what they took a thing for, and what it really was.

triage

Sort the findings Claude

Two testers trip on the same thing: confirmed, fix it. One tester alone: Claude reproduces it first. Fixes go worst-first, then the whole loop runs again with brand-new testers.
