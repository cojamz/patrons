# Novelty check (2026-10-14, research agent with web search; not exhaustive)

- **Closest to a lift:** the follower ladder mirrors Dominion (2008): Estate/Duchy/Province at 1/3/6 VP, a limited top tier as the end clock, and a Big Money baseline. It's legal, but recognizable. Patrons now differs more: costs 2/4/8, plus a 10→10 top tier.
- **Relatives:**
  - Dominion: buy every turn, swappable kingdom.
  - Agricola (2007): action spaces that open each round and stay open.
  - Tzolk'in (2012): rotating board, but its gears actually move workers; Patrons' spin is only for navigation.
  - Pharaon (2019): an action wheel that changes costs.
  - Lords of Waterdeep / Splendor: generic overlaps.
- **Uncommon or new:**
  - The swappable quadrant is the kingdom: it changes both spaces and scoring.
  - Every placement is snake-drafted, with last place picking first.
  - A buy follows every placement.
  - One visible-state majority contest per quadrant, rising 2/4/6.
- **Lean harder:**
  - Make the wheel matter mechanically (adjacency or rotation effects).
  - Make the follower ladder our own (sphere-tied prices, or a clock tier drawn from members).
