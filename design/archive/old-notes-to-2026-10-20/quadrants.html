Four quadrants

Each one is a separate personality with a single way to score. They connect through shared things (resources, spaces, items, rivals, the leader), so any quadrant can leave the table without breaking the rest.

The last Diadem ends the game when that round closes. That's the clock anyone can push.

How they touch, without naming each other

Spillover. Sponsor, Bounty, Zenith, Echo Chamber and Gift Basket give rivals a little too.

Watching the table. Tithe Box, Informant, Cuttings and Beacon pay off when rivals act. Echo and Graft borrow whatever others built.

Harm only points up. Pilfer, Extort, Heist and the Shadow's scoring only reach players ahead of you. Vault and Veil guard against any thief.

Bending shared rules. Slip shares an occupied space, Smuggle buys from any stall, and Kaleidoscope and Broker's Scale bend costs.
